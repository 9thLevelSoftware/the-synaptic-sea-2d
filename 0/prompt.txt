I have been inspired by watching/reading "Beyond The Aquila Rift" and "Pandorum" recently, while also playing Project Zomboid.  I want to combine all of these ideas to create a "high-definition pixel graphic" (i.e. pixels but very detailed pixels) 2d offset top-down multiplayer game in which players wake up in a derelict spaceship in the cryo bay and eventually realize they are on a ship that is trapped in a giant horrible web like Beyond The Aquila Rift.  It would be a long-play crafting survival sim ala Project Zomboid without a clear end game beyond survival, but the unique mechanic is that, instead of roaming through pre-built neighborhoods and taking over houses to reinforce, you find a small podcraft that you repair attached to your spawn derelict and use it to travel to other procedurally generated craft that are trapped in the web.  These derelicts can be repaired to be made operational, or if it is only part of a derelict (as not all the ships are whole) you can start to weld them together to create a mobile home, and as you build up one or more functional spacecraft, you branch out and find more derelicts for loot and such.  The closer you get to the center of the web, the more horrors you encounter, but the better the rewards as well.  I want to lean into galactic eldritch horror elements, wherein exposure to the web and its spawns have effects on your sanity, and unstable sanity can result in hallucinations of all sorts.
