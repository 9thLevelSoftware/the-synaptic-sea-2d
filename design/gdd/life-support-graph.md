## Summary

The Life-Support Graph is the ship's circulation as one integer-only, scene-tree-free simulation block: five loops (O₂, power, coolant, fuel, integrity) as typed ports, nodes and edges, plus a per-compartment atmosphere (gas amount, temperature; pressure and bands derived). It is the physical ground truth every trail, gauge, hiss, breath-fog and cold-damage tick is read from, and the only thing in the game that never lies — hallucinations are tested against it, never written into it. Failures spread one hop per 10 Hz propagation run through the quantities they change, every endogenous loss is preceded by ≥ 10 s of two-channel physical warning, and welding another ship's section joins its subgraph along a seam whose sub-edges start disconnected. Everything that fails, glows, leaks or is salvaged reads or writes it through 18 published mutators and one true-value read view.

> **Quick reference** — Layer: `Core` · Priority: `MVP` · Key deps: `Command & Simulation Layer` (designed; one block `graph.state`, three hooks at divisor 6); `Ship Section & Layout Data Model` and `Item & Module Database` (undesigned — ownership and port-type contracts stated here, provisional); art bible (trail and loop grammar). Depended on by 14 systems (Failure & Trail, Suit, Emitter State, Attention, Interaction, Assembly, Horror, Perception/Instruments, Audio, Save/Load, Sanity, Web Map, Wear, Failure Contract, Co-op, Onboarding).

## Overview

The Life-Support Graph is the ship's circulation written as data: one integer-only simulation block that holds **five loops** — O₂, power, coolant, fuel and integrity — as typed ports on nodes joined by typed edges, plus one atmosphere record (gas amount, temperature) per compartment. It is the physical ground truth the whole game is measured against. Every pipe that leaks, bus that scorches, seam that frosts, scrubber that stalls and hull plate that breaches is a change to this block, made only through its published mutators inside the kernel's write scope; every gauge, trail, hiss, breath-fog and cold-damage tick is a *reading* of it. The Graph is scene-tree free, headless-testable and deterministic (same seed and command stream → identical `state_hash`), serialises as one versioned snapshot, and is the data that co-op will later sync by summary.

The player never operates the Graph as a menu or a schematic. They meet it as a place: a corridor colder than it should be, chevrons crawling slow along a pipe, mist curling from a clamp, a lamp that dims when the scrubber draws. Their verbs — patch, flip a breaker, seal a bulkhead, close a valve, vent, salvage a module, weld a section on, crank by hand — arrive as Interaction commands that call Graph mutators; welding joins another ship's subgraph to this one along a seam. Failures are physical and emergent: a starved scrubber, a frozen pipe or an overloaded cable spreads one hop per propagation run through the quantities it changes, never by script, and every endogenous loss is preceded by a readable physical warning (the fairness gate). The Graph has no notion of a hallucination: it publishes true values only, so a false "fixed" scrubber can be caught by the cold, the thinning air and the hiss the Graph still drives.

Without this system there is no Pillar 1. Without a shared, honest, falsifiable physical state, the hallucination design (Pillar 4) has nothing to lie *against*, the web has no power draw to watch (Pillar 3), and salvage and welding have nothing to act on (Pillar 5). It is the design-order-3 bottleneck with fourteen dependents, and the MVP hypothesis — *the boarding→breach loop over a visible life-support graph is tense for 30+ minutes* — cannot be tested without it.

*Implementation questions raised while designing and deferred to ADRs (→ `/architecture-decision`): struct-of-arrays packed fields vs objects (ADR-LSG-01); the fixed-point unit system (ADR-LSG-02); the explicit one-pass propagation scheme (ADR-LSG-03); append-only topology identity (ADR-LSG-04); snapshot/restore and migration (ADR-LSG-05); dirty-key encoding (ADR-LSG-06); keyed RNG (ADR-LSG-07); stable content type IDs (ADR-LSG-08); and two routed amendments outside this system — the kernel's per-block cached hash (K-1) and Save/Load's binary interchange for 64-bit values (S-1).*

## Player Fantasy

**Framing: indirect, with a direct layer on top.** The player experiences the Graph through its consequences and acts on it through physical verbs; they never see "the Graph". *(creative-director consulted; Framing A adopted with B as its competence and C as its guarantee.)*

> The ship is a body the crew assembled from other ships' corpses, and the Life-Support Graph is its circulation: oxygen, power, coolant, fuel and the hull that holds them. The player never operates the Graph as a menu. They tend the ship: a corridor colder than it should be, chevrons crawling slow along a pipe, frost fanning out from a weld seam, a hiss behind a panel. They go to it with a light and their hands, and they reroute, seal, weld or cut.
>
> Competence is literacy, not optimisation. Every failure leaves a trail along the edge it travels, thickest at its source, so a veteran crew does not run the ship better than a new one; they understand sooner what it is telling them and reach the right seam faster.
>
> And the body never lies. Gauges can be wrong and eyes can be wrong, but the cold, the thin air and the falling pressure in a compartment come from the Graph's true state for every player. When the instruments and the room disagree, the room wins, and the player learns to trust it.

**Anchor moment.** You come back up the pod tube with a salvaged scrubber on your back, and the corridor is colder than you left it. The O₂ pipe along the ceiling is still lit cyan, but its chevrons are crawling slower than they should, and there is a fine white fan of frost on the elbow by the galley. Your own breath hangs in the beam. You set the scrubber down, put your glove on the pipe, and follow the cold back to where it started — the way you would check a sleeping friend's forehead. The emotion is *caretaking under threat*: tenderness and vigilance. The power is not control; it is keeping something alive by attention — the Project Zomboid base you built with your own hands, made precarious (concept, Core Fantasy).

**Pillar alignment.** Pillar 1 verbatim: *"Pipes leak, frost forms, air hisses; the physical world is ground truth. Only instruments and perception can be wrong … A pipe may look fixed, but the room still gets cold, the air still thins, the pressure still drops."* The Graph is that ground truth. Pillar 2 (Dread Before Chaos): every fault has a long, readable tell before it cascades. Pillar 3: keeping the air clean draws power, and power draw is something the web can read. Pillar 4: the Graph is the falsification ground every hallucination is tested against. Pillar 5: salvage, welding and wear act on its nodes; loss is the failure contract's business, not the Graph's.

**Design test.** *When a choice makes the ship easier to read from a screen vs. easier to read from the room → choose the room. When a choice rewards running the ship well vs. rewards noticing it is unwell → choose noticing.* Corollaries: a fault persists and spreads until someone goes to it (never times out); a failure's origin is shown by a trail the player follows back along the edge, never by a marker; a hallucinated "fixed" state never quiets the real hiss or warms the real room.

**Under hallucination the fantasy deepens rather than breaks.** Pillar 4 lets the lie extend from the gauge to the sprite — a broken scrubber may render whole and spinning. What cannot change is the **consequence channel** — a closed list: compartment temperature, O₂ and pressure as the body feels them; the frost, mist, scorch and drip trails; the hiss, creak and whistle at a descriptor's anchor; lamp brightness; draught. Failure & Trail, Suit and Adaptive Audio render these as pure readers of the Graph's true values, and the Filter may neither remove nor add to them. What *can* lie is the module-body channel: sprites, gauges, inspect cards and a module's own wear state. The caretaker who can no longer trust their eyes falls back on breath, cold and touch — the oldest reading of a body. Verification costs rise in order: the room itself; a second instrument elsewhere on the ship; a saner crewmate (VS-B); time.

**Reference techniques, taken precisely.** Project Zomboid — the base degrades on its own clock and competence is the routine of noticing (*adopt*); its numeric context-menu condition readouts (*reject*). Barotrauma — repair is a place you go with the right tool; a junction box fails *where it is* (*adopt*); the Status Monitor ship-overview screen (*reject*). Dwarf Fortress fluids — propagation is visible in the medium, not announced (*adopt*). The Long Dark — cold is read from breath vapour before any number (*adopt*). SS13 / Stationeers — precision is something you go and get (*adopt* as the lit inspect card); their gas chemistry (*reject*: five loops, not chemistry). FTL — door-control causality (*adopt*); per-room O₂ shading on a schematic (*reject*). Alien: Isolation — checking costs you (*adopt*). Pandorum — the failing ship as the only reliable narrator (*adopt*).

**What this GDD does not promise (anti-pillar guard rails).**
1. No mastery-by-optimisation: no efficiency, "optimal layout", throughput score, uptime grade or any reward for running loops near capacity. Staying alive is the only payoff.
2. The web is not a puzzle to solve: failures are not authored puzzles with one correct sequence; there is no "solved" state and no completion ping.
3. No schematic, overlay, minimap or ship-status screen. Precision exists only as the lit inspect card of a specific instrument (art bible 7.0), which lies when that gauge lies. The suit's O₂ numeral on the critical strip is the *suit's* reading, not a Graph overlay.
4. No progression from the Graph: nothing unlocks or levels from repairs; better modules come from salvage.
5. No readability in the dark: "What You Light, You Can Read" — unlit compartments are readable only through the body channels (cold, breath, hiss, pressure), never by sprite state.
6. No sixth loop and no "heat loop": heat and pressure are per-compartment atmosphere.
7. No freeform plumbing: rerouting is valves, breakers, hatches, seams and cuts on pre-shaped modules.
8. No guarantee the ship can always be saved. Losing a section or the ship is allowed; the failure contract (Pillar 5) is the safety net, not the Graph.

## Detailed Design

### Core Rules

*Every rule below is a sim rule of the Graph owner module (`src/sim/graph/`), stated so that an implementer can write it and a tester can falsify it. Registry constants are cited by their registered name (`TICK_RATE`, `MAX_DIRTY_KEYS`, `STEP_BUDGET_MS`, `rng_derive`, `state_hash`, `hook_runs_this_tick`, `TILE_PX`); every constant this GDD introduces is written by the registry name it proposes (Section G marks the candidates). Type IDs are explicit, stable, checked unique at boot and never reused (R19). Where a rule records an alternative that was considered and rejected, the rejection is part of the rule: a later change must answer it.*

**LS-1 — One block, one determinism contract.** The Graph is exactly one persisted SIM state block, `graph.state` (owner Graph; `class` SIM; `persist` true; `persist_scope` WORLD; `scope` SHARED; `repl_hint` SHARED_SUMMARY; `schema_version` 1; `restore_after: [layout.state]`), registered by the Graph's composition-root call during BOOTING. It is a struct-of-arrays object (`GraphState extends RefCounted`): `PackedInt64Array` for conserved quantities and anything that can exceed 2^31, `PackedInt32Array` for ids, indices and basis points, `PackedByteArray` for enums and flags; there are no per-node objects, no `Resource` for state or for saves, and no `Node` anywhere in the module (kernel H10; gdUnit4 exit 101 is impossible by construction). The contract: (a) **units** — gas in mGU (`GAS_SCALE` 1000; 1 GU is the amount giving 0.1 kPa in one tile), volume in tiles, pressure derived in dkPa (`STD_PRESSURE_DKPA` 1000), temperature in mC, power in W ≡ dJ per step, energy in dJ, liquids in mL, integrity in bp 0..10000 (`HP_FULL` 10000), fractions in permille; (b) **zero floats** stored, hashed, replicated or used on the propagation path — a designer float in the data file is converted once at boot by `int(round(x × scale))` into the immutable `GraphTuning` and is not block state; (c) **store extensive, derive intensive** — `n_o2`, `n_inert`, `(T, t_rem)` as the energy pair `U = T × C + t_rem`, battery `stock` in dJ, liquid `stock_ml`, `hp`; `P`, `p_o2`, `o2_pm`, bands, `served_pm`, `circ`, every descriptor and every cache are derived on read or in the run and never persisted; (d) **every flux is a non-negative magnitude with an explicit direction**, so integer division is applied only to non-negative operands and truncation is floor everywhere except the two signed remainder-carrying thermal steps, which truncate toward zero and carry the remainder; (e) **exact conservation** — each section carries persisted counters `vented_mgu` (gas lost to EXTERIOR), `radiated_dj` (net heat exchanged with the exterior sink through hull and radiators) and `spilled_cool_ml` (coolant lost from the loop); fuel lost from a pipe lands in the compartment's `fuel_puddle_ml`; the invariants `Σ n + vented_mgu`, `Σ U / 1000 + radiated_dj − Σ produced`, `Σ stock_ml + spilled_cool_ml` and `Σ fuel + Σ fuel_puddle_ml − burned` are exact integer equalities over any number of steps and every test asserts them; (f) **every product the propagation code forms has documented operand maxima** and the boot assertion of D.13 refuses a build whose table violates `max_a × max_b < 2^62`; (g) **no `sqrt`, `exp`, `pow` or `sin` on the propagation path** — laws are linear or piecewise-linear integer tables, and per-step decay is written `x −= x × k_ppm / 10^6`; (h) **stability** — `Σ_j k_ij ≤ 1` per compartment (D.12), asserted at boot and re-checked by `can_join` at every weld; (i) **iteration is by ascending slot over dense arrays** and slot order equals ascending-id order at all times (slots are append-only within a session, removal tombstones, no compaction in MVP), so no result depends on a Dictionary's order and sim code never iterates a Dictionary; (j) **one explicit local-exchange pass per loop per run**, computed from a start-of-phase snapshot into accumulators and applied afterwards (Jacobi), with the source-depletion clamp resolved by scaling all outflows of an over-drawn node by `available / requested` in ascending edge id — never an iterative or global solver, never catch-up ("we are behind, run two steps"): the kernel's lag policy drops time and the Graph must not re-add it. *Alternative considered:* two blocks (topology; flow/failure/atmosphere) as the kernel's Interactions row 1 says in the plural — rejected because a weld writes both and G4 wants one snapshot (R2; a routed wording fix "Blocks" → "Block" goes to the kernel owner). *Alternative considered:* SI units (mmol, J, mK) with an ideal-gas constant — rejected because a 2D game with tile volumes needs no gas constant and the worst product of the game units is 2 × 10^13 (D.13), which makes TD's two-stage temperature form and tank-pressure caveat moot (R1).

**LS-2 — Five loops, no sixth.** Loop ids: `0 NONE` (non-loop couplings), `1 O2`, `2 POWER`, `3 COOLANT`, `4 FUEL`, `5 INTEGRITY`. What "flow" means per loop is fixed here and nowhere else: **O2** — a pool of connected O2_TANK stocks; SCRUBBER adds, VENT withdraws into its compartment, consumers are bounded by `path_cap` (the bottleneck DUCT, seam or VALVE on the unique tree path, derived at topology refresh); **POWER** — an instantaneous island balance with no inventory except BATTERY, allocated by tier (D.6); edge load is the served power below the edge on the island's tree and exists only for D.9; **COOLANT** — `circ` ‰ (D.8) says how much of the nominal heat exchange happens; heat is carried as the RESERVOIR's `(t_cool, tc_rem)` pair; **FUEL** — stock moves tank → burner limited by `path_cap`, burned per reactor output; **INTEGRITY** — static; `hp` is the store, STRUT load `ρ = load / cap` exists for VS-A redistribution and is not computed in MVP. Heat and pressure are **per-compartment atmosphere values** (LS-6), not loops: the O2 loop feeds compartments, the COOLANT loop moves heat between machines and radiators, and passive conduction and gas exchange between rooms are properties of OPENING edges. Anything proposed later (CO2, humidity, advection, fire) must either live per compartment or be out of scope; it may not become loop 6.

**LS-3 — Node kinds.** Stable ids: `1 COMPARTMENT`, `2 EXTERIOR` (one per world, node id 0, `P_EXT` 0 dkPa and `T_AMB_MC` −30000 in MVP, R14), `10 REACTOR`, `11 BATTERY`, `12 CABLE_JUNCTION` (holds `breaker_closed`), `13 LOAD` (with `subtype`; `HEATER` is a LOAD subtype with `waste_permille` 1000 — one in the MVP home-ship, R8), `20 SCRUBBER`, `21 O2_TANK`, `22 VENT`, `23 DUCT_JUNCTION`, `30 PUMP`, `31 RADIATOR`, `32 RESERVOIR`, `33 PIPE_JUNCTION`, `34 HEAT_EXCHANGER` (VS-A), `40 FUEL_TANK`, `41 FUEL_JUNCTION`, `42 FUEL_PORT` (VS-A), `50 HULL_SEGMENT`, `51 FRAME_NODE` (VS-A). Common persisted fields on every node: `id` (u32, never reused), `kind`, `section_id`, `def_id` (Item DB string, empty for COMPARTMENT/EXTERIOR/HULL_SEGMENT), `state` (LS-10), `hp`, `hp_cap` (default `HP_FULL`), `forced_mode`, `forced_mode_next` (the cascade queue, LS-12), `fail_since_step`, `warn_since_step`, `patched_at_step`, `compartment_id` (where it physically sits — the reader's anchor), `ports[]`. Kind-specific persisted fields: REACTOR `out_w`, `fuel_rem`, `scram`, `starve_steps`; BATTERY `stock`; LOAD `req_w`, `tier`, `subtype`, `energised_at_step`; VENT `setpoint_pp`; PUMP `throttle_pm`; RESERVOIR `stock_ml`, `t_cool`, `tc_rem`; FUEL_TANK `stock_ml`; O2_TANK `stock`; HULL_SEGMENT `bound_compartment`, `exposed`, `tiles`, `fatigued`; COMPARTMENT per LS-6. Ports: `port_type_id`, `local_index`, `cap` (loop units per step, from the module def), `open_end`. Modules in the Item & Module Database declare `node_kind`, `ports[] = (port_type_id, cap, local_index)`, `tier`, `req_w`, `hp_start`, `waste_permille` and reference the port-type ids of LS-4 by number — the Item DB does not define them.

**LS-4 — Port types and the connectability matrix.** Encoding `loop × 100 + role` (roles: 1 SOURCE, 2 SINK, 3 STORE, 4 THRU); 9xx are non-loop couplings. `101 O2_SRC`, `103 O2_STORE`, `104 O2_THRU`; `201 PWR_SRC`, `202 PWR_SNK`, `203 PWR_STORE`, `204 PWR_THRU`; `301 CLT_HEAT_IN`, `302 CLT_HEAT_OUT`, `303 CLT_STORE`, `304 CLT_THRU`; `401 FUEL_SRC`, `402 FUEL_SNK`, `403 FUEL_STORE`, `404 FUEL_THRU`; `501 STR_ANCHOR`, `504 STR_THRU`; `901 ATMO_VENT` (node ↔ its compartment's gas), `902 THERMAL_COUPLE` (waste heat ↔ compartment, and the machine proxy), `903 HULL_FACE` (segment bounds a compartment and the exterior), `904 OPENING_FACE` (door, hatch or seam-gap side). `connectable(a, b)` is pure and used by data load and `can_join`: same loop; THRU joins anything of its loop; SRC–SNK, SRC–STORE, STORE–SNK allowed; SRC–SRC, SNK–SNK, STORE–STORE rejected (must go through a THRU); 301 and 302 attach only to 304; 904 only to 904; 901–903 never carry edges. Ids are append-only forever; a new port type is a new number.

**LS-5 — Edge kinds.** `1 DUCT` (O2; `cap` mGU/step, `len`, `exposed` — a hull-run duct whose leak goes to EXTERIOR instead of its compartment), `2 CABLE` (POWER; `cap` W), `3 PIPE` (COOLANT or FUEL by its `loop` field; `cap` mL/step), `4 STRUT` (INTEGRITY; VS-A), `5 VALVE` (O2/COOLANT/FUEL; `open`; closed is impassable and leak-isolating), `6 SEAM` (any loop; a loop edge crossing sections with `seam_id` and the seam's `q`; created only by `weld_join`), `7 OPENING` (no loop; `area` in 1/64 tile, `door_state`; `area` 0 is a sealed door). Breach holes and seam weeps are **virtual OPENINGs to EXTERIOR derived from `hp` and `(q, leak_bp)` each run** (D.2), never stored; a virtual opening counts toward a compartment's degree for D.1/D.12 exactly like a stored one. Common persisted edge fields: `id`, `kind`, `loop`, `a_node`, `a_port`, `b_node`, `b_port`, `hp`, `hp_cap`, `state`, `forced_mode`, `forced_mode_next`, `fail_since_step`, `warn_since_step`, `patched_at_step`, `seam_id` (0 = none), `capped`. `SeamRecord`: `seam_id`, `section_a`, `section_b`, `q`, `hp`, `leak_bp`, `edge_ids[]`, `opening_ids[]`, `L_seam`, `built_step`, `state`.

**LS-6 — Compartments, atmosphere and who owns what.** A COMPARTMENT is the Graph's model of a Layout room, keyed by Layout `room_id` (`layout_room_ref`). **Layout owns** room identity, polygon, `V` (tiles) and `exposed_tiles`; the Graph caches `V` and `exposed_tiles` as derived fields (not hashed; rebuilt in `post_restore`, which is why `restore_after: [layout.state]`). **The Graph owns** the atmosphere — `n_o2`, `n_inert`, `T`, `t_rem`, `fuel_puddle_ml` — and every opening/hatch `area` and `door_state`, every VALVE `open` and every breaker `closed`; Interaction issues the commands, the Graph publishes the mutators (R11; one writer per fact, kernel H3). Derived each run: `P`, `p_o2`, `o2_pm`, `band_o2`, `band_p`, `band_t`, `pressure_trend` (D.3). Scope of the physics (R14): pressure from `n` and `V` only (no T → P coupling), exterior always vacuum (`P_EXT` 0) with one radiative sink `T_AMB_MC`, no CO2, humidity or advected heat (heat moves only by conduction through openings and hull), no global rate dial — the constants are tuned, not scaled. The couplings the player reads are physical chains through these fields: scrubber (needs POWER) → pool → vent → `n_o2`; crew → `draw_air` → `n_o2`; opening → D.1 and D.7; breach → D.2 → D.1; machine → `THERMAL_COUPLE` → `Q_src_room`; coolant → D.8 → radiator; power loss → tier shedding → scrubbers/pumps/heater stop; cold → coolant FREEZE → burst on thaw; seam → weep. *Alternative considered:* per-tile atmosphere so frost can creep tile by tile — rejected (TD R4): it multiplies N by 20–60 per room; spatial gradients are presentation derived from the true compartment values plus the leak anchor and `since_steps`, which still honours P1 because the frost reflects the true temperature.

**LS-7 — Bands and thresholds (the single source of truth).** The Graph publishes these; Suit, Failure & Trail, Instruments and Adaptive Audio consume them and never redefine them. Hysteresis `H_P` 5 dkPa / `H_T` 500 mC on re-entry (D.3). Registry candidates in bold are the constants Suit reads for damage onset: **`COLD_DMG_ONSET_MC` = −15000**, **`HEAT_DMG_ONSET_MC` = 45000**, **`HYPOXIA_PP` = 140**, **`DECOMP_P` = 400** (suit insulation shifts the cold onset; that is Suit's formula over this constant).

Failure thresholds live in the same table family: `HP_OK` 7000, `HP_FAIL` 3000, `LEAK_HP` 4000, `BREACH_HP` 4000, `FREEZE_WARN_MC` −10000, `FREEZE_ON_MC` −20000, `FREEZE_OFF_MC` −10000, `OVERHEAT_ON_MC` 70000, `OVERHEAT_OFF_MC` 50000, `OVERHEAT_ON_STEPS` 50, `OVERHEAT_SCRAM_STEPS` 300, `FUEL_STARVE_STEPS` 100, `RUPTURE_HP` 3600, `PATCH_CAP_BP` 6000. Time scale anchors (D.15): a door-sized breach decompresses a 20-tile room to LOW in 5.6 s; a pinhole takes 6 min to ANOXIC with one crew; a breached room frosts in 4 min and freezes its pipes in 12 min, while a healthy ship on one 500 W heater stays at COMFORT indefinitely and an unpowered one fogs breath at 6 min and frosts at 13; one battery cell runs the life-critical tiers for 6 min.

**LS-8 — Three hooks, twelve phases, fixed order.** The Graph registers three real step hooks, all at (divisor 6, offset 0), chained by `after:` (kernel CR-3, `hook_runs_this_tick` = `(T mod divisor) == offset`): `graph.propagate.power` (root; phases 0–3), `graph.propagate.atmo` (`after: [graph.propagate.power]`; phases 4–7), `graph.propagate.failure` (`after: [graph.propagate.atmo]`; phases 8–11). One run = one step = `GRAPH_STEP_TICKS` 6 ticks = 0.1 s; `step_no = T / 6`, valid only on hook ticks and persisted as `last_step_no`. Dependents name the hook they read: Suit vitals `after: graph.propagate.atmo`, Attention `after: graph.propagate.power`; Failure & Trail is presentation and reads dirty keys. Because the kernel has no alias concept, this is CR-3 as written, and the VS-A stagger is only an offset change on two registrations (with the documented consequence that a reader must then share the offset of the hook it follows or see a run-old value, kernel edge case). The phases:

| Phase | Hook | Work | Order | Reads | Writes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | power | Apply queued unconditional inputs: `draw_air` requests, footprint impulses, `set_load_request` changes, `forced_mode_next` → `forced_mode` | ascending target id | queues, `forced_mode_next` | node/compartment fields |

The one-run delay on cascades (phase 10 queues, phase 0 of the next run applies) removes any dependence on update order inside a run and gives every hop a minimum latency of 0.1 s, so a cascade reads as a sequence, never as an instant. Cross-loop coupling therefore has up to one run of latency per hop; nothing in this GDD assumes same-tick cross-loop consistency, so the VS-A stagger changes no rule.

**LS-9 — Randomness is keyed, never counted.** The only stochastic rules are thaw-burst (C5) and, in VS-A, spark ignition (C9). Each draw is `rng_derive(world_seed, "graph.fail", (step_no << 20) | subject_id)` — the registry form is `mix64(world_seed, hash(stream_name), counter)` with "counter (u64 stored in the owning block)"; the Graph supplies the key as that counter, so the draw is a pure function of state, order-independent, and unchanged by weld, cut, restore or content added elsewhere. `subject_id < 2^20` is asserted (`MAX_GRAPH_NODES` 4096 and `MAX_GRAPH_EDGES` 8192 fit with room), leaving 43 bits of `step_no`. The clarification that "a stored running counter is one option; any owner-defined 63-bit key is valid" is a **routed amendment to the kernel's D.10**, not this GDD's rule (R4). *Alternative considered:* a sequential counter in the block — rejected (TD R10): a weld adds candidates and the draws of an unrelated derelict change.

**LS-10 — Failure lifecycle: six states, five severities.** States: `0 OK` (`hp ≥ HP_OK`, no forced mode), `1 DEGRADED` (`HP_FAIL < hp < HP_OK`; `eff` from D.4; hazard modes may be active; with none active the subject carries mode WORN, LS-11 — the module-body trace *(Amended 2026-10-26 — CD-GDD-ALIGN)*), `2 FAILED` (`hp ≤ HP_FAIL` or `forced_mode ≠ 0`; no function; leaks continue; repairable), `3 DESTROYED` (`hp` = 0; permanent scrap; repair rejected `NOT_REPAIRABLE`; salvage then replace), `4 SALVAGED` (removed by `salvage_node`; tombstone slot; fluid ports `open_end`), `5 ABSENT` (never populated; inert; no ports connected; no descriptors). Severity `sev` 0..4 from D.4 (0 none; 1 trace — the weep, the flicker, the frost on the lagging; 2 minor; 3 major — hazard to nearby play, first `alarm_eligible` level; 4 critical — arcing, jetting, runaway), raised by hazard rules (breach `A_hole` ≥ 32 → 4; OVERLOAD `ρ` ≥ 1500 → 3; any forced mode → ≥ 3). Damage is monotonic per cause; repair raises `hp` only through `repair_step` up to `hp_cap`. The complete transition table is in *States and Transitions*.

**LS-11 — Failure modes, triggers, pre-stages.** Mode ids: `1 LEAK` (DUCT, PIPE, tanks, reservoir; `hp < LEAK_HP`; clears when `hp ≥ LEAK_HP` or isolated by a closed VALVE upstream — isolated means the leak draws from nothing, the mode stays, the descriptor's `intensity_pm` is 0), `2 SHORT` (CABLE, junction, LOAD; `hp < HP_FAIL` under load; clears on repair or breaker open), `3 OVERHEAT` (REACTOR, LOAD machine, RESERVOIR; `T_mach > OVERHEAT_ON_MC` for `OVERHEAT_ON_STEPS`; clears at `T_mach < OVERHEAT_OFF_MC`; pre-stage: `T_mach` in HOT → sev 2 shimmer), `4 FREEZE` (coolant PIPE, RESERVOIR, liquid tanks; **pre-stage** sev 1 "frost on the lagging" at `T_room < FREEZE_WARN_MC`; forced FAILED at `T_room < FREEZE_ON_MC` once the D.11 gate is open; clears at `T_room > FREEZE_OFF_MC` with the thaw-burst roll), `5 BREACH` (HULL_SEGMENT, SEAM; `hp < BREACH_HP`, gated; pre-stage is the sev-1 hairline at `hp < HP_OK`; clears when `hp ≥ BREACH_HP`), `6 STARVE` (any powered node with `req_w > 0` and `served_pm < 1000`; a status, not a state change — it exists so the player can tell "off" from "broken"), `7 OVERLOAD` (CABLE; `ρ > 1000`; sev 1 from the first step over — the hum climbs, the box warms — which is the warning that opens the window for the SHORT/FAILED that follows), `8 SEVERED` (edge or port cut or salvaged with `open_end`; clears by `cap_port`), `9 SCRAM` (REACTOR; OVERHEAT sustained `OVERHEAT_SCRAM_STEPS` or fuel starve `FUEL_STARVE_STEPS`; clears by `reset_node` when `T_mach < OVERHEAT_OFF_MC` and fuel is present), `10 WORN` *(Amended 2026-10-26 — CD-GDD-ALIGN)* (any node or edge with `hp < HP_OK` and no mode 1–9 live on it — the un-modal trace of a worn module, the one a subject losing hp to duty wear or a GOUGE carries when nothing leaks, shorts or freezes; `sev` from D.4, `medium` NONE, `acoustic` NONE, never `alarm_eligible`; it is the module-body channel of LS-14 — readable on the sprite, the gauge and the inspect card, corruptible by Perception — and it counts toward `warn_since_step` like any `sev ≥ 1` descriptor; a subject emits WORN only while no mode 1–9 is live on it, a hazard mode replaces it, and it clears at `hp ≥ HP_OK`). The closed-hatch descriptor `SEAL_STRAIN` is an OPENING descriptor (id 26, LS-14, D.14), the closed sibling of `AIRFLOW`, not a subject mode. Every mode that can force FAILED has a pre-stage at sev ≥ 1 that necessarily precedes it by more than `W_MIN_STEPS`, so LS-13 never has to invent a warning.

**LS-12 — Cascades are physical chains, one hop per run, bounded.** No scripted "if A fails then B fails" exists; the chains below are what the numbers do, listed so that content, tests and the fairness audit can name them. **LS-13 — The fairness gate is a sim rule.** `W_MIN_WARNING_S` = 10 (`W_MIN_STEPS` 100) is a hard rule of this block (R7) *(Amended 2026-10-26 — CD-GDD-ALIGN: raised from 8 s to 10 s)*: an **endogenous** transition to FAILED or DESTROYED (node or edge) and a hull or seam LEAK → BREACH escalation require that the subject has emitted a descriptor of `sev ≥ 1` for at least 100 consecutive steps (`warn_elapsed`, D.11). While the gate is closed `hp` is floored at `HP_FAIL + 1` (or `BREACH_HP` for the breach case) and the excess damage is discarded. **Exogenous** causes bypass the gate: player verbs (cut, vent, salvage, overload by breaker), footprint-channel RUPTURE and GOUGE, and `apply_damage` with `cause = EXOGENOUS`. **Exported requirement — exogenous sources carry their own tell** *(Amended 2026-10-26 — CD-GDD-ALIGN)*: the gate protects against the sim, not against agency, so it never delays an exogenous apply; every non-player exogenous source — M17 RUPTURE, GOUGE and STRESS from Horror Entities or Attention, and environmental M18 `apply_damage(…, EXOGENOUS)` — must therefore be preceded by that system's own Pillar 2 tell of 10–30 s (the stalk, the scrape, the rhythm change) stated in its GDD. The Graph neither emits nor checks that tell; a source that applies exogenous damage with no tell is in breach of this requirement, which the (review) criterion in Section H discharges. Player death is Suit's rule; the Graph guarantees that the *cause* — the room's band, the module's failure — was warned. `alarm_eligible` on a descriptor is true only at `sev ≥ 3` **and** with the gate open, so alarm-class presentation (the rotating sweep) is never the first channel; exported requirement to Perception Filter, Sanity & Hallucination and Failure & Trail (R17): no consumer may suppress a consequence-channel descriptor's acoustic or physical channel (LS-14: any descriptor with `medium ≠ NONE`; a WORN descriptor is module-body and is the Filter's to corrupt *(Amended 2026-10-26 — CD-GDD-ALIGN)*), a filter may add or alter sprites and gauges but never remove a hiss, a cold or a thin-air, and `alarm_eligible` never first. *Alternative considered:* a content guideline rather than a sim gate — rejected (GD Q5): deaths must have a nameable cause in every build, not only in well-authored content.

**Two channels (P4)** *(Amended 2026-10-26 — CD-GDD-ALIGN)*. Every descriptor is on exactly one of two channels, fixed by its `mode`. The **consequence channel** is every COMPARTMENT, EDGE, SEAM and OPENING descriptor and every leak/hazard mode 1–9 on a node — everything with `medium ≠ NONE`. It reaches the player only through a closed list: trail VFX (mist, drip, frost, scorch, debris, puddle), the `acoustic` classes played at the descriptor's anchor, Suit body cues from the bands, lamp brightness from `served_pm`, and the draught of `AIRFLOW` and the strain of `SEAL_STRAIN`. Perception may neither remove, alter nor add to a consequence-channel descriptor or to any item on that list. The **module-body channel** is a module's own body state — its sprite, its gauge, its inspect card and its WORN descriptors (`medium` NONE, `acoustic` NONE); Perception may corrupt it. The split is what makes LS-22 falsifiable: a lie can live only on the body channel, and the consequence channel is the witness against it.

**LS-15 — Seams: a weld moves nothing.** Sections are instantiated into the one world graph when they exist (`instantiate_section`); ids come from the block's `next_*` counters in template order, so a weld **only adds edges** and never renumbers. A weld plan is plain data `{ section_a, section_b, pairs[]: (node_a, port_a, node_b, port_b, loop), openings[]: (comp_a, comp_b, area_max), L_seam, q }` validated by the pure query `can_join(plan)` with the closed reason set `SECTION_UNKNOWN`, `SECTION_NOT_LOADED` (dormant counts as not loaded), `SOCKET_OCCUPIED`, `PORT_INCOMPAT`, `PORT_ABSENT` (ABSENT, DESTROYED or SALVAGED endpoint), `SEAM_LIMIT` (> `MAX_SEAMS_PER_SECTION` 8), `ALREADY_JOINED`, `CAPACITY` (`MAX_GRAPH_NODES` 4096 / `MAX_GRAPH_EDGES` 8192), `POWER_CYCLE` (the cable pairs would close a cycle in POWER wiring — R12: islands are trees, cycles are rejected here and at data load, VS-A may lift it), `STABILITY` (D.12). It returns the fully resolved plan (slot indices, edge count) and informational `surge[]` per sub-edge (D.14), flagging an opening whose `|ΔP| > WELD_DP_WARN` 300 dkPa — **a warning, never a reject**; the warning is diegetic: the closed hatch's own `SEAL_STRAIN` descriptor (CREAK at the rim, rim frost when `|ΔT| > HATCH_DT_FROST_MC`; D.14, LS-14) and the corruptible gauge. `surge[]` reaches the player only through those tells and instruments — never as a number in a weld preview or any other UI; Assembly's preview may consume `surge[].warn` to pick a diegetic cue, not to print the value *(Amended 2026-10-26 — CD-GDD-ALIGN)*. `weld_join` then only appends the pre-validated edges, writes the SeamRecord, sets every sub-edge **disconnected** (R9: opening `area` 0, pipe and duct valves closed, cable breaker open), bumps `topology_version` and marks `T` + `S/<seam_id>` + `SEC/<a>` + `SEC/<b>`; nothing in it can fail, so CR-9's infallible apply holds. Connecting is then three lit verbs (`set_opening_area`, `set_valve`, `set_breaker`), each with its own surge read-out. The seam weeps through `A_seam` from the step it exists (D.2, D.14) until wear-in or V11 — the weld's own P1 tell. *Alternative considered:* opening the seam at weld with a hard reject above a `ΔP` limit (SD OQ-13) — rejected (R9): it created a vacuum-section soft-lock and a loop-surge leak; the disconnected start removes both and makes every connection a readable choice.

**LS-16 — Cuts, only along seams.** `cut_detach(seam_id, boundary_mode)` is atomic in one P2 apply. `SEALED` (cut at a bulkhead or hatch): the seam's OPENINGs are removed, its loop sub-edges tombstoned, both sides' fluid ports `capped` — nothing leaks. `RAGGED` (cut through the weld): for each former opening a virtual hole of `A_CUT` 24 is attached to each side's compartment as a hull stub with `hp` 2500 (sev 3, BREACH, exogenous — no gate), and each side's fluid ports become `open_end` (SEVERED) leaking at `OPEN_END_RATE` until `cap_port`. Guarantees: no inventory lives in edges, so removing them creates or destroys nothing; power has no inventory; the current step's exchange across the seam is simply not computed; one notification batch with `T`, `S/<seam_id>`, affected `C/<id>`; the next run re-derives components. Cutting through a compartment is not a Graph operation and is not allowed (R10): a compartment belongs to one section and a cut never splits gas by a volume ratio. `discard_section(section_id)` (jettison, Web Map unload in VS-A) tombstones a section's slots; mass in it leaves the world and is added to the counters as `discarded_*` so the invariant still closes; ids are never reused.

**LS-17 — Sections and dormancy.** Every node, edge and compartment carries `section_id`; a section record holds `kind` (HOME / DERELICT), `dormant`, `rate_pm` (LS-25), `stress_until_step`, the three conservation counters and `discarded_*`. A section is **dormant** when its derelict is not loaded (zoomed out, left behind): its slots are skipped by every phase, its atmosphere and failures stand still, its state is persisted and hashed unchanged, and it cannot be welded (`SECTION_NOT_LOADED`). Loading it clears `dormant` and it resumes from its last state with no fast-forward (R13; SD OQ-2 resolved: cheapest, and a derelict that "does not degrade on its own" is the boarding rhythm GD asks for). VS-A (ADR-LSG-04): on unload the partition is serialised into Web Map's deltas and the slots are compacted away by an explicit order-preserving compaction mutator; reload re-creates them with **new** ids. Quiescence sleeping of islands (D.16) is the VS-A lever on top of dormancy, with its flag persisted so a restore resumes with the same awake set.

**LS-19 — `read_view` is the one true channel.** Pure, side-effect free, callable from `validate`, P3 and presentation; it may call `ensure_derived()` (a rebuild of non-hashed caches), which keeps `validate`'s hash-unchanged guarantee. **LS-20 — Dirty keys confirm `MAX_DIRTY_KEYS`.** Keys are ints `(KIND << 32) | id` with KIND ∈ {COMP 1, BAND 2, NODE 3, EDGE 4, DESC 5, LOOP 6, SEAM 7, TOPO 8, SECTION 9} (written in this document by their family names `C/<cid>`, `B/<cid>`, `N/<id>`, `F/<id>`, `L/<loop>/<anchor>`, `S/<seam_id>`, `T`, `SEC/<id>`); no String is built per key per run. Rules: continuous values never dirty per step — a key fires only when `⌊value / quantum⌋` changes bucket (quanta: `T` 1000 mC, `P` 10 dkPa, `p_o2` 5 dkPa, store 20 ‰, intensity 100 ‰, `hp` 250 bp); one `C/<cid>` per compartment per step, not one per field; structural change uses `T` + `SEC/<id>`, never per-node keys; last-emitted buckets are derived and `post_restore` seeds them so a restore yields one `ALL` and then silence; Instruments and Audio poll `read_view` at render rate for smooth needles — keys wake discrete presentation only (trail spawn/despawn, loop on/off). D.10 bounds the worst designed cascade at 38 (MVP) and 135 (VS-A) keys, so **`MAX_DIRTY_KEYS` 256 stays as registered**; the Graph confirms it and asks that it never fall below 128. *Alternative considered:* SD's string-keyed families — the semantics are identical; the int encoding is chosen for the per-run allocation cost at VS-A (ADR-LSG-06) [UNV that the kernel's key type is Variant; if it is String, the family strings are the encoding and nothing else changes].

**LS-21 — Serialization, hash, summary, interchange.** *Persisted (canonical):* `schema_version`, `next_node_id`, `next_edge_id`, `next_seam_id`, `next_section_id`, `last_step_no`; nodes and edges with every field of LS-3/LS-5 including tombstones; SeamRecords; sections with `dormant`, `rate_pm`, `stress_pm`, `stress_until_step`, `vented_mgu`, `radiated_dj`, `spilled_cool_ml`, `discarded_*`; compartments `layout_room_ref`, `n_o2`, `n_inert`, `T`, `t_rem`, `fuel_puddle_ml`. *Derived, never in a snapshot:* `V`, `exposed_tiles` (from Layout), components, trees, `path_cap`, degrees, id → slot maps, `served_pm`, descriptors, bands, `P`/`p_o2`/`o2_pm`, last-emitted buckets, the cached hash. Rule: anything the next step depends on that is not re-derivable from persisted fields is persisted (`t_rem`, `tc_rem`, `fuel_rem`, `out_w`, `forced_mode_next`, `warn_since_step`); `served_pm` is not, because phase 2 recomputes it before use. `canonical_bytes()` = the persisted fields in a fixed documented order, each as `[u32 tag][u32 length][field.to_byte_array()]`, raw slot arrays with tombstones — native memcpy, no interpreted loop. **`block_hash()`** is published: cached, recomputed only when the block was written since the last hash, H64 over `canonical_bytes()`; the kernel's `state_hash` is registered as `hash(canonical_snapshot(blocks))` and the Graph's bytes feed it unchanged. The one-level Merkle form (`state_hash` over per-block cached hashes, H64 via native `HashingContext` or S0-d's winner) is **kernel amendment K-1, routed to the kernel owner as an ADR candidate** and not a rule of this GDD (R5). **SHARED_SUMMARY** (VS-B): per compartment `T_dC` (int16, 0.1 °C), `P` (u16), `p_o2` (u16), three bands (3 bits each); per node/edge `state` (3 bits) + `sev` (3 bits) + `served_q` (0..15); per loop component `pool_q`; active descriptors compact `(id, mode, sev, alarm_eligible)`; seam list; about 0.4 KB at MVP, deltas ride the dirty keys; clients never receive `t_rem`, queues or RNG inputs — they receive truth, quantised. **Interchange (S-1, routed to Save/Load):** every Graph value stays below 2^53; the canonical form is packed int arrays; any JSON text form must base64 the packed bytes (`var_to_bytes(allow_objects = false)`); never a `Resource` for saves (R6; Godot JSON parses numbers as float — TD, sourced). Restore is two-phase (decode and validate into a scratch state, then swap); `restore(data, stored_version)` supports exactly version 1 in MVP, any other version is a reported failure, and every failure is a named `GRAPH_RESTORE_*` reason with the live state untouched (Section E). Type ids are append-only forever — the only forward-compatibility promise v1 makes.

**LS-23 — Patch, repair, decay.** `repair_step(PATCH)` raises `hp` by its delta up to `hp_cap = min(hp_cap, PATCH_CAP_BP 6000)` and records `patched_at_step`; a patched subject is therefore permanently DEGRADED at sev 1 (a patch weeps) — it works at `eff` 750 and the leak mode is cleared because `hp ≥ LEAK_HP`. After `PATCH_DECAY_STEPS` (2400 = `PATCH_DECAY_S` 240 × 10) the patch decays at `PATCH_DECAY_BP_PER_100` per wear period and re-leaks when `hp` drops below `LEAK_HP` again; the re-leak is endogenous and gated, and the gate is open because the sev-1 descriptor has been live since the patch (R15). `repair_step(FULL)` requires the Item DB part named by the subject's `def_id` and restores `hp_cap = HP_FULL`. DESTROYED is never repairable; the path is salvage → install. Nothing in the Graph rewards repair: no progression value is written anywhere (brief: no player progression from the Graph).

**LS-24 — Invariants, FAULTED tiers, bounded work.** Two tiers, and every invariant in this GDD belongs to one. *Structural (hard):* edge endpoint slot out of range or pointing at a tombstone; array lengths disagree; ids not strictly ascending; `next_id ≤ max id`; CSR inconsistent with edges; a seam sub-edge whose SeamRecord is missing — checked after every topology mutator in debug, in `post_restore` in all builds, every run in debug; a violation is a kernel step-invariant failure → FAULTED (dev halts; release returns to the menu; no autosave). *Numeric (soft):* an amount below 0, a field above its documented max, conservation drift ≠ 0, `ΔT` clamped — in release clamp to range, increment `graph_saturations[field]` and continue; in debug assert. Every test suite asserts `graph_saturations` is all zeros, so a rounding defect still fails CI without ejecting a player from a 40-minute session. Work per run is bounded by construction (D.16): one pass per loop, `MAX_SPREAD_PER_STEP` failures, `MAX_FOOTPRINTS_PER_STEP` writes, never catch-up; `STEP_BUDGET_MS` (registry 4 ms, "Diagnostic alarm for P3; target is 2 ms") is the kernel's logging threshold, not a cut-off, and the Graph's own budget of record is the sum of its three hooks at **P99 ≤ 0.5 ms and max ≤ 1.0 ms per run at MVP** on the Release export template, VS-A ≤ 1.0 ms P99 per tick after island dormancy and stagger, GDExtension only if VS-A still misses after both (R3).

**LS-25 — Endogenous rates: wear, compression, stress.** Everything that damages a subject without a player or a horror doing it is one of: overload (D.9), wear on period steps (LEAK, OVERHEAT, fatigue, patch decay, and **duty** — `W_DUTY_BP_PER_100` on every running SCRUBBER, PUMP, REACTOR and HEATER, D.9, so that a fully repaired ship is quiet for a session but never permanently safe (P5): at 2 bp per period a healthy machine reaches `HP_OK` after 1500 periods = 4.2 h of continuous duty and carries WORN from then on *(Amended 2026-10-26 — CD-GDD-ALIGN)*), thaw-burst (C5). All of it is multiplied by the section's `rate_pm` (HOME 1000; DERELICT `10^6 / DERELICT_FAULT_COMPRESSION` = 2000, so derelict faults run their A→B window at half length) and by `stress_pm` (`BREACH_PROPAGATION_MULT` 2000 while the section is stressed by M17 STRESS, 1000 otherwise). The multipliers never touch gas exchange, heat conduction or supply: the breach rhythm makes the ship *fail* faster, not *leak* faster — the hole still sets the hiss. On a derelict, faults that are "dead" (dried trails) are authored as subjects at `hp` ≤ `HP_FAIL` with a `def_id` whose module is powered-off, so they emit descriptors with `intensity_pm` 0 and `since_steps` large (the dried trail) and only wake when power or coolant reaches them.

## Formulas

*Scope note.* Every formula below is integer arithmetic over the fields of `graph.state` and the immutable tuning table `GraphTuning`; nothing here reads Attention, sanity, rhythm state or any `PERCEPTION_OUT` value (LS-21 — a formula whose inputs include any of those is a Pillar 4 violation, not a tuning choice). **Every numeric default is provisional until spikes S0–S3 and the MVP playtest** (Section H); the time-band figures in D.15 are the acceptance targets the defaults were tuned to, not the other way round. Units are the R1 game units: gas in mGU (`GAS_SCALE` 1000 mGU per GU; 1 GU is the amount that gives 0.1 kPa in a 1-tile volume), volume in tiles, pressure derived in dkPa (standard `STD_PRESSURE_DKPA` 1000, standard O2 partial pressure `STD_O2_PP_DKPA` 210), temperature in mC, power in W which is identically dJ per step, energy in dJ, liquids in mL, integrity in basis points 0..10000, fractions in permille. One step is `GRAPH_STEP_TICKS` = 6 ticks = 0.1 s at `TICK_RATE` 60; "per step" is the only rate anywhere in this section. Source column vocabulary follows the kernel GDD: `constant` = compiled, changing it is a code change; `data file` = read at boot from `assets/data/life_support_graph.json` into `GraphTuning` (a balance pass edits the file, not the code; a designer-authored float is converted once at boot by `int(round(x × scale))` and never hashed); `calculated` = derived at runtime and never persisted; `state` = a persisted field of `graph.state` (hashed, replicated by summary); `registry` = a locked cross-system fact cited verbatim from `design/registry/entities.yaml`. Three arithmetic rules hold everywhere and are not repeated per formula: (1) **every flux is a non-negative magnitude with an explicit direction**, so integer division is applied only to non-negative operands and truncation is `floor` — written `⌊x⌋`; where a ceiling is wanted it is written `⌈x⌉` and computed as `(num + den − 1) / den` on non-negative operands; the only signed divisions are the two remainder-carrying thermal steps (D.7, D.8), which truncate toward zero and carry the remainder so nothing is lost; (2) **no `sqrt`, `exp`, `pow` or `sin` on the propagation path** — D.15 uses `ln` because it is a design-time estimate evaluated by the test harness, never by the sim; (3) every product the propagation code forms appears in D.13's table with its operand maxima, and the boot assertion there refuses to start a build whose table violates `max_a × max_b < 2^62`.

**D.1 Opening gas flow — mGU moved through one opening per step**

The `opening_gas_flow` formula is defined as:

`Δn = min( ⌊G_OPEN × A × ΔP⌋ , ⌊ΔP × V_a × V_b × GAS_SCALE / ((V_a + V_b) × deg_max)⌋ )` with `ΔP = |P_a − P_b|`, moved from the higher-pressure side to the lower

Derived: when the lower side is EXTERIOR (`P_EXT` = 0), the second term is `⌊n_tot_src / deg_src⌋` and `Δn` is added to the source section's `vented_mgu`
Derived: species split `Δn_o2 = ⌊Δn × n_o2_src / n_tot_src⌋` (0 when `n_tot_src` = 0), `Δn_inert = Δn − Δn_o2`
Derived: per-step fraction `k = G_OPEN × A / (V_src × GAS_SCALE)` — the single number every time-to-band estimate in D.15 is built from

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| G_OPEN | int | 1–10 (default **2**) | data file | mGU moved per (area unit × dkPa × step); the decompression-speed knob |
| A | int | 0–512 (door 64, pinhole 1) | state (`area` of an OPENING edge) or calculated (D.2 virtual hole) | Opening area in 1/64 tile |
| P_a, P_b | int | 0–1500 dkPa | calculated (D.3, from the start-of-phase snapshot) | Pressures on each side; EXTERIOR reads `P_EXT` = 0 (R14) |
| V_a, V_b | int | 4–200 tiles | state (cached from Layout, LS-6) | Volumes |
| GAS_SCALE | int | 1000 | constant (registry candidate) | mGU per GU |
| deg_max | int | 1–16 | calculated (topology refresh) | `max(deg_a, deg_b)` where `deg` counts the OPENING edges and virtual holes of a compartment with area > 0; EXTERIOR counts as degree 1 — the D.12 stability divisor |
| n_o2_src, n_tot_src | int | 0–3×10^7 mGU | state | Composition of the source compartment at the start of the phase |
| Δn | int | 0–`n_tot_src` | calculated | mGU moved this step |

**Output Range:** 0 to the equalisation amount divided by the degree; it never overshoots (the second term is the amount that would exactly equalise two finite volumes, or everything the room holds against vacuum) and it never goes negative (magnitude with direction). It is 0 when `A` = 0, when `ΔP` = 0, and — the truncation bias TD required this document to state — whenever `G_OPEN × A × ΔP < 1`: **equalisation stalls within `⌈1/k⌉` mGU** of the exact balance, which for a pinhole (`A` = 1, `V` = 20) is 10,000 mGU = 0.5 dkPa in that room and for a door is 156 mGU. This is expected behaviour, not a defect: the residual is below the 10 dkPa dirty-key quantum and below anything a gauge or a lung can register. Maximum for a door at full vacuum: `2 × 64 × 1000` = 128,000 mGU/step (a 20-tile room empties through an open hatch in about 160 steps = 16 s; LOW band in 5.6 s — the "chaos" end of the scale, Section C LS-7). The model is linear and unchoked: a vent through a hole slows as the pressure falls, which is the readable behaviour (the hiss fades as the room dies) and the reason no table or square-root law is needed.
**Example:** room a (`V_a` = 20, `P_a` = 1000, `n_tot` = 20,000,000, `n_o2` = 4,200,000, `deg_a` = 2) to room b (`V_b` = 40, `P_b` = 400, `deg_b` = 3) through a door `A` = 64: `ΔP` = 600; first term `2 × 64 × 600` = 76,800; second term `600 × 20 × 40 × 1000 / (60 × 3)` = 480,000,000 / 180 = 2,666,666; `Δn` = 76,800; `Δn_o2` = `⌊76,800 × 4,200,000 / 20,000,000⌋` = 16,128; `Δn_inert` = 60,672. After the step `n_a` = 19,923,200 → `P_a` = `⌊19,923,200 / 20,000⌋` = 996; `n_b` = 16,000,000 + 76,800 → `P_b` = `⌊16,076,800 / 40,000⌋` = 401. Pinhole to vacuum from the same room: `Δn` = `min(⌊2 × 1 × 1000⌋, ⌊20,000,000 / 2⌋)` = 2,000 mGU/step, `k` = 0.0001 per step.

**D.2 Leak geometry and rates from integrity — virtual hole, seam weep, duct and pipe leaks**

The `leak_from_integrity` formula is defined as:

`A_hole = ⌈A_HOLE_MAX × (BREACH_HP − hp) / BREACH_HP⌉` if `hp < BREACH_HP`, else 0

Derived: `A_seam = L_seam × ⌈A_SEAM_MAX × leak_bp × (1000 − q)² / 10^10⌉`
Derived: `leak_mgu = min( pool , ⌈LEAK_MGU_MAX × (LEAK_HP − hp) / LEAK_HP⌉ )` for a DUCT edge or O2_TANK node in mode LEAK; the gas goes to the subject's compartment (`exposed` = false) or to EXTERIOR and `vented_mgu` (`exposed` = true)
Derived: `leak_ml = min( stock , ⌈LEAK_ML_MAX × (LEAK_HP − hp) / LEAK_HP⌉ )` for a PIPE edge, RESERVOIR or FUEL_TANK in mode LEAK; coolant goes to the section's `spilled_cool_ml`, fuel to the compartment's `fuel_puddle_ml`

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| hp | int | 0–10000 bp | state | Integrity of the hull segment, seam, duct, pipe or tank |
| BREACH_HP | int | 1000–6000 (default 4000) | data file | Below this a hull segment is BREACHED and feeds D.1 as a virtual opening to EXTERIOR |
| A_HOLE_MAX | int | 32–128 (default 64) | data file | Hole area at `hp` = 0 — one door's worth |
| A_hole | int | 0–A_HOLE_MAX | calculated | Virtual opening area; not stored, re-derived each run from `hp` |
| q | int | 0–1000 ‰ | state (SeamRecord, set by Assembly at weld, D.14) | Weld quality |
| leak_bp | int | 0–10000 | state (SeamRecord; starts at `SEAM_LEAK_INITIAL_BP`, D.14) | How much of the seam's geometric imperfection currently weeps; wear-in and SEAL_SEAM (V11, VS-A) lower it |
| A_SEAM_MAX | int | 4–32 (default 16) | data file | Area per seam tile at `q` = 0 and `leak_bp` = 10000 |
| L_seam | int | 1–12 tiles | state (SeamRecord) | Seam length |
| A_seam | int | 0–384 | calculated | Seam weep area to EXTERIOR, split evenly across the seam's compartments (remainder to the lower id) |
| LEAK_HP | int | 2000–6000 (default 4000) | data file | Below this a duct, pipe or tank is in mode LEAK |
| LEAK_MGU_MAX | int | 100–2000 (default **500**) | data file | Duct/tank gas leak at `hp` = 0 |
| LEAK_ML_MAX | int | 2–60 (default **20**) | data file | Pipe/reservoir/tank liquid leak at `hp` = 0 |
| pool, stock | int | ≥ 0 | state | What the leak can draw from this step (the pool of connected O2 stores; the node's own liquid) |
| leak_mgu, leak_ml | int | 0–max | calculated | Amount lost this step |

**Output Range:** `A_hole` is 0 at `hp` ≥ 4000, 1 at `hp` = 3999, 64 at `hp` = 0 (a DESTROYED segment is a door-sized hole). `A_seam` is 0 only when `q` = 1000 or `leak_bp` = 0; for any imperfection it is at least 1 per seam tile — the ceiling is deliberate, an imperfect weld always weeps (P1), and the only ways to silence it are wear-in to `leak_bp` = 0 or V11. `leak_mgu` and `leak_ml` are likewise at least 1 the moment `hp` drops below `LEAK_HP` (the first drip is the tell that opens the fairness window, D.11) and are bounded by what exists, so stocks never go negative. Nothing here is persisted: a save stores `hp`, `q`, `leak_bp` and `L_seam` and the geometry is re-derived.
**Example:** hull segment `hp` = 1500 → `A_hole` = `⌈64 × 2500 / 4000⌉` = `⌈40.0⌉` = 40. Fatigued segment at `hp` = 3950 → `⌈64 × 50 / 4000⌉` = `⌈0.8⌉` = 1 (a pinhole). Seam `q` = 850, `L_seam` = 3, `leak_bp` = 10000: `⌈16 × 10000 × 150² / 10^10⌉` = `⌈0.36⌉` = 1 per tile → `A_seam` = 3; in a 20-tile room that is `k` = `2 × 3 / 20,000` = 0.0003 per step, an audible weep that takes about 20 min to reach LOW on its own — findable, fixable, not an emergency. Duct at `hp` = 2000: `leak_mgu` = `⌈500 × 2000 / 4000⌉` = 250 mGU/step. Coolant pipe at `hp` = 3800: `leak_ml` = `⌈20 × 200 / 4000⌉` = `⌈1.0⌉` = 1 mL/step; at `hp` = 3000: 5 mL/step.

**D.3 Atmosphere derivation and bands — pressure, O2 partial pressure, fraction, band with hysteresis**

The `atmosphere_derive` formula is defined as:

`P = ⌊(n_o2 + n_inert) / (V × GAS_SCALE)⌋` ; `p_o2 = ⌊n_o2 / (V × GAS_SCALE)⌋` ; `o2_pm = ⌊1000 × n_o2 / (n_o2 + n_inert)⌋` (0 when the room is empty)

Derived: `band = lookup(value, table LS-7)` with hysteresis — a band is left the step its boundary is crossed in the worsening direction and re-entered only once the value is `H` beyond the boundary in the improving direction (`H_P` = 5 dkPa for `P` and `p_o2`, `H_T` = 500 mC for `T`)
Derived: `pressure_trend = sign(P − P_prev_run)` ∈ {−1, 0, +1}, from the previous run's derived `P` (not persisted; 0 on the first run after restore)

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| n_o2, n_inert | int | 0–3×10^7 mGU | state | Gas amounts per compartment |
| V | int | 4–200 tiles | state (cached from Layout) | Volume |
| P, p_o2 | int | 0–1500 dkPa | calculated | Total and O2 partial pressure |
| o2_pm | int | 0–1000 ‰ | calculated | O2 fraction (display only; physiology and bands use `p_o2`) |
| H_P, H_T | int | 0–20 dkPa (default 5) / 0–2000 mC (default 500) | data file | Hysteresis margins |
| band_o2, band_p, band_t | enum | LS-7 tables | calculated | The felt consequence; the single source of truth for Suit, Failure & Trail, Audio |

**Output Range:** all non-negative; `p_o2 ≤ P` always; `P` for a standard room is 1000 and `p_o2` 210. Pressure depends on `n` and `V` only — there is no temperature-to-pressure coupling (R14), so a cold room does not read low and a hot one does not read high; what the gauge says is what the lungs get. Hysteresis means a value oscillating within 5 dkPa of a boundary flips the band once, not every run; the band change is what dirties `B/<cid>` (D.10).
**Example:** `V` = 20, `n_o2` = 2,900,000, `n_inert` = 15,800,000 → `P` = `⌊18,700,000 / 20,000⌋` = 935 (NORMAL, ≥ 700); `p_o2` = `⌊2,900,000 / 20,000⌋` = 145 (THIN, 140..179); `o2_pm` = `⌊1000 × 2,900,000 / 18,700,000⌋` = 155. If `p_o2` then rises to 182 the band stays THIN until it reaches 185 (`180 + H_P`).

**D.4 Module efficiency and severity from integrity**

The `efficiency_and_severity` formula is defined as:

`eff = clamp( (hp − HP_FAIL) × 1000 / (HP_OK − HP_FAIL) , 0 , 1000 )` ; `sev = 0` if `hp ≥ HP_OK`, else `min( 4 , 1 + (HP_OK − hp) / SEV_STEP )`

Derived: a non-zero `forced_mode` (FREEZE, SCRAM, SEVERED) sets `eff` = 0 and `sev = max(sev, 3)`; a hazard mode may raise `sev` above the hp-derived value by its own rule (a BREACH with `A_hole` ≥ 32 is sev 4; an OVERLOAD at `ρ` ≥ 1500 is sev 3)

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| hp | int | 0–10000 bp | state | Node or edge integrity |
| HP_OK | int | 5000–9000 (default 7000) | data file (registry candidate `HP_OK`) | At or above: OK; below: DEGRADED |
| HP_FAIL | int | 1000–4000 (default 3000) | data file (registry candidate `HP_FAIL`) | At or below: FAILED |
| HP_FULL | int | 10000 | constant (registry candidate `HP_FULL`) | Full integrity; the `hp_cap` of an un-patched subject |
| SEV_STEP | int | 500–3000 (default 1500) | data file | bp per severity level |
| eff | int | 0–1000 ‰ | calculated | Output multiplier applied by D.5, D.6, D.8 |
| sev | int | 0–4 | calculated | Descriptor severity (LS-13); 1 is the trace that opens the D.11 window, 3 is the first `alarm_eligible` level (R7) |

**Output Range:** `eff` is 1000 at `hp` ≥ 7000, 0 at `hp` ≤ 3000 and linear between (a subject half-way through DEGRADED works at half output — the gauge reads it and the hum drops). `sev` is 0 above 7000, 1 at 5501..6999, 2 at 4001..5500, 3 at 2501..4000, 4 at ≤ 2500. The two thresholds are the state boundaries of LS-9, so a state change and a severity change always coincide with a visible change in output.
**Example:** `hp` = 5000 → `eff` = `2000 × 1000 / 4000` = 500; `sev` = `1 + 2000 / 1500` = `1 + 1` = 2. A patched subject at its cap `hp` = 6000 → `eff` = 750, `sev` = 1: a patch is never silent (D.9 patch decay relies on this).

**D.5 O2 supply chain — scrubber output, vent injection, respiration**

The `o2_supply` formula is defined as:

`out_scr = ⌊R_SCR × eff × sat / 10^6⌋` ; `inj = min( ⌊R_VENT × eff_v / 1000⌋ , pool , path_cap , max(0, SP − p_o2) × V × GAS_SCALE )`

Derived: respiration `draw = min(request, n_o2)` per `draw_air` call (M15), applied in phase 0 of the next run in ascending compartment id; the shortfall `request − draw` is returned to Suit and is Suit's business, never the Graph's
Derived: pool accounting per O2 component per run: `pool' = pool + Σ out_scr − Σ inj − Σ leak_mgu`, with stores debited in ascending node id until the amount is met

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| R_SCR | int | 100–1000 (default 400) mGU/step | data file (module def, Item DB) | Nominal scrubber output |
| eff, eff_v | int | 0–1000 | calculated (D.4) | Scrubber and vent integrity multipliers |
| sat | int | 0–1000 | calculated (D.6, tier 0) | Power satisfaction of the scrubber's tier |
| R_VENT | int | 200–1500 (default 600) mGU/step | data file | Vent maximum injection |
| pool | int | ≥ 0 | state (sum of connected O2_TANK stocks) | O2 available to this vent's component |
| path_cap | int | 0–duct cap | calculated (topology refresh) | Bottleneck DUCT or seam capacity between pool and vent; 0 if any VALVE on the only path is closed |
| SP | int | 140–230 dkPa (default 210) | state (vent setpoint, M10) | Target O2 partial pressure |
| p_o2 | int | 0–300 | calculated (D.3) | Current O2 partial pressure of the vent's compartment |
| R_BREATH | int | 100–600 (default 300) mGU/step per crew | data file (Suit-owned; the Graph only applies the request) | Respiration |
| out_scr, inj | int | 0–R_SCR, 0–R_VENT | calculated | mGU produced / injected this step |

**Output Range:** `out_scr` 0..400 and `inj` 0..600 at defaults; never negative, never more than the pool holds, never past the setpoint, never more than the ducts carry. A healthy scrubber (400) outruns one crew (300) by 100 mGU/step; a half-degraded one (200) loses to one crew by 100 — the margin is deliberately thin so that scrubber integrity and scrubber power are both felt. With `path_cap` = 0 (closed valve, cut seam, FAILED duct) the vent is silent and the room is on its own air: the valve verb V5 has an immediate, audible consequence.
**Example:** scrubber `hp` 5000 (`eff` = 500), `sat` = 1000 → `out_scr` = `⌊400 × 500 × 1000 / 10^6⌋` = 200. Vent: `R_VENT` = 600, `eff_v` = 1000, `pool` = 1,500,000, `SP` = 210, `p_o2` = 145, `V` = 20, `path_cap` = 800 → deficit `65 × 20 × 1000` = 1,300,000 → `inj` = `min(600, 1,500,000, 800, 1,300,000)` = 600. One crew in that room draws 300; net +300 mGU/step, so the room climbs toward 210 at 0.015 dkPa/step — about 7 min from THIN back to NORMAL on one vent.

**D.6 Power allocation by tier — island balance and brownout**

The `power_allocation` formula is defined as:

`avail = S + D` ; `served_k = min( R_k , avail − Σ_{j<k} served_j )` for `k` = 0..4 in order ; `sat_k = ⌊1000 × served_k / R_k⌋` (`R_k` = 0 → 1000)

Derived: `batt_used = max(0, Σ served − S)` drawn from BATTERY stocks in ascending node id; `charge = min(S − Σ served, CH_CAP)` when there is surplus, deposited likewise
Derived: reactor output `S_reactor' = clamp(target, S_reactor − RAMP, S_reactor + RAMP)` where `target = out_w_rated × eff` (0 under SCRAM or fuel starve) — the ramp is why `out_w` is persisted
Derived: inrush — a LOAD re-energised within the last `INRUSH_STEPS` requests `⌊req_w × INRUSH_PM / 1000⌋` instead of `req_w`
Derived: every consumer in tier `k` receives `⌊req × sat_k / 1000⌋` and publishes it as `served_pm = sat_k`; edge load for D.9 is the sum of served power on the tree path below the edge (islands are trees by R12, so the path is unique)

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| S | int | 0–10000 W | calculated | Sum of source outputs × eff in the island (reactor ramp-limited) |
| D | int | 0–min(Σ batt_dis_cap, Σ stock) | calculated | Battery discharge available this step |
| R_k | int | 0–5000 W | calculated | Total requested in tier `k` (0 life-critical: scrubber; 1 pumps; 2 doors; 3 lights and the HEATER — light and heat go first, air and coolant last, so a SCRAM is felt as dark, then fog, then frost; 4 other) |
| served_k | int | 0–R_k | calculated | Allocated to tier `k` |
| sat_k | int | 0–1000 ‰ | calculated | Satisfaction of tier `k` |
| CH_CAP | int | 0–500 W (default 300) | data file | Battery charge rate cap |
| RAMP | int | 10–1000 W/step (default 100) | data file | Reactor output change per step |
| BATT_CELL | int | 10^6–10^7 dJ (default 3,600,000) | data file (module def) | One battery's full stock = 360 kJ |
| INRUSH_PM, INRUSH_STEPS | int | 1000–3000 (default 1500) / 0–20 (default 5) | data file | Re-energise surge magnitude and duration (V3's Attention spike and overload risk) |

**Output Range:** tiers are served fully in order; `Σ served ≤ S + D`; no value is negative. The lowest tiers go dark first, and dim in proportion (`sat_3` drives lamp brightness through `served_pm`, P1). A full cell at the tier-0+1 load of the MVP home-ship (1000 W) lasts `3,600,000 / 1000` = 3600 steps = 6 min — the emergency-power clock after a SCRAM.
**Example A:** `S` = 2500, `D` = 1000, `R` = [600, 400, 200, 1500, 300] → all 3000 served, `batt_used` = 500. **Example B (SCRAM):** `S` = 0, `D` = 1000 → `served` = [600, 400, 0, 0, 0], `sat` = [1000, 1000, 0, 0, 0]: lamps out, scrubber and pump running on the battery for 6 min. **Example C (inrush):** breaker closed on a branch carrying a 600 W scrubber and 200 W of lamps at `INRUSH_PM` 1500: for 5 steps the branch requests 900 + 300 = 1200 W; on a 1000 W cable that is `ρ` = 1200 → D.9 takes 16 bp/step for half a second — a scorch tick at the box, and a 1200 W spike in `power_use.delta_w_last5` for Attention.

**D.7 Compartment thermal step — conduction through hull and openings, remainder-carried**

The `thermal_step` formula is defined as:

`Q_net = Q_src_room + Σ Q_in − Σ Q_out − Q_hull` ; `E = t_rem + 1000 × Q_net` ; `ΔT = clamp( trunc(E / C) , −MAX_DT , +MAX_DT )` ; `t_rem' = E − ΔT × C` ; `T' = T + ΔT` with `C = V × C_TILE`

Derived: hull loss `Q_hull = ⌊K_HULL × S_ext × |T − T_amb| / 100000⌋ + ⌊K_HOLE × Σ A_hole × |T − T_amb| / 1000⌋` — an insulated-plate term and a breach-hole term — from the warmer side to the colder; outward loss is added to the section's `radiated_dj`, inward gain subtracted (a signed net counter)
Derived: opening conduction `Q_open = ⌊K_OPEN × A_o × |T_i − T_j| / 4000⌋` per OPENING edge (stored or virtual), moved from the warmer compartment to the colder, computed from the start-of-phase snapshot for every edge before any is applied (Jacobi)
Derived: the pair `(T, t_rem)` is the conserved quantity `U = T × C + t_rem` in milli-dJ; `T` is its intensive projection — this is how "store extensive, derive intensive" (R1) holds for heat while the readable field stays in mC

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| Q_src_room | int | 0–4000 dJ/step | calculated (D.8: machine waste heat after coolant capture, plus HEATER output, plus footprint CHILL/BURN impulses) | Heat entering the room this step |
| K_HULL | int | 10–100 (default **25**) centi-dJ/step/°C per tile (0.25 dJ at default) | data file | Insulated hull conductance — a ship that stays warm on ~1 kW of waste heat needs this below 1 dJ |
| K_HOLE | int | 1–10 (default **2**) dJ/step/°C per area unit | data file | Conductance of a breach hole — uninsulated, and the proxy for heat leaving with the gas (R14 forbids advection) |
| S_ext | int | 0–80 tiles | state (`exposed_tiles`, cached from Layout) | Exterior-facing hull tiles |
| A_hole | int | 0–64 each | calculated (D.2) | Breach hole area |
| T, T_o | int | −200000..+150000 mC | state | Room and neighbour temperatures |
| T_amb | int | −200000..+150000 (default **−30000**) | data file (`T_AMB_MC`, per world region; one value in MVP) | The exterior's effective radiative sink |
| K_OPEN | int | 1–8 (default **4**) | data file | Opening conduction per area unit — 640 dJ/step per 10 °C across an open door, which is what lets one heater warm five rooms |
| A_o | int | 0–512 | state / calculated | Opening area |
| C_TILE | int | 1000–6000 (default 3000) dJ/°C per tile | data file | Thermal mass per tile |
| C | int | 12,000–1,200,000 dJ/°C | calculated | Room thermal mass |
| t_rem | int | −C..+C (exclusive) | state | Carried milli-dJ remainder |
| MAX_DT | int | 500–5000 (default 2000) mC/step | data file | Per-step clamp; a hit increments `graph_saturations[DT_CLAMP]` — a content bug, not a mechanic |
| ΔT | int | ±MAX_DT | calculated | Temperature change this step |

**Output Range:** `T` is bounded below by `T_amb` (the plate term stops within `⌈100000 / (K_HULL × S_ext)⌉` mC of it — 182 mC for the example room: the thermal truncation stall, expected) and above by the balance between `Q_src_room` and the loss terms, `ΔT_eq = Q_src_room / (K_HULL × S_ext / 100 + K_HOLE × Σ A_hole)` °C above ambient. The plate time constant `τ = 100 × C / (K_HULL × S_ext)` is 16,364 steps = 27 min for the example room on its own — a sealed, unheated room is a slow cold — but a hole of area `A` adds `K_HOLE × A` dJ/°C to a plate conductance of only 5.5, so a RUPTURE hole (`A` 7) cuts `τ` to 4,615 steps and a breached room reaches FROST in 3.9 min and freezes its pipes in 12 min (D.15), which is the cold the player meets. **The healthy baseline is the constraint these defaults were set to:** the MVP home-ship (Σ `S_ext` 80 → 20 dJ/°C of plate loss) keeps every compartment in COMFORT on one 500 W heater plus lamp, scrubber and pump waste (≈ 1,020 dJ/step → about 21 °C uniform against a −30 °C sink, with `K_OPEN` 4 moving 640 dJ/step per 10 °C across each open door); an earlier draft at `K_HULL` ≡ 1 dJ and `T_amb` −40000 lost 1,320 dJ/step per outer room and would have held a *healthy* engine room in FREEZING with its pipes' pre-stage permanently lit — a constantly warning healthy ship, which P1 and P2 forbid. With the heater off (power loss) the whole ship cools with `τ_ship` ≈ 15,000 steps: breath fog at 6 min, frost at 13 min, pipes freezing only where a hole adds conductance — the dark is cold, but freezing is a breach's or a derelict's business, which is why `T_amb` is −30000 (a sink only 10 °C below `FREEZE_ON_MC`, the Section G build assertion) and not deeper. Energy is conserved exactly: `Σ U + radiated_dj × 1000` over a section is constant when no machine runs, because every `Q_open` is subtracted once and added once and every `Q_hull` is counted into `radiated_dj`. The remainder is carried, never dropped, so a room 0.5 °C from equilibrium still moves (the stall above is from the hull term's own floor, not from the step).
**Example:** `V` = 30 → `C` = 90,000; `S_ext` = 22; `T` = 40,000; `T_amb` = −30,000; `Q_src_room` = 516 (D.8 example); `t_rem` = 12,500; no openings or holes. `Q_hull` = `⌊25 × 22 × 70,000 / 100,000⌋` = 385 outward; `Q_net` = 516 − 385 = 131; `E` = 12,500 + 131,000 = 143,500; `ΔT` = `trunc(143,500 / 90,000)` = 1; `t_rem'` = 143,500 − 90,000 = 53,500; `T'` = 40,001; `radiated_dj` += 385. Check: `U` before = 40,000 × 90,000 + 12,500 = 3,600,012,500; after = 40,001 × 90,000 + 53,500 = 3,600,143,500; difference 131,000 = 1000 × (516 − 385) exactly. With a RUPTURE hole (`A` 7) in the same room the hole term adds `⌊2 × 7 × 70,000 / 1000⌋` = 980, `Q_net` = −849, and the room cools 9 mC per step.

**D.8 Coolant circulation, heat capture, radiator and the machine temperature proxy**

The `coolant_loop` formula is defined as:

`lvl_f = clamp( (level_pm − L_CAV_LO) × 1000 / (L_CAV_HI − L_CAV_LO) , 0 , 1000 )` ; `circ = ⌊pump_eff × sat × lvl_f / 10^6⌋` ; `Q_cap = min( Q_src , ⌊K_CAP × circ × max(0, T_room − t_cool) / 10^6⌋ )`

Derived: radiator `Q_rad = ⌊K_RAD × eff_r × circ × max(0, t_cool − T_amb_r) / 10^9⌋`, added to the section's `radiated_dj`
Derived: coolant temperature `E_c = tc_rem + 1000 × (Σ Q_cap − Σ Q_rad)`; `Δt_cool = trunc(E_c / (C_COOL × level_ml))`; `tc_rem' = E_c − Δt_cool × C_COOL × level_ml` (with `level_ml` ≥ 1; at 0 the reservoir carries no heat and `Q_cap` is already 0)
Derived: uncaptured heat `Q_src_room = Q_src − Q_cap` enters the machine's compartment (D.7)
Derived: machine temperature proxy `T_mach = T_room + ⌊Q_src_room × K_MACH⌋` — the OVERHEAT trigger (LS-10) and the HOT/shimmer pre-stage that opens the fairness window before a SCRAM
Derived: `circ` through a path is 0 when any PIPE on it is FAILED (cap 0), any VALVE closed, or the PUMP's node is not OK/DEGRADED; `level_pm = ⌊1000 × level_ml / cap_ml⌋`

**D.9 Overload, wear and the damage multipliers — every endogenous hp loss**

The `endogenous_damage` formula is defined as:

`ρ = ⌊1000 × load / cap⌋` ; `Δhp_ovl = min( DHP_MAX , ⌊OVL_K × max(0, ρ − 1000)² / 10^4⌋ )` per step ; `Δhp_wear = W_base` on steps where `step_no mod WEAR_PERIOD_STEPS = 0`, else 0 ; `Δhp = ⌊(Δhp_ovl + Δhp_wear) × rate_pm × stress_pm / 10^6⌋`

Derived: `W_base` is the sum of the wear terms that apply to the subject: `LEAK_WEAR_BP_PER_100` while in mode LEAK; `HEAT_WEAR_BP_PER_100` while in mode OVERHEAT; `HULL_FATIGUE_BP_PER_100` while a HULL_SEGMENT's `fatigued` flag is set; `PATCH_DECAY_BP_PER_100` while `hp_cap < HP_FULL` and `step_no − patched_at_step ≥ PATCH_DECAY_STEPS`; **duty wear** `⌈W_DUTY_BP_PER_100 × duty_pm / 1000⌉` on a SCRUBBER, PUMP, HEATER or REACTOR in state OK or DEGRADED while `duty_pm > 0` (a RADIATOR only if its module def sets `duty_wear` true — off in MVP), where `duty_pm = served_pm` for the three consumers and `⌊1000 × out_w / out_w_rated⌋` for the REACTOR — the ceiling means a machine that runs at all wears at least 1 bp per period *(Amended 2026-10-26 — CD-GDD-ALIGN: no safe harbour, P5)*
Derived: `rate_pm = 1000` for a HOME section, `10^6 / DERELICT_FAULT_COMPRESSION` = 2000 for a DERELICT section; `stress_pm = BREACH_PROPAGATION_MULT` while `step_no < stress_until_step`, else 1000
Derived: the result is applied as `hp' = max(hp − Δhp, floor_hp)` where `floor_hp = HP_FAIL + 1` while the D.11 gate is closed for a subject above `HP_FAIL`, `floor_hp = 1` while it is closed for a subject already FAILED (the gated FAILED → DESTROYED case), and 0 once it is open; `Δhp` never carries over — damage discarded by the gate is gone

**Output Range:** `Δhp_ovl` is 0 for `ρ ≤ 1000`, 1 bp/step at `ρ` = 1050, 4 at 1100, 16 at 1200, 64 at 1400, clamped at 2000 (`ρ` ≥ 3236). Wear is coarse by design: hp falls in visible lumps every 10 s (50 bp for a fatigued plate), so the tell has a rhythm, not a creep. Duty wear is the floor under that rhythm *(Amended 2026-10-26 — CD-GDD-ALIGN)*: 2 bp per period on every running machine is 360 bp over a 30-minute session (a healthy machine ends it at `hp` 9640, still OK, no descriptor — the HEALTHY criterion in Section H) and 720 bp per hour, so `HP_OK` falls at 4.2 h and the machine carries a WORN sev-1 descriptor from then on; a fully repaired ship is therefore quiet for a session and never permanently safe (P5), and because the term is inside `W_base` it is doubled on a DERELICT section and under STRESS like every other wear. **D.11 Fairness gate — `warn_elapsed` and `alarm_eligible`**

The `fairness_gate` formula is defined as:

`warn_elapsed = step_no − warn_since_step` if `warn_since_step > 0` and the subject's `sev ≥ 1` on every run since, else 0 ; `gate_open = warn_elapsed ≥ W_MIN_STEPS` with `W_MIN_STEPS = W_MIN_WARNING_S × TICK_RATE / GRAPH_STEP_TICKS`

Derived: `warn_since_step` is set to `step_no` on the run a subject's `sev` goes 0 → ≥ 1 and reset to 0 on the run it returns to 0 (phase 9); it is persisted
Derived: an **endogenous** transition to FAILED or DESTROYED, and a hull or seam LEAK → BREACH escalation, is applied only if `gate_open`; otherwise hp is floored at `HP_FAIL + 1` (D.9), at 1 for a FAILED subject heading to DESTROYED, or at `BREACH_HP` for the breach case, and the transition happens on the first run the gate opens. **Exogenous** causes — player verbs (cut, vent, salvage, overload by breaker), footprint-channel RUPTURE and GOUGE, `apply_damage` with `cause = EXOGENOUS` — bypass the gate (R7)

**D.12 Stability rule — Σ k ≤ 1 per node, per-edge cap, re-check at weld**

The `stability_rule` formula is defined as:

`Σ_j k_ij ≤ 10^6` for every compartment `i`, with `k_ij = ⌈G_OPEN × A_max_ij × 10^6 / (V_i × GAS_SCALE)⌉` ppm over its OPENING edges and potential virtual holes

Derived: the per-edge runtime cap in D.1 — `Δn ≤ ⌊equalising amount / deg_max⌋` — guarantees the convex combination whatever the current areas are; the boot and weld assertion above guarantees it for the *maximum* areas so that the cap is never the binding term in normal play
Derived: thermal analogue `k_th_ij = K_OPEN × A_o × 10^6 / (4 × C_i)` ppm, asserted the same way (it is three orders of magnitude smaller and never binds)
Derived: `A_max_ij` is the door's full area for a stored OPENING, `A_HOLE_MAX` for every HULL_SEGMENT bound to the compartment, and `L_seam × A_SEAM_MAX` for a seam; `can_join` recomputes the sum for both seam compartments with the plan's openings added and rejects with `STABILITY` if either exceeds 10^6

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| k_ij | int | 0–10^6 ppm | calculated | Fraction of compartment `i`'s imbalance that edge `j` can move in one step |
| A_max_ij | int | 1–512 | data file / calculated | Largest area edge `j` can ever have |
| V_i | int | 4–200 | state | Volume |
| deg_max | int | 1–16 | calculated | As in D.1 |
| Σ_j k_ij | int | 0–10^6 | calculated at boot / weld | The convex-combination bound |

**Output Range:** satisfied with large margin by any MVP content: a 20-tile room with two doors (64 each), two breachable segments (64 each) and a 3-tile seam (48) sums to `2 × 304 × 10^6 / 20,000` = 30,400 ppm — 3 % of the bound. It binds only for absurd content (a 4-tile closet with eight doors: 8 × 32,000 = 256,000 ppm, still fine; a 4-tile closet with sixteen door-sized holes at `G_OPEN` 10 fails). Without this rule a room of degree ≥ 3 under Jacobi exchange can overshoot and oscillate with its neighbours — pulsing gauges and frost that flickers, which looks like a hallucination when it is a bug (TD R1b); with it, S1's monotone-gradient test holds: the maximum `|ΔP|` over all open edges is non-increasing run over run and no edge's flux direction alternates.

**D.14 Seam properties at weld — capacity, integrity, weep, and the disconnected start**

The `seam_at_weld` formula is defined as:

`cap_seam = ⌊min(cap_a, cap_b) × (500 + q / 2) / 1000⌋` per loop sub-edge ; `hp_seam = q × 10` ; `leak_bp = SEAM_LEAK_INITIAL_BP` ; `A_seam` per D.2

Derived: every seam sub-edge is created **disconnected** (R9): OPENING `area` = 0 (hatch closed), PIPE and DUCT sub-edges carry a VALVE state `open` = false, CABLE sub-edges a breaker `closed` = false; each is opened afterwards by its own verb (`set_opening_area`, `set_valve`, `set_breaker`)
Derived: `surge[]` returned by `can_join`, one entry per sub-edge, informational: openings `|P_a − P_b|` dkPa (flag `warn` when > `WELD_DP_WARN`); pipes `|P_a − P_b|` and `|t_cool_a − t_cool_b|`; cables the served/charge differential `|sat_a − sat_b|`
Derived: wear-in `leak_bp' = max(0, leak_bp − SEAM_WEARIN_BP_PER_100)` on each wear period (D.9's cadence) — a seam that is left alone tightens over `SEAM_LEAK_INITIAL_BP / SEAM_WEARIN_BP_PER_100` periods; V11 SEAL_SEAM (VS-A) sets `leak_bp` to 0 at once
Derived *(Amended 2026-10-26 — CD-GDD-ALIGN: the closed-hatch witness, P4)*: every run in phase 11, not only at weld, a stored OPENING at `area` 0 (any door, hatch or seam hatch) emits a `SEAL_STRAIN` descriptor (LS-14 id 26; `subject_kind` OPENING; `sev` 1; `intensity_pm = min(1000, |P_a − P_b|)`; `dir` toward the lower-pressure side and `compartment_id` that side) while `|P_a − P_b| > WELD_DP_WARN` — `medium` GAS, `acoustic` CREAK — or, when only `|T_a − T_b| > HATCH_DT_FROST_MC` holds, with `medium` HEAT, `acoustic` TICK and `compartment_id` the warmer side (the rim frost); it clears with hysteresis when `|ΔP| ≤ WELD_DP_WARN − H_P` and `|ΔT| ≤ HATCH_DT_FROST_MC − H_T` (D.3's margins), and on any run the opening has `area > 0`, where `AIRFLOW` and D.1 take over. With `area` 0 no gas moves (D.1 is 0 at `A` = 0), so this descriptor is the only thing the pressure behind a closed hatch does to the world — and `surge[]` has no other outlet than it and the corruptible instruments

**D.15 Time-to-band estimates — the dread clock, closed-form, against the R21 targets**

**D.16 Dormancy and partition cost bound — what one run may cost**

## Edge Cases

*Every outcome below is the exact behaviour of the Graph owner module — its three hooks, its mutators, its codec — never of a presentation system; "diagnostics" names the counter the Debug Overlay exposes from the Graph's diagnostics channel (`graph_*`, following the kernel's `WRITE_OUTSIDE_SCOPE` style; TD's `graph_saturations[field]` is the numeric-tier counter, LS-24). Where an entry resolves a degenerate strategy from the player-facing design (D1–D9) the countermeasure is the sim rule that defeats it, not a content promise. Nothing here is "handled gracefully": each entry says what the numbers do.*

**Arithmetic and conservation**

- **If two compartments are joined by an opening and the pressure gradient falls below `1 / (G_OPEN × A)` dkPa**: `Δn` floors to 0 and the rooms stop exchanging gas — **equalisation stalls within `⌈1/k⌉` mGU** of exact balance (10,000 mGU = 0.5 dkPa for a pinhole in a 20-tile room; 156 mGU for a door). This is expected behaviour, stated so QA does not file it: the residual is below the 10 dkPa key quantum and below any band boundary's hysteresis, and TD's truncation discipline (floor on non-negative magnitudes) buys exact conservation in exchange for it. Diagnostics: `graph_stall_edges` (count of open edges with `ΔP > 0` and `Δn` = 0 this run; informational).
- **If a compartment's `n_o2 + n_inert` is 0** (vented to vacuum, or a derelict authored empty): `P` = `p_o2` = `o2_pm` = 0 with no division (the `o2_pm` rule returns 0 on an empty room); D.1 from it moves nothing; D.1 into it is bounded by the source's degree share; the band is VACUUM/ANOXIC and the `ATMO_DECOMP` sev 4 descriptor is live. No diagnostics — this is a dead room, which the MVP derelicts contain by design.
- **If a thermal step's `ΔT` would exceed `MAX_DT` (2000 mC)**: `ΔT` is clamped, `t_rem'` carries the whole excess (`E − ΔT_clamped × C`, so `|t_rem|` may exceed `C` for a few runs and the heat is not lost), and `graph_saturations[DT_CLAMP]` increments. A hit in play is a content bug (a BURN footprint or a heater in a 4-tile closet), never a mechanic; every test suite asserts the counter is 0.
- **If a room's temperature is within `⌈100000 / (K_HULL × S_ext)⌉` mC of `T_amb` (182 mC for a 22-tile hull at `K_HULL` 25)**: the plate term floors to 0 and the room holds there — the thermal stall, the sibling of the gas stall. It cannot prevent `FREEZE_ON_MC` (−20000) because `T_AMB_MC` is −30000 and Section G's build assertion `FREEZE_ON_MC ≥ T_AMB_MC + 10000` keeps the margin; a tuning that sets `T_AMB_MC` to −20000 refuses to boot rather than silently making C5 unreachable.
- **If a SCRAMmed reactor is reset the moment its clear condition holds**: it can be — `out_w` ramps to 0 within 25 steps of the SCRAM, `Q_src` with it, so `T_mach` collapses to `T_room` and `STILL_HOT` clears about 3 s after the trip; the cost of a SCRAM is the 25-step ramp back to full output (the lamps come up slowly) and, if the cause is still there (`circ` 0), a second OVERHEAT in 50 steps and a second SCRAM 300 steps later — a 40-second trip-reset cycle the player can hear and must break by fixing the loop. This is the MVP rule (`T_mach` has no inertia of its own; the machine's casing is a proxy, not a store); VS-A may add a persisted machine heat store if playtest wants a SCRAM to cost minutes. Not a defect.
- **If any product on the propagation path would exceed 2^62 because a designer raised a field maximum**: the build does not start — D.13's assertion reports `GRAPH_BOOT_PRODUCT_OVERFLOW(a, b)` with the two operand names. GDScript `int` wraps silently [UNV, S0-b], so overflow is designed out at boot, not detected at run time.
- **If a release build finds an amount below 0, a value above its documented maximum, or a conservation sum that does not close**: the value is clamped to its range, `graph_saturations[field]` increments and the run continues (numeric soft tier, LS-24); the same condition in a debug build asserts and the kernel goes FAULTED. Rationale: a one-mGU rounding defect must not eject a player from a 40-minute session, and CI still fails because every suite asserts the counter array is all zeros.
- **If `step_no` approaches 2^43** (the RNG key's step field): it cannot — 2^43 steps is 27,000 years at 10 Hz. The assertion `subject_id < 2^20` is the live one; `MAX_GRAPH_EDGES` 8192 keeps ids below 2^14 and a build assertion in Section G keeps the cap below 2^20.

**Openings, leaks and breaches**

- **If two leaks open in the same compartment** (two breached segments, or a seam weep and a breach): each is its own virtual OPENING edge in phase 6 with its own `Δn` from the same start-of-phase snapshot, both count toward the room's degree (so `deg_max` rises and each edge's equalisation share falls — D.12's cap), and each emits its own descriptor with its own anchor and `intensity_pm`. The room loses gas at the sum of the two rates; the hiss has two sources, which is the P1 reading the player needs to find both. Diagnostics: none.
- **If a DUCT in mode LEAK is `exposed` = false** (runs inside the ship): its `leak_mgu` moves from the pool into the duct's compartment — conserved, so the room's `P` rises above 1000 (a 250 mGU/step leak lifts a 20-tile room 0.75 dkPa per minute) and the room pushes a draught out of its doors (`AIRFLOW` descriptor). It is a loss of regulation, not of air; the ship-clock fault of LS-26 is therefore authored on an `exposed` duct, whose leak goes to EXTERIOR and `vented_mgu`.
- **If a VALVE upstream of a leaking PIPE or DUCT is closed**: `path_cap` to the leak becomes 0, `leak_ml`/`leak_mgu` draws from nothing, the mode LEAK stays (hp is unchanged) but the descriptor's `intensity_pm` is 0 — the drip stops, the frost stops growing, the sight-glass stops falling. Everything downstream of the valve is STARVE'd of fluid. The valve is the surgical verb (V5); its wrong use is "a silent dead consumer elsewhere", exactly as the player design intends.
- **If a vent's only path to the pool crosses a FAILED duct, a closed valve or a CUT seam**: `path_cap` = 0, `inj` = 0, the vent is silent and the room is on its own air — THIN in `⌈ln(7.2/6.6)/k⌉`-type times from respiration alone (23 min per crew in a sealed 20-tile room with no leak). The vent's node carries STARVE (medium GAS) so the "off, not broken" distinction is readable.
- **If an O2_TANK is DESTROYED** (`hp` 0): its stock leaks at `LEAK_MGU_MAX` per step (500) into its compartment or to EXTERIOR by its `exposed` flag until empty; it still counts in the pool only for what remains. A tank that was the pool's only store leaves the vents with `pool` 0 within `stock / 500` steps.
- **If a hull segment reaches `hp` 0 by an endogenous cause**: the transition FAILED → DESTROYED is gated like any other (the segment has carried sev ≥ 3 since `BREACH_HP`, so the gate is long open) and `A_hole` becomes `A_HOLE_MAX` — a door-sized hole, VACUUM in 36 s for a 20-tile room. It is not repairable; the room is lost until a plate is installed (M5 on the segment's hardpoint, VS-A content) — "loss is not total" (P5): the ship is not.
- **If a breach opens in a compartment whose stored OPENINGs to neighbours are all at `area` 0**: the loss stays in that room (C6 does not propagate), its neighbours' `AIRFLOW` descriptors stay silent, and the sealed doors are the reason — this is the designed payoff of V4 and the reason the pull-toward-the-hole descriptor matters only on the breached side.

**Fairness gate**

- **If an endogenous failure would cross `HP_FAIL` before `warn_elapsed` reaches 100** (an overload at `ρ` ≥ 1317, 40+ bp/step) *(Amended 2026-10-26 — CD-GDD-ALIGN)*: `hp` holds at `HP_FAIL + 1` and the excess damage is discarded, run after run, until the gate opens; on that run the pending transition applies with whatever damage that run brings. The hold is at most `100 − warn_elapsed` steps: 37 steps (3.7 s) for the D.11 example at `ρ` 1400, and nearly the whole 10 s for an arc-flash at `DHP_MAX` (sev 1 on step 2, `HP_FAIL` reached on step 4, held to step 102). Diagnostics: `graph_gate_holds` (subject, steps held). *Alternative considered:* deferring the discarded damage as a debt — rejected because a debt makes the eventual FAILED → DESTROYED jump larger than the physics, which is a surprise the gate exists to prevent.
- **If an exogenous cause (RUPTURE, GOUGE, `apply_damage` EXOGENOUS, a RAGGED cut, a breaker closed onto an overload, a salvage) takes a subject below `HP_FAIL` or to 0 in one apply**: the transition happens that run, ungated, with `cause` = EXOGENOUS on the descriptor. The player did it or a horror did it; the gate protects against the sim, not against agency. `warn_since_step` is set on that run so that any *later* endogenous escalation (the gouged plate's fatigue) is gated normally.
- **If an endogenous and an exogenous cause damage the same subject on the same run**: phase 0 applies the exogenous damage first (queued inputs), phase 8 applies the endogenous damage against the gate; the floor is computed from the post-exogenous `hp`, so a GOUGE that lands at 3200 and an overload that wants 300 more leave the cable at 3001 until the gate opens — the exogenous part is never held, the endogenous part always is.
- **If a subject's `sev` returns to 0 before the gate opens** (a leak patched at `hp` 4100 after 30 steps): `warn_since_step` resets to 0 and a later decline starts a fresh window. A warning that stopped is not a warning the player can still be charged with.
- **If a forced mode's pre-stage is skipped by content** (a derelict authored at `T_room` −25000 with liquid pipes): on the first run the pipes show FREEZE sev 1 and `warn_since_step` is set; they may freeze solid only 100 steps later *(Amended 2026-10-26 — CD-GDD-ALIGN)*. A derelict that is "already frozen" must be authored with `forced_mode` = FREEZE in its template, which is a content fact, not an endogenous transition, and is not gated. The content lint checks for the inconsistent case (liquid pipe below `FREEZE_ON_MC` without the mode) and reports it.
- **If `alarm_eligible` is requested by a reader for a descriptor at sev 3 whose gate is still closed**: it is false; the sweep may not run; the hum, glow and scorch are the only channels (R17 "never first"). Any consumer that plays an alarm from `sev` alone, bypassing the flag, is in breach of the exported requirement and the (lint) criterion in Section H catches it.

**Patch, repair, salvage, replace**

- **If a patch (`PATCH_CAP_BP` 6000) is applied to a leaking pipe**: `hp` rises to at most 6000, LEAK clears (`hp ≥ LEAK_HP`), the drip stops, but the subject stays DEGRADED at sev 1 (`eff` 750) — a patch weeps. After `PATCH_DECAY_STEPS` (2400 = 4 min) it loses `PATCH_DECAY_BP_PER_100` (100) per wear period and re-enters LEAK when `hp < 4000`, 20 periods = 200 s later; the re-leak is endogenous and gated, and the gate is already open because the sev-1 descriptor has been live for 4 min (R15). A second patch restarts the clock; a FULL repair (Item DB part) ends it by restoring `hp_cap`. Diagnostics: none — the decay is the rule.
- **If a duty machine runs at partial `served_pm`** (a brownout, a throttled pump) *(Amended 2026-10-26 — CD-GDD-ALIGN)*: its duty wear scales with `duty_pm` — `⌈W_DUTY_BP_PER_100 × duty_pm / 1000⌉` per period, so at the default 2 bp a machine at `served_pm` 1..500 loses 1 bp per period and at 501..1000 loses 2; a machine at `served_pm` 0 (STARVE, breaker open, SCRAM) loses nothing, and a FAILED or DESTROYED one is not running and loses nothing. The ceiling is deliberate (a machine that runs at all wears), the coarseness is accepted (it is 1 or 2 bp per 10 s under a 250 bp dirty-key quantum), and the only way to stop duty wear is to stop the machine — which has its own cost on every other clock. Diagnostics: none.
- **If `repair_step` targets a DESTROYED subject**: the owner command's `validate` fails with `NOT_REPAIRABLE` via `check_repair_step`; no apply. The path is `salvage_node` (scrap) then `install_module`; a DESTROYED hull segment is replaced through its hardpoint.
- **If a subject is at `hp` 0, SALVAGED or ABSENT** — three different things: `hp` 0 is DESTROYED, present, ports connected, leaking at maximum (a DESTROYED duct leaks 500 mGU/step, a DESTROYED plate is a 64-area hole), descriptors live at sev 4; SALVAGED is a tombstoned slot whose fluid ports are `open_end` (SEVERED on the edges that touched it, leaking at `OPEN_END_RATE_PM` of cap until `cap_port`), no descriptor of its own; ABSENT is an empty hardpoint, inert, with no ports connected and nothing to leak. The reader's rule: DESTROYED is the loudest thing in the room, SALVAGED hisses at the stubs, ABSENT is silence.
- **If the player salvages the node that is currently running the ship** (the home-ship BATTERY during a SCRAM, the only SCRUBBER): M6 applies (it is a verb, exogenous), the island re-solves next run with `D` = 0 or `out_scr` = 0, every dependant goes STARVE on that run with HUM_DROP descriptors, and the room clocks begin. The tell precedes the act — the hum of the thing being cut is its warning — and the "stupid but plausible death" is nameable afterwards. No special case.
- **If `install_module` is called with a `def_id` whose ports do not match the hardpoint's declared port types**: `check_install_module` fails with `PORT_INCOMPAT`; the Item DB's loot and the Layout's hardpoints are responsible for never offering it, and the lint over templates reports a hardpoint with no compatible module.
- **If `reset_node` is called on a SCRAMmed reactor while `T_mach ≥ OVERHEAT_OFF_MC` or with `starve_steps > 0`**: the result is `BLOCKED` with the physical reason (`STILL_HOT` or `NO_FUEL`), not a command reject — the casing is still scorching, the sight-glass is empty; the player reads why. The command succeeds (it was valid) and did nothing.

**Seams, weld and cut**

- **If a weld joins the home-ship to a section whose compartments are at vacuum** (a dead derelict): nothing moves (R9) — the seam OPENING is created at `area` 0, the seam DUCT valve closed, the seam CABLE breaker open; the home-ship's pressure, temperature and charge are unchanged on the weld run and every run after it until the player opens something. `can_join` returned `surge` = 1000 dkPa for the opening with `warn` = true (> `WELD_DP_WARN` 300) — not a reject, and not a number: from the weld run the closed seam hatch carries a `SEAL_STRAIN` descriptor (CREAK at the rim, `intensity_pm` 1000, rim frost because `|ΔT|` 50,000 > `HATCH_DT_FROST_MC`) and the corruptible gauge swings *(Amended 2026-10-26 — CD-GDD-ALIGN)*. Opening the hatch is then the door verb (V4 through `set_opening_area`) with the full consequence: 128,000 mGU on the first step, LOW in the home-ship room within 6 s if it is the only connected room. The soft-lock of the first draft (welding drains the ship) cannot occur.
- **If a hatch under `SEAL_STRAIN` is opened** (`set_opening_area` > 0 at `|ΔP|` 850) *(Amended 2026-10-26 — CD-GDD-ALIGN)*: the mutator applies in P2; on the next run the opening has `area > 0`, so phase 11 emits no `SEAL_STRAIN` for it and the gas flow follows D.1 in that run's phase 6 (108,800 mGU on the first step at `A` 64), with `AIRFLOW` taking the hatch's descriptor slot from the same run; the ear-pressure band and the draught are the consequence. If the hatch is instead left closed the descriptor clears only when `|ΔP|` falls to `WELD_DP_WARN − H_P` (295) — through the seam weep, a leak on either side or the player venting — and the rim frost only when `|ΔT|` falls to `HATCH_DT_FROST_MC − H_T` (19,500) through `K_OPEN` conduction, which a closed door still has. Nothing moves through a closed hatch at any `|ΔP|`: a hatch never bursts, because `area` is the Graph's and only M13 writes it. Diagnostics: none.
- **If a weld joins a dead section** (no power, batteries flat, every node FAILED or SALVAGED): the seam CABLE's breaker starts open; closing it (V3) merges the islands and, if the derelict's bus is a dead short (a FAILED CABLE_JUNCTION at `hp` 2000 under load → SHORT), the home-ship's cable serving the seam overloads per D.9 with its OVERLOAD descriptor from the first step — the scorch at the seam box is the tell, the breaker is the fix. Nothing on the derelict wakes until served.
- **If a weld joins a frozen section** (pipes in FREEZE, reservoir frozen): the seam PIPE valve starts closed; opening it (V5) connects a path through a FAILED (frozen) pipe whose `cap` is 0, so `circ` through that path is 0 and the home-ship's coolant loop is unaffected except that the seam pipe itself now sits in a FROST room and begins its own FREEZE pre-stage. Thawing the section (heater, M14) brings the burst roll (C5) — the readable cost of growth.
- **If a weld's seam CABLE has `cap_seam` lower than the load the player then routes across it** (a `q` 300 weld gives 65 % of the lesser cap): the seam cable overloads under D.9 with its OVERLOAD descriptor at the seam — a rough weld is a weak bus, which the player sees before it fails. `q` is Assembly's number; the Graph only makes its consequence physical.
- **If a seam is cut `SEALED`**: openings removed, sub-edges tombstoned, both sides' fluid ports `capped`, no leak on either side, one batch `T` + `S/<seam_id>` + affected `C/`; the derelict's compartments keep their gas. **If cut `RAGGED`**: each side's compartment gets a virtual hole `A_CUT` 24 on a hull stub at `hp` 2500 (sev 3 BREACH, exogenous, ungated) — DECOMP in `ln(2.5)/(2 × 24 / 20,000)` = 382 steps = 38 s for a 20-tile room — and every fluid port is `open_end`, hissing at `OPEN_END_RATE_PM` of its cap until `cap_port` (M12). Items in the section are Assembly's and the Item DB's loss; the Graph only takes the air.
- **If a breach opens on either side while Assembly's weld channel is in progress** (before the `weld` command's apply): the Graph does nothing special — the breach is an ordinary D.2 event in its own section; when `weld_join` applies, the seam opening starts at 0 so the breach cannot cross into the other section until the player opens the hatch, and `can_join` (run at the command's `validate`, the same tick as apply) reports the now-larger `surge`. The "breach window during the weld" the player design describes is the seam's own weep (`A_seam` ≥ `L_seam` from the weld run) plus the player's choice to open — not a hole the weld creates.
- **If a weld plan would close a cycle in POWER wiring** (both sections already share an island through another seam's cable, or the plan pairs two cables): `can_join` fails with `POWER_CYCLE` and the command is rejected at `validate`; the same check at data load refuses a template with a cycle. R12: islands are trees, no iterative solver; VS-A may lift it with a routing rule, not by removing the check.
- **If a weld or a derelict load would exceed `MAX_GRAPH_NODES` (4096) or `MAX_GRAPH_EDGES` (8192)**: `can_join` returns `CAPACITY` (domain reject `VALIDATION_FAILED(graph.CAPACITY)`); M1 refuses the section with the same reason before allocating anything. The caps are set so that MVP (≤ 120 nodes) and VS-A (≤ 1,200) content never reach them; the content lint reports any template within 10 % of a cap.
- **If a weld plan passes `can_join` but the D.12 sum for a seam compartment would exceed 10^6 ppm**: `can_join` returns `STABILITY` — in practice only for absurd content (Section D.12); the reject names the compartment and the sum so the content author can see it.
- **If `can_join` is called from presentation (the weld preview) rather than from `validate`**: it is pure and allowed; it may call `ensure_derived()`, which rebuilds non-hashed caches and leaves `state_hash` unchanged with zero dirty keys (S2's atomicity criterion).

**Degenerate strategies (D1–D9 from the player-facing design)**

- **If the player seals every door and lives in one room (D1)**: the room's O2 falls by `R_BREATH` per crew per step with no vent supply unless its vent has `path_cap` > 0 and the pool is fed — which needs the scrubber powered, which needs the reactor fuelled and cooled; the sealed room's `P` and `T` are its own, so a single hull plate's fatigue (50 bp per period, content-authored on outer rooms) is its only breach risk but its heat loss through `S_ext` continues; door motors are tier-2 LOADs that draw while holding; and nothing the Graph does refills the fuel tank or the O2 tank. The bot test in Section H drives this strategy for 30 sim-minutes and asserts the room reaches THIN. Countermeasure is the physics, not a script.
- **If the player tries to obtain unlimited power (D2)**: `S` is the reactor's rated `out_w × eff` ramp-limited; `D` is bounded by `Σ stock`; `CH_CAP` caps recharge at 300 W; there is no second source in MVP; the reactor's heat needs `circ` > 0 or `T_mach` reaches OVERHEAT in 35 s; and Attention reads `power_use`, so surplus draw is never free. The bot test asserts no command sequence of ≤ 200 commands raises `Σ stock` above the initial cell plus the reactor's integrated surplus.
- **If the player repeats patches to farm anything (D3)**: `repair_step` writes `hp` and `hp_cap` only; the Graph has no progression field, no loot hook and no reward callback (brief: no player progression from the Graph); patch kits are the Item DB's finite consumable; a patch decays. There is nothing to farm.
- **If the player idles in a sealed scrubber room ignoring sorties (D4)**: the fuel tank drains at `FUEL_UML_PER_W` (111 min for 20,000 mL at 2500 W), outer plates authored `fatigued` creep toward breach, and the heater's draw keeps the reactor burning; the Graph's clocks run whether or not the player acts. The meso-loop pull is the concept's; the Graph supplies the slow decay.
- **If the player vents every compartment to purge hazards (D5, V6)**: the vented room's gas goes to `vented_mgu` and is gone; refilling it from the pool costs `(STD_O2_PP_DKPA × V × GAS_SCALE)` of O2 per room (4,200,000 mGU for a 20-tile room — more than the MVP tank holds) at `R_VENT` 600 per step (2 h per room on one vent); the room's heat is lost with its air only through the open hole's conduction (`A_hole` in D.7) — venting is the nuclear option because the pool is finite.
- **If the player keeps the breakers off permanently to hide (D6)**: every load behind the open breakers (scrubber, heater, pump, lamps, door motors) is unserved → STARVE; the pool stops refilling, the ship cools toward −30 °C with `τ_ship` 25 min — breath fog at 6 min, frost at 13 min, FREEZING at 31 — and any breached room freezes its pipes in 12 min with the burst roll waiting on thaw; door motors do not hold. The Graph makes the dark expensive; the suit clock is Suit's.
- **If the player cycles weld and cut to duplicate anything (D7)**: a weld adds edges only (no nodes), a cut tombstones them; `cut_detach` never creates a node; `discard_section` destroys mass into `discarded_*`; ids are never reused; the section item is Assembly's and conserves the section. No Graph quantity is created by any mutator except M1 (content instantiation) and M5 (an Item DB part).
- **If the player plays by sound alone, never looking (D8)**: every descriptor has an `acoustic` class and every band a felt consequence; the Graph is indifferent. Not degenerate; listed so no one "fixes" it.
- **If the player save-scums a rupture (D9)**: the RNG is keyed by `(step_no, subject_id)` from `world_seed`, so a thaw-burst roll at step N on edge E has the same outcome after any number of reloads of the same world; the only way to change it is to change the state (thaw later, patch first). Single-slot autosave is Save/Load's and the user's decision (R16); the Graph's determinism removes the incentive either way.

**Mutators, API and dirty keys**

- **If Suit's `draw_air` requests more O2 than the compartment holds** (`request > n_o2`): the grant is `n_o2`, the room goes to `n_o2` = 0 (ANOXIC), and the shortfall `request − granted` is returned to Suit, which owns what a lungful of nothing does. Multiple crew in one room are applied in ascending crew order within phase 0 against the running balance, so the last to breathe gets the least — order is deterministic (the queue is sorted by (compartment id, crew id)). Diagnostics: `graph_draw_shortfall` (total mGU unmet this run).
- **If a Class B mutator (M14–M18) is called with an unknown, tombstoned or dormant-section id**: it is a no-op returning 0/false and increments `graph_stale_calls`; it never rejects (CR-9 path (a) cannot fail inside an apply). A `set_load_request` on a SALVAGED lamp changes nothing; a `deposit_trace` on a discarded section is dropped.
- **If the footprint channel receives more than `MAX_FOOTPRINTS_PER_STEP` (16) writes in one run**: the first 16 in arrival order within P2 are honoured, the rest are counted in `graph_footprints_dropped` and dropped — not deferred, because a horror's trace is a moment, not a queue. A single call above its per-kind cap (`FOOTPRINT_MAX_Q`, `FOOTPRINT_MAX_GOUGE_BP`) is clamped to the cap and counted in `graph_footprints_clamped`.
- **If `set_opening_area` is called with `area > area_max`** (the door's full size): clamped to `area_max` by the Class A check (`AREA_RANGE`); a door cannot be opened wider than it is. A hatch on a seam has `area_max` from the weld plan.
- **If a breaker is closed onto a branch whose request exceeds the cable's cap** (V3 after a short, or inrush on a loaded branch): the cable's OVERLOAD descriptor appears on the same run's phase 11 (sev 1 at `ρ` > 1000, sev 3 at ≥ 1500) and D.9 damage begins; the transition that follows is **exogenous** (overload by breaker, R7) for the first `INRUSH_STEPS` and endogenous thereafter — the player who slams a breaker onto a short gets the arc. Attention sees the spike in `delta_w_last5`.
- **If a run would emit more than `MAX_DIRTY_KEYS` (256) keys** (a derelict instantiation of 35 nodes + 45 edges + 5 compartments with every band and descriptor; a VS-A multi-section cascade beyond D.10's 135): the kernel collapses the notification to `ALL` and every subscriber re-reads its view once; the Graph does nothing different. `ALL` is legitimate at structural events and the S1 spike reports its frequency in a breach window; steady state must stay ≤ 32 keys P95 or the quanta are revisited — the kernel constant is not.
- **If a continuous value crosses a quantum boundary back and forth on consecutive runs** (`P` at 699/700 with a pinhole): the `C/<cid>` key fires on each crossing — at most once per run, bounded by the run rate; bands do not flicker because of `H_P`, and the key cost is 1 per run per such room. If more than a handful of rooms sit on a quantum boundary at once the P95 criterion catches it and the quantum, not the kernel constant, is adjusted.
- **If a reader subscribes with class PRESENTATION and polls `read_view` between hook ticks**: it reads the values committed at the last Graph run (up to 5 ticks old); there is no interpolation inside the Graph, and `tick_alpha` is the presentation's business. Readers that need the same-run value (Suit, Attention) are hooks with `after:` and read in the same tick.

**Hooks, order, FAULTED, budget**

- **If a structural invariant fails in `graph.propagate.failure` while a cascade is mid-flight** (phase 10 has queued `forced_mode_next` on twelve subjects and an edge endpoint is found pointing at a tombstone): the kernel's step-invariant path fires — FAULTED, `Dropped(FAULTED)` for the tick's commands, no P5 notification, no autosave; the partial writes of the run stay in the block for the diagnostic dump; the dev build halts, the release build returns to the menu. Because `forced_mode_next` is persisted, the last good snapshot (taken at a tick boundary) carries the cascade as it stood then and a restore resumes it exactly — no hop is lost or doubled. Diagnostics: the kernel's FAULTED record names the invariant (`GRAPH_INV_EDGE_ENDPOINT(edge_id)` etc.).
- **If a mutator's assertion fails after a passing `validate`** (a bug, since `check_*` is meant to make apply infallible): FAULTED with partial writes, per kernel CR-9 — the fault-injection criterion in Section H proves the path. Mutators are written so that the only operations after the check are array appends, element writes and flag sets, with no lookup that can miss.
- **If the kernel pauses, snapshots or saves "mid-step"**: it cannot — the three hooks are atomic inside P3 of one tick, every buffered delta is applied before `graph.propagate.atmo` returns, and the kernel snapshots only at tick boundaries after P5. A snapshot therefore never observes a half-applied phase, `last_step_no` always equals the last completed run, and the restored block resumes with the next run. PAUSED simply stops ticks; the Graph has no wall-clock dependence.
- **If the kernel's lag policy drops ticks** (`LAG_FORGIVE_TICKS`): the Graph runs on whichever ticks the kernel steps and never catches up — a dropped tick that was a hook tick is a step that did not happen, simulated time is shorter, and the clocks in D.15 are measured in steps that ran. No catch-up, no double step (LS-1 (j)).
- **If a run exceeds `STEP_BUDGET_MS`** (4 ms): the kernel logs it; the Graph does not truncate, skip a phase or change any result — the budget is a diagnostic, and the Graph's own bound (`GRAPH_HOOK_BUDGET_MS`, measured in S1) is what approval rests on. Diagnostics: the kernel's overrun log carries the three hooks' individual times from the diagnostics wrapper, never visible to sim code.
- **If `MAX_SPREAD_PER_STEP` (32) is reached in phase 10**: the 33rd and later candidates (in ascending subject id) are not queued this run and are re-evaluated next run from the same persisted states; the front advances at most 32 subjects per 0.1 s. A fully dead bus of 40 loads takes two runs to show every STARVE. Diagnostics: `graph_spread_deferred`.
- **If a section is dormant** (derelict unloaded or zoomed out): its slots are skipped by every phase — no leak, no cooling, no wear, no roll; its atmosphere and `warn_since_step` values are frozen in place; `can_join` against it returns `SECTION_NOT_LOADED`; its state is in every snapshot and hash unchanged. Loading it resumes from the same values with no fast-forward (R13): a derelict left mid-breach is still mid-breach when the player returns, with `since_steps` reflecting the steps it *ran*, not the time away — which is what "the derelict does not degrade on its own" means and what Web Map's VS-A deltas will serialise.
- **If a sealed room with a running scrubber or heater is a candidate for VS-A quiescence**: it does not sleep — the rule is "every flux 0 **and** every node produced and consumed nothing"; a producer or consumer keeps the island awake. A truly sealed, dead, equalised room sleeps after `QUIESCE_STEPS` and wakes on any mutator touching it or a non-zero flux across a seam into it.
- **If the VS-A hook stagger moves `graph.propagate.atmo` to offset 2**: Suit's `after: graph.propagate.atmo` must share the offset or it reads a run-old atmosphere (kernel edge case); nothing in the Graph's rules changes, because no rule assumes same-tick cross-loop consistency (LS-8). This document records it so the stagger is a two-registration change, not a multi-GDD one.

**Serialization and restore**

- **If a snapshot is restored with any of the following**, restore fails with the named reason, the live block is untouched (two-phase: decode and validate into scratch, swap only on success) and the kernel's restore-failure path reports it — never a silent default, never a partially applied world: `GRAPH_RESTORE_BAD_VERSION(stored, supported)` (anything but 1 in MVP, including 0 and `current + 1`); `GRAPH_RESTORE_BAD_TAG(tag)` (unknown or duplicated field tag); `GRAPH_RESTORE_BAD_LENGTH(field, expected, got)` (array lengths disagree or a field is truncated); `GRAPH_RESTORE_BAD_ENUM(field, slot, value)` (state, kind, mode, port type, loop or edge kind outside the type tables); `GRAPH_RESTORE_OUT_OF_RANGE(field, slot, value)` (a value 1 above its documented max or below 0 — a numeric *soft* violation in play is a *hard* failure on load, because a corrupt save must not be "fixed" into a different world); `GRAPH_RESTORE_ID_ORDER(slot)` (ids not strictly ascending); `GRAPH_RESTORE_COUNTER(counter, max_id)` (`next_id ≤ max id`); `GRAPH_RESTORE_BAD_EDGE_ENDPOINT(edge_id)` (slot out of range or a live edge pointing at a tombstone); `GRAPH_RESTORE_SEAM_MISMATCH(seam_id)` (a seam sub-edge whose record is missing, or a record naming a dead edge); `GRAPH_RESTORE_UNKNOWN_DEF(def_id)` (the Item DB does not know the module — silent substitution would change ports); `GRAPH_RESTORE_UNKNOWN_ROOM(room_ref)` (Layout, restored first, has no such room); `GRAPH_RESTORE_INVARIANT(name)` (any LS-24 structural check that the above did not already name). S3's 1,000-mutation fuzz drives each.
- **If a restore succeeds**: `post_restore` rebuilds every derived cache (components, trees, `path_cap`, degrees, `V` and `exposed_tiles` from Layout, id → slot maps), seeds the last-emitted dirty buckets from the restored values, emits exactly one `ALL`, and the next hook tick runs step `last_step_no + 1`. The canonical bytes immediately after restore are byte-identical to the snapshot's (tombstones included) — the S3 criterion.
- **If a save is written as JSON text by Save/Load**: every Graph int must come back through `int()` and be range-checked, and the Graph's packed arrays must travel as base64 of `var_to_bytes(allow_objects = false)` — constraint S-1, routed to Save/Load; a save that stored a packed array as a JSON number list is rejected by `GRAPH_RESTORE_BAD_TAG` because the Graph's decoder accepts only its tagged byte form. The Graph's own values are all below 2^53, so the float hazard is the kernel's 64-bit counters' and the hash's, not this block's — but the decoder does not rely on that.
- **If `restore_after: [layout.state]` is not honoured** (Layout restored after the Graph): `post_restore` finds no rooms and fails with `GRAPH_RESTORE_UNKNOWN_ROOM` for the first compartment — loud, by design, because a compartment without a volume cannot derive a pressure. The kernel's block ordering is the fix, not a default `V`.
- **If a template's `def_id` is renamed between builds**: every save referencing it fails with `GRAPH_RESTORE_UNKNOWN_DEF` — pre-release saves are disposable (SD OQ-11, no migration before the first public build); after release a rename needs a migration step that maps the old string, which is why `def_id` is a string and type ids are numbers.

**Content and composition**

- **If a derelict template contains a liquid pipe, a vent and a hull segment all in one 4-tile compartment with eight openings**: D.12's sum is `⌈2 × (8 × 64 + 64 + 48) × 10^6 / 4000⌉` = 312,000 ppm — accepted; the content lint also reports any compartment above 500,000 ppm as "fragile" so a later `G_OPEN` change does not surprise. Only a sixteen-door closet at `G_OPEN` 10 is refused.
- **If a template places the HEATER (500 W, `waste_permille` 1000) in a sealed 4-tile compartment** (`C` 12,000, `S_ext` 4 → 1 dJ/°C of plate loss): equilibrium is 500 °C above ambient — SCORCH within 2 min and `MAX_DT` saturations on the way; with its door open to a corridor the 640 dJ/step per 10 °C of `K_OPEN` conduction carries the heat out and the closet sits a few degrees above the corridor. The content lint computes `ΔT_eq` for every heated compartment *with its doors closed* and reports any above 60 °C; the heater's `req_max` is a module-def cap, not a Graph clamp.
- **If a fuel puddle (`fuel_puddle_ml > 0`) sits in a compartment that goes below 0 °C**: nothing changes in MVP — the puddle descriptor stays, fire is VS-A (R8), and the Body's "slows walking" is read from `fuel_puddle_ml` whether frozen or not. VS-A may add a `frozen` presentation hint from the room band; the Graph will not add a field for it.
- **If the home-ship's cryo bay is authored at `T` −2000 mC (FROST band) so the first fault's coolant drip frosts** (LS-26): the room reads FROST from the first run with its `ATMO_COLD` sev 2 descriptor, the heater (tier 3, 500 W) warms it at `ΔT` ≈ 500 × 1000 / 60,000 = 8 mC per step once powered — 2.5 min to COMFORT — and the frost trail from the leak stops growing when the band leaves FROST. The onboarding sequence is a physical one: restore power, feel the room warm, follow the drip to the clamp, patch it.
- **If Layout changes a compartment's `V` while the world runs** (not possible in MVP; a VS-A Assembly feature): the Graph re-bases the energy pair so `U = T × C + t_rem` is preserved (`T` unchanged, `t_rem` recomputed against the new `C`) and `P` changes by the volume ratio on the next derivation — the gas did not move, the room did. Recorded here so the VS-A rule is written against conservation, not against `P`.

## Visual/Audio Requirements

The Graph draws nothing and plays nothing. It owns the **semantics** that Failure & Trail, In-World Instruments and Adaptive Audio render, and it constrains them:

- **Descriptors, not visuals.** Every presentation of a failure is derived from a `FailureDescriptor` (mode, medium, severity 0–4, intensity ‰, anchor ids, acoustic class, `alarm_eligible`). Medium selects the art bible's trail word — O₂ → mist, coolant → frost, power → scorch, fuel → drip, integrity → hairline/buckling — and severity selects the band; the Graph never names a colour, a sprite or a sound.
- **One trail per source, continuous along its edge** (art bible rule). A descriptor carries the source anchor and the edge it travels, so a trail is thickest at the source and never appears at a node without upstream evidence on the connecting edge. Trails are on the consequence channel: the Perception Filter can neither remove nor add one, so an orphan trail cannot occur.
- **Spatial gradients are presentation.** Atmosphere is per compartment; frost creeping from a leak point is Failure & Trail's interpolation of the true compartment value plus the leak anchor and its age — honest to the Graph, never a per-tile simulation.
- **Lamps dim honestly.** `served_pm` drives lamp brightness under brownout; a lamp never renders brighter than the power it is served (P1).
- **Audio is true-state.** Hiss pitch/rate, hum, ticking and the loop whisper are read from true values and belong to the consequence channel: a hallucination may neither remove a real hiss nor add a hiss, creak or whistle in a witness acoustic class (phantom-entity audio is the Filter's own, in its own classes); `alarm_eligible` (severity ≥ 3) is the only state in which an alarm-class sound or the Alarm Red sweep may play — never as the first warning.
- **The first fault the player ever sees** is the seeded coolant frost trail running from the cryo bay leak to the far wall (art bible carried element).

> 📌 **Asset Spec** — no sprites are owned here; the descriptor→trail mapping table is the input to `/asset-spec system:failure-and-trail-system` once that GDD exists.

## Game Feel

The Graph's feel is **slow burn, then cascade, then silence** — and the player should be able to say afterwards exactly why.

- **A body that decays on its own clock.** Left alone, the home-ship wears: its authored faults run on minute-scale clocks (first tell to lethal ≈ 6 min for an O₂ pinhole in a room, ≈ 9 for the ship; 4.5 for a power overload branch; 9 for coolant; 12 for hull fatigue), and the machines that keep it alive wear a little every hour they run (`W_DUTY`), so a fully repaired ship is quiet, never safe. Nothing resolves itself.
- **Long tell, short collapse.** A long tell, a spreading wrongness, a short collapse, then silence. The real tells run in minutes (D.15); the sim's own floor beneath them is `W_MIN_WARNING_S` = 10 s of two-channel physical warning before any loss the ship causes by itself, so "death is a bitch" is always nameable, never arbitrary. Horrors and the web have their own tells (Attention, Horror Entities) that must precede anything they do to the ship.
- **Every fix moves a cost.** Power for air (the scrubber draws, the web watches); air for heat; a sealed door for a stranded room; a weld for a leaky seam; a salvaged battery for a dark ship. Triage of five coupled loops, not a repair list.
- **Derelicts are already dead.** They don't tick until the player brings power; then faults that wake run twice as fast — but still warn first; a breach is already past its tell by the time the hull gives.
- **Rooms never quite settle.** A faint draught at a nearly-shut hatch is real and stays; two rooms do not balance to the last breath. Expected, not a bug.
- **Silence is a tell.** When a leak stops hissing, it is because there is nothing left to leak.

*Anti-targets*: self-healing faults, a rate dial that speeds everything, scripted "if A then B" cascades, dice-roll deaths, a status screen.

## UI Requirements

The Graph has no screens. It defines what three diegetic surfaces must be able to show, and forbids a fourth:

1. **In-World Instruments & Inspect View** — gauges read `read_view` at render rate for smooth needles (dirty keys only wake discrete presentation); a lit inspect card shows one instrument's precise reading and nothing else; gauges are per node, so two instruments on different nodes are the player's "second opinion".
2. **Failure & Trail** — trail spawn/despawn and band changes from `F/`, `C/`, `B/` keys; `T`/`SEC/` for structural rebuilds.
3. **HUD & Suit Layer** — the suit O₂ numeral on the critical strip is the suit's reading of compartment atmosphere via Suit, not a Graph overlay.
4. **Forbidden** — any schematic, overlay, minimap, ship-status or loop-summary screen that shows Graph state at a distance. The Debug Overlay (true values) is a development tool and is never shipped as a player surface.
