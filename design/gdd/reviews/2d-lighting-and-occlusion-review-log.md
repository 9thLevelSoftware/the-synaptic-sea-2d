Findings:
- [BLOCKING] LO-17 (L238) vs LO-23 (L268), S.1 DEMOTED row (L345), Edge Cases (L867), D.19, AC-LO-38 (L1272) / AC-LO-40: [main review][qa-lead][creative-director, verified] LO-17 lists 'soft pools at r_lit' among truth rules 'never reduced at any tier'. LO-23, the S.1 DEMOTED row and AC-LO-38 then draw a demoted light as an unshaded blob of about r_lit/2, while the simulation keeps reporting lit out to r_lit. AC-LO-38 makes the r_lit/2 stand-in a pass condition, so the test suite would ship the Pillar 1 lie with every AC green. Lanterns are the first lights demoted and are the 'ship drawn by what you keep alive' image, and it happens at zoom-out, which is exactly where the Player Fantasy asks the player to step back and count them. This breaks the GDD's primary pillar, and the doc contradicts itself. I verified it in the text.
- [BLOCKING] Formulas D.3 Output Range (L513) vs registry lit hysteresis (LIT_OFF_PM 120, REG:3460, L1115); AC-LO-8, AC-LO-9: [systems-designer][creative-director, verified] D.3 claims 'r_draw >= r_lit always, so a prompt can never appear outside the visible pool'. That holds only for subjects approaching the light. Emitter State's lit is a latch (on at 150 permille, off at 120), so a subject that is already lit keeps its prompt out to r_off = floor(R*(output_pm-120)/output_pm). With R 144, the gap is +4 px at output 1000, +14 px at 300 and +27 px at 160. At output 140 the pool is 8 px wide but the prompt persists to 20 px. The gap grows as a lamp is starved, which is the ship-failing state the game is about. LIT_OFF_PM appears in this GDD only as a spike item and never in a formula. This is the same honesty contract as the first blocker, and fixing only the stand-in leaves it broken.
- [BLOCKING] D.2 / LO-6 / LO-15 (L486-496; layout 'apex at left-middle, reach spans the full side'); registry light_shape L147: [systems-designer][creative-director, verified] The default square texture layout cannot hold the fan. The registry fan half-angle is 683/4096 turn = 60 degrees at reach 168. That needs a lateral half-width of 145 px, but the reach-sized square gives only 84 px, so the fan's flanks are clipped by about 30 % of reach. The result is a cone the simulation lights but the screen draws dark. The wide beam (30 degrees, 288 px) has exactly zero margin, so its edge band is clipped at the corners. AC-LO-6 and AC-LO-7 measure only the aim axis, so the lint passes. Spike step 2 lists the tight box only as an alternative, and the stated default is broken by construction.
- [BLOCKING] LO-33 / Cross-References E PD-1 / Open Question 21 / AC-LO-68..70: [main review][godot-specialist][qa-lead][performance-analyst] Containment for non-shadow lights is undecided, and the decision is architectural. Any fixture, lantern, fault glow or glowstick can draw a pool through a bulkhead into a sim-dark compartment. The preferred mechanism, C1, depends on an unverified 20-bit any-shared-bit rule and on Layout Ask E-J, which Layout has no row for. godot-specialist [BIN] found that it also cannot be built on the assumed one-TileMapLayer-per-section model, because light_mask is per CanvasItem and there is no per-tile mask. C2 needs cross-canvas lighting and splits the Y-sorted layer 0. AC-LO-69 hard-codes C1 and passes vacuously at CONTAIN_COLOURS 1. systems-designer adds that tall props with OCCLUDES_LIGHT (and NODE_LANTERNs at machinery nodes) leak inside a compartment under every candidate. This is a decision that targeted edits can settle; it does not need a design rethink.
- [BLOCKING] LO-22 (L266) / D.18 / Ask E-E vs Emitter State EM AC-90 and Player Controller row 18; AC-LO-26/27: [qa-lead, raised from main RECOMMENDED][main review][creative-director] The locked photosensitivity limit (FLASH_MAX_PER_S 3) depends on a cooldown that no document owns. LO-22 forbids the governor from retiming committed BEAM, BEACON and FAULT_GLOW changes or any lit-bearing change. Emitter State AC-90 says the simulation adds no debounce. The Controller's 10-tick guard covers only local beam toggles: fixture set_switch and remote beams have no guard at all. The 10-tick derivation has zero margin. It is applied to commands, not commits, while EM-12 adds 0-5 ticks of ON-only latency. D.18 never states whether transitions pair as non-overlapping or sliding, and under sliding pairing a perfect 10-tick toggle already exceeds 3/s. The documents contradict each other on a safety and accessibility requirement, so it cannot stay RECOMMENDED.
- [RECOMMENDED] Dependencies engine table / LO-2, 10, 12, 13, 14, 15, 24 / D.9: [main review][godot-specialist][performance-analyst][qa-lead] The ±1 px headline promise and the bloom design rest on [UNV] engine behaviour. Several preconditions are also unnamed or unowned. Light texture filtering may inherit the Pipeline's NEAREST default, which would defeat the edge band. use_hdr_2d and the world-viewport glow Environment have no owner. R-1 for the hull floor is probably unworkable under carrier B. DirectionalLight2D was never tested as an ambient carrier. Spike ACs can never fail, because the placeholder-revision rule rewrites the number instead.
- [RECOMMENDED] D.12 / LO-17 tier keying: [systems-designer][main review][creative-director] Tier caps key on window pixel count, so the same 640x360 field gives 6 lanterns at 1080p and 4 at 1440p or 2160p. Co-op clients on different monitors therefore see different 'ship as lanterns' images and different Attention tell channels, and the count also depends on pass A versus pass B. Creatively, the core image must not depend on monitor resolution.
- [RECOMMENDED] D.1 / D.8 / LO-14 / Tuning Knobs: [main review][systems-designer][godot-specialist][performance-analyst] The bloom-threshold interval is fragile. D.8 ignores KIND_GAIN; with a FIXTURE gain of 1.4 the interval is empty at n=2. VS-B with 4 beams on one mark needs E_1000 <= 0.55. The D.1 range is misstated: 0.30-2.10, not 0-1.4. n_overlap is undefined, and LIGHTS_PER_ITEM_MAX 12 is being used as the bloom n, the fill bound and the engine headroom all at once.
- [RECOMMENDED] D.15 / D.18 / LO-18-20 / D.13 Example 2 / AC-LO-24: [main review][systems-designer][qa-lead] (1) The layer that carries the Attention gutter is ambiguous (M1 vs M2). D.13 Example 2 and AC-LO-24 pin a state that is unreachable in the MVP. (2) At tier 3 with about 10 lamps in view the governor engages often (P about 0.33 per second at frame mean) and retimes the gutter spacing, which IS the Attention tell. The counting rule (10 % relative to the start energy, ignoring dilution) is stricter than any standard requires. (3) The micro-flicker 'never counts' claim fails inside a dip. Creatively, the tell is a core horror channel and must not be distorted by the safety proxy.
- [RECOMMENDED] LO-3 / AC-LO-4 / LO-29 / AC-LO-47: [main review][qa-lead] The determinism claim overreaches: present_time and render rate retime envelopes and the governor. AC-LO-47 ('picture equals pre-save picture') contradicts LO-29, which discards epochs and the governor history on restore. Restore is the MVP death path, so this case will be hit constantly.
- [RECOMMENDED] D.6 / D.7 / LO-13 / AC-LO-20, 22 / Open Question 2: [systems-designer][main review] D.6 slew is non-integer, so floor division makes it take 40 frames and it freezes above 80. mean_fixture_pm is 0/0 for a section with no fixtures. In D.7 the worked ring-1 value of 4.18 codes violates the 1-2 code contract, rings 2 and 3 differ by about 0.1 code, enc() is undefined and the units are mixed. Reading S versus reading U (Void Steel) is unresolved and differs by about 7x.
- [RECOMMENDED] D.9 / D.2 / EDGE_TEXELS: [systems-designer][godot-specialist] The pass-A budget double-counts the ±1 px (D.2 lint tolerance plus 0.875 sampling error). EDGE_TEXELS 3-4 fail the contract by construction. Pass-A minification of 2.67x on a 2-texel band is the dominant error term.
- [RECOMMENDED] AC-LO-53 / AC-LO-54 / LIGHT_CPU_BUDGET_US / LO-9 / LO-11 / LO-29: [performance-analyst] No Lighting GPU ms sub-budget or min-spec GPU exists, and the Pipeline GDD excludes Lighting's cost. Findings:
- [BLOCKING] LO-33 (L331), Edge Cases L858/L899 vs L909, D.19, AC-LO-69 C0 set, AC-LO-73: [main-review, confirmed by creative-director spot-check] The stand-in lie (prior blocker 1) holds only in empty rooms. L909 promises the stand-in disc ends at r_draw, so counting lanterns at k_z 1 and reading the prompt up close 'agree to 1 base px'. L899 (and L331, L858) clamps the radius isotropically to the nearest OCCLUDES_LIGHT edge, with a 4 px floor. Machinery props and hull count as such edges, so in almost every authored room the drawn edge is smaller than the prompt edge, and AC-LO-69 enforces that shrink. This is a Pillar 1 problem in the picture: a light the player can count from far away has a smaller pool than the area in which its prompt appears.
- [BLOCKING] LO-22, D.18, Ask E-E, Cross-References C rows 25/30, Interactions L465/L474, AC-LO-27, OQ 13: [main-review, confirmed by creative-director spot-check] The photosensitivity guard Lighting relies on does not exist in the owner's GDD. Emitter State ships a beam-only TOO_SOON guard: BEAM_TOGGLE_MIN_INTERVAL_TICKS 10, measured on the apply tick, zero beam latency. COOLDOWN appears in EM only once, at L161, as first-draft rationale that EM retracts ('was wrong'). SWITCH_MIN_SPACING_TICKS does not appear in EM at all. EM OQ 25 leaves SYSTEM set_switch and set_beacon unguarded. Lighting's flash rule uses ceil(N/2) while the registry and EM use floor(n/2). AC-LO-27 models a 0-5 tick latency that beams do not have, and it is written as a test that fails by design. This is a safety and accessibility requirement with two documents in conflict.
- [BLOCKING] LO-7, AC-LO-8 vs EM AC-56 (EM L1180); Interactions L429; Edge Cases L879; C row 29; OQ 25: [main-review, confirmed by creative-director spot-check] Two BLOCKING acceptance criteria cannot both pass on one build. EM AC-56 (re-amended 2026-10-27) requires a drawn soft pool at r_lit +/- 1 px. Lighting draws to r_off: 126 against 122 at full output, a 4 px gap. Lighting's L429 still quotes EM's 'draws soft pools at r_lit' and marks it 'Agrees', which is false. L879 still says the clamp is at r_lit.
- [RECOMMENDED] LO-33 C3 residual bound (i), PD1_EXCEPTION_MAX_PX, R_DRAW_MAX_PX: [main-review] With R_DRAW_MAX_PX 126, spine_corridor has s = 30, over the 24 px exception limit, so the C0 set of AC-LO-69 fails on the home ship. R_DRAW_MAX_PX is written as a constant, but it is derived from LIT_OFF_PM, whose allowed range would give up to 135. DA still cites 122.
- [RECOMMENDED] LO-9 lent slots, D.4, S.2: [main-review] Nothing bounds how often a lent slot moves. When the seat runs at V_RUN past lamps 100-150 px apart, the lent slots move about once a second, so the lights nearest the player are often partly unshadowed.
- [RECOMMENDED] LO-20, D.15, AC-LO-77: [main-review] The onset stagger runs at about 82 % utilisation in Tier 3, and the doc does not say what happens when an onset cannot be placed within 60 frames. AC-LO-77 may be unsatisfiable as written.
- [RECOMMENDED] LO-14, D.8, N_OVERLAP_MAX: [main-review] The bloom bound is only argued for two overlapping lights. Nothing lints three or more lights over a trail stamp. Bloom on trail marks would corrupt diegetic evidence (Pillar 1).
- [RECOMMENDED] LO-3, LO-23, D.19, Tuning Knobs: [main-review] The non-shadow node pool is not sized for the over-cap case: up to 34 lights at T3 against a cap of 24.
- [RECOMMENDED] LO-24, S.6 REBUILD: [main-review] The switch from mask bit 1 to partition bits when a second body goes live is unspecified. During the switch, light can cross between bodies, or a frame can go black.
- [RECOMMENDED] Dependencies/Interactions (Graph, Controller, Suit): [main-review] life-support-graph.md does not mention Lighting, which breaks the bidirectionality rule. The Controller dependency status is undecided. The Suit status differs between the two tables.
- [RECOMMENDED] OQ 28, AC-LO-53: [main-review] There is no GPU budget or min-spec GPU, so the central performance criterion is not gated.
- [RECOMMENDED] LO-22, D.14, Edge Cases L920: [main-review] It is ambiguous whether the photosensitivity governor may retime the warm-up envelope.
- [RECOMMENDED] AC-LO-75: [main-review] The Deck's 640x400 field means the stand-in set cannot be identical across the four targets.
- [RECOMMENDED] Acceptance Criteria (untestable list): [main-review] Fourteen ACs are untestable as written: AC-LO-8, 14, 23, 27, 30, 45, 50, 52/57, 53, 58, 59, 64, 65, 71 and 77 (52 and 57 counted as one). They rely on wording like 'reads', 'no shimmer', 'art-director present' and 'not gated'.
