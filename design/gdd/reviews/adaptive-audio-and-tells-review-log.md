Findings:
- [BLOCKING] Formulas D.2 / AA-20(d), AA-27 / E-AA-11, E-AA-15 / AC-AA-22a, AC-AA-26: [main review, confirmed by systems-designer, qa-lead and the creative-director spot-check at L598, L613, L975, L979, L1731] D.2 says emit(T) = hit(T) AND (T - last_alarm_T >= ALARM_MIN_GAP_TICKS), with last_alarm_T shared by attn and ann. Under that formula a second pulse on the same tick has gap 0 and is suppressed, which is what E-AA-11 says. The D.2 Output Range, E-AA-15 and AC-AA-22a/26 say two pulses on one tick are 'one rhythm with two stacked timbres' and both are requested. A test written from the formula fails the AC. The rule that the entry event is exempt from the gap is implied and never stated. [qa-lead] adds that an ann pulse up to 63 ticks before breach entry can stack with pulse 0, and the ann phase jumps at entry and at exit. [systems-designer] adds that the union-duty bound W/S depends on a pulse-length lint that AA-47 does not contain, and pulse 0 is explicitly longer than W.
- [BLOCKING] Detailed Design AA-4 vs AA-13 / D.1, D.11 / AC-AA-5, AC-AA-44: [main review RECOMMENDED; game-designer argues BLOCKING; creative-director agrees with BLOCKING after the spot-check at L203 and L283] The document describes two delivery pipelines as one. AA-4 says one poll per render frame classifies the net change, with no catch-up. AA-13 and AC-AA-5/44 say the request is created in the handler of the P5 notification. If the handler runs once per sim step in a catch-up frame, beats play as a burst, which breaks AA-4 and AUD-3. If the handler only records state, AC-AA-5 cannot pass as written. This is the first code built for the P2 tell, so it has to be settled before implementation. It is not a tuning matter.
- [RECOMMENDED] Player Fantasy 'We will know this was right if' (L141) / Feel Acceptance Criteria (L1498) vs AA-22, AA-28(a): [game-designer; confirmed by the creative-director at L169 and L1498] The negative success gate fails by construction. AA-22 deliberately ships a four-step creak ladder keyed to ambient_cue_step, and Pillar 3 says attention is shown by creaking. AA-28(a) keeps a positional creep with falloff, which by definition tells the player how far away the monster is. 'No tester could tell how much attention / how close a monster' therefore contradicts the pillars. The main review's questionnaire rewrite would also fail by design. I rank this as the top vision-level fix: the gate should test what the vision forbids, which is a continuous, numeric or non-diegetic readout.
- [RECOMMENDED] Player Fantasy (L43, L63) vs D.7/D.8/D.9 hearing range; AA-9 pan: [game-designer] The fantasy promises hearing the machinery 'two rooms away'. The linear 3 dB per tile falloff and R_AUDIBLE_MAX_PX 288 px deliver about one screen, and a running loop is culled about 3 tiles behind a closed door. Panning is horizontal only, so the y position of a leak is barely conveyed. This is the core of 'earned certainty in the dark' and needs a user decision, not just tuning.
- [RECOMMENDED] AA-25 / D.6 / D.7 / D.8 / D.15 (audibility versus level; masking): [main review, systems-designer, game-designer, godot-specialist] The anti-suppression guarantee holds on paper but not in the ear. The witness floor of -54 dBr sits 10 to 16 dB under the room tone (-38) and the ambience (-44). Run footfalls at -3 dBr and hal leaks at up to -18 dBr are in neither the D.6 concurrent set nor the D.8 relation. A stalling machine is the quietest thing in the room while the lie that the machine is meant to disprove is 10 dB or more louder, which breaks P4 at its own anchor. Beats and onset have no level margin, and the tell has no test with a moving listener. There is no peak or headroom budget against 0 dBFS while L-8 bans limiters.
- [RECOMMENDED] D.7 / D.8 / D.10 / E-AA-26 / Tuning Knobs F (count caps): [main review, systems-designer, godot-specialist, game-designer] RUN_MAX_VOICES and WIT_VOICE_CAP silence true-state sounds for reasons of count, not distance, which contradicts AA-25 and AA-12. The running-loop cap ranks by pixel distance before attenuation, has no hysteresis, and hard-cuts a loop on FAILED. DRIP, TICK and CREAK each consume several transient voices that D123 does not count.
- [RECOMMENDED] D.2 ALIGNED at breach entry / S3 / AC-AA-20, AC-AA-95: [main review, systems-designer, game-designer] The beacon is commanded at T+1, so on the home ship the mode at entry is always FALLBACK. The ALIGNED worked examples (spot-checked at L615) describe a case the rules never produce, and the player can hear up to 2.1 s of silence after the entry event. The mode can also flap when the beacon's output toggles.
- [RECOMMENDED] Formulas D.5, D.8, D.9, D.11, D.17, D.18 (numerical defects): [systems-designer] Several formulas misbehave at their edges. D.9 open_pm has no clamp (a large hole gives negative attenuation), and a hull with a hole in it still costs 15 dB. D.5 includes the bed in the post-entry window although the bed hard-stops, which can also click. The D.18 duck pumps at default values, contrary to the text. D.17 at n = 1 never plays. The rate classes in D.8 have no recurrence formula consistent with AUD-1. The R_AUDIBLE_MAX_PX relation can go negative. D.11 leaves a gap of up to 67 ms between the lead-in and the strike. Ranges hard-code registry placeholders.
- [RECOMMENDED] AA-5 pause / AA-6 buses / AA-9 listener / AA-44 formats (engine mechanisms): [godot-specialist, qa-lead] Pause, envelopes, ducking and pan are asserted without a Godot mechanism. A Tween would violate AC-AA-73. A duck applied at bus level would also duck tier-1 footfalls that share the Foley bus. The built-in AudioStreamPlayer2D attenuation cannot express D.9, so AC-AA-81 fails by construction. Vorbis decoding for every one-shot, at an unpinned mix rate, is the wrong default.
- [RECOMMENDED] AA-27 / E-AA-16, E-AA-17 (annunciator fatigue): [game-designer] For as long as a sev-3 descriptor is eligible, the ann klaxon sounds every 2.1 s, at tier 1, never ducked, with no listener scope. This is the Barotrauma pattern the GDD names as an anti-reference, and it breaks the 'workmanlike honesty, never drama' register. It also trains players to ignore the one alarm that reduce-motion players depend on.
- [RECOMMENDED] Player Fantasy anchor (L63) / AA-18, AA-19 (P2 vs P3 cross-pillar): [game-designer] Escalation follows tell_elapsed, not player behaviour. The best realistic abort comes after about 10 s, so the player who goes still hears tier 2 and the hull-stress layer before the release arrives: 'worse, worse, worse, release'. This tension sits between Audio and Attention, and Audio cannot resolve it alone.
- [RECOMMENDED] Header, Overview, Dependencies, Cross-References X-4/X-6, AA-36 legibility_range_px: [main review] Neighbour status and the refresh register are out of date. Chart Table, HUD and Instruments are now Designed. Graph opening(edge_id) and the Layout hull_anchor are published. RF-1/2/3 have been applied. 2D Lighting is missing from Dependencies, and Pixel-Perfect and Save/Load never name Audio, which breaks the bidirectional rule. legibility_range_px(ref) has no owner, and AC-AA-129 (clamp) contradicts E-AA-60 (refuse).
- [RECOMMENDED] Acceptance Criteria (testability, infrastructure, panel): [main review, qa-lead, godot-specialist] The criteria assume infrastructure that does not exist yet: a request-capture harness, a lint runner, a draw-order rig, a defined reference machine and gdUnit4. AC-AA-84's 'writes disabled' cannot be implemented in GDScript. Compound ACs bundle 8 to 15 assertions each. Random fixtures are not committed. The grid tests have no runtime budget. Blind tests have no trial design. AC-AA-8 cannot be proved on laptop speakers. AC-AA-12 contradicts the Feel bullet at L1493. Nobody is named to sign off audio Visual/Feel evidence (OQ-AA-14). The untestable ACs listed by the main review need rewriting, with the corrections in the disagreements list applied.
- [RECOMMENDED] Scope / AA-7 / effort index: [main review; qa-lead corrected the counts] The document has 47 rules, 18 formulas, about 130 ACs (about 11 needing a listening panel, not 30), 13 asset families and 5 spikes, which is XL rather than M for a solo developer. P2 Suit cues may fall back to placeholders, yet AC-AA-101/102/105 demand 90 per cent blind identification on them.
- [RECOMMENDED] VOICE_POOL_SIZE (D.10 / OQ-AA-9): [main review, corrected by qa-lead and godot-specialist] At the default of 32 the DIAGNOSTIC does not fire (32 >= D123 = 31), contrary to the main review. The real defect is that one voice is left for all tier-4 content, and D123 adds caps that cannot all be reached at once.
