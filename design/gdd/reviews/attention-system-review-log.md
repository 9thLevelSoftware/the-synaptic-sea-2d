- [BLOCKING] Edge Cases E-23 / Detailed Rules AT-22, AT-12(iv) / Player Fantasy (:51), Moments B and C / OQ-AT-12: [creative-director, raised from main review and game-designer RECOMMENDED] E-23 says a pod hop during a TELL never aborts the tell, and the locus falls back to the home ship. The due-run breach then STRESSes the ship the player just escaped to. This contradicts the Player Fantasy at :51 ('Leave, and the web lets go'). It also punishes the game's natural escape verb in a lethal-breach game (Horror HE-F16), which teaches players not to flee during a tell. That runs against P3's 'a price chosen with full warning'. This is not a wording problem: implementing E-23 as written would build anti-pillar behaviour into the state machine. Under Web Map WM-24 and WM-10, X10 and AC-AT-46 clause 2 cannot be reached at MVP either.
- [RECOMMENDED] AT-11 / AC-AT-117 / AC-AT-58 / OQ-AT-24: [main review rated BLOCKING; systems-designer and game-designer rate RECOMMENDED; creative-director agrees with the downgrade] The guarantee that a breach has teeth on derelict A depends on a fault-content ruling. It does not block building attention.step. game-designer notes that Horror is now designed and supplies teeth: HE-F16 says a stationary seat dies in a breach of 986 ticks or more. AT-11's line 'Horror alone is not enough' was written before Horror existed.
- [RECOMMENDED] Player Fantasy (:47, :51) / Overview contract / Principle 1 / F-25: [game-designer; main review #6; audio-director] The restraint verbs are presented as peers but they do not weigh the same. Stillness and stopping the tool are the lever. Killing the lamp saves about 100 ticks, and opening the breaker costs a 270 AU inrush. The contract wording 'could have aborted by going dark' names the wrong lever. '[Every lamp] is something it can see' is true only as a small shortening of the time to cross the line.
- [RECOMMENDED] Player Fantasy Moment C / F-6 / E-40 / AT-5: [game-designer; systems-designer; main review #4] Moment C is false at MVP on three separate grounds. Dormancy means derelict A contributes nothing once the player leaves. Lights alone settle at 1,679 AU against a theta of 6,000. Noise dominates the locus ledger, so lights left on cannot move the locus (CR-LURE is inert).
- [RECOMMENDED] F-15 / F-15b / F-16 / AT-6 / AC-AT-64: [game-designer; systems-designer; main review #7] The abortability lint assumes the home fixtures are dark. With the home ship lit and the player away, the at-due path keeps only about 25 to 50 ticks of slack at ring 1 (with the wake glows), and fails at rings 2 and 3. Positive power transients from the Graph that the player did not cause (auto-reclose after a SCRAM, duty cycling, STRESS-driven faults) are not bounded, and one can use up ATT_MARGIN.
- [RECOMMENDED] AT-9 / AT-10 / AUD-6 / Game Feel: [game-designer; audio-director] Escalation is keyed to elapsed time, so a player who complies hears the tell get worse until the binary release. This teaches panic rather than restraint (a Self-Determination Theory competence failure), and it can produce commit-dominance through player psychology rather than through the mechanics.
- [RECOMMENDED] F-15b / Moment A / E-26 / AC-AT-105: [systems-designer] The 'favourable case', where the player resumes before the partial TTL expires, needs a reaction within 6 ticks (100 ms), so no human can reach it. In practice an abort always forfeits the partial.
- [RECOMMENDED] Visual/Audio V9 vs Adaptive Audio AA-20 / AA-16; Accessibility vs Audio M-4: [audio-director, confirmed by creative-director grep] attention-system.md:1127 says the tell bed continues during BREACH, while adaptive-audio-and-tells.md:318 says it ends at BREACH. - [RECOMMENDED] Overview contract / AT-15 / AUD-9 / OQ-AT-1: [main review; audio-director] Deaf and muted players lose their only channel when they go dark. The two-senses contract cannot be made true by rewording it.
- [RECOMMENDED] AC-AT-80 / Edge Cases (throttle play): [game-designer] No target for breaches per session is defined. Horror's HE-F16 assumes 3 to 8. Neither degenerate pole has a gate: throttle play produces near-zero breaches, and riding every tell produces up to about 13.

- [RECOMMENDED] ATT_THRESHOLD knob / F-25 / AC-AT-57 / OQ-AT-22: [systems-designer; main review #8] A theta above about 8,000 makes sustained verbs unable to cause a tell. Every number is still a placeholder, and calibration waits on a harness that does not exist. U_LOUD 600 is outside its own documented range, and the 2.7 percent figure is wrong (it should be 3.0).

'Audio first' is checked only as dispatch order, which ignores output latency. The 6 dB masking margin has no measurement method, and it is a BLOCKING Visual/Feel gate.

Findings:
- [BLOCKING] Formulas F-6 / F-2 vs E-40 and Player Fantasy Moment C: [main-review, escalated by creative-director] The ledger contradicts itself about light and power, and the locus rule depends on it. Spot-check: line 510 defines `I_s` as 'the part of `I_run` attributed to section `s` (F-1 to F-4)', so light and power count. Lines 77 and 948 say 'No light term is added to the ledger'. The two readings pick different locus sections in ordinary play: a quiet player in a derelict while the home ship is lit. The locus is where the horror lands, so it carries Pillar weight. An implementer cannot build AT-2 or F-6 without guessing. Only an edit is needed, but it has to happen before a story is written.
- [BLOCKING] AT-22 / E-23 / OQ-AT-12 vs web-map-and-transit.md WM-26 / WM-26b / ASK-A4: [main-review, escalated by creative-director] Two GDDs give opposite rules for a hop during a TELL.
