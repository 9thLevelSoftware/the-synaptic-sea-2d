Findings:
- [BLOCKING] Formulas D.6 / EM-20 (a) / registry rect_algebra_closed_span / Layout LY-12 (b): [systems-designer] Light leaks through wall seams, and the creative-director confirmed it. D.6 L468 tests segments against the CLOSED span [x0, x1-1] x [y0, y1-1] of half-open rects on integer coordinates. Two abutting rects ([0,24) and [24,48)) therefore leave an open strip between x 23 and 24. An oblique ray such as S(23,80) to P(24,140) crosses the wall at x 23.27..23.65 and touches neither box. Every worked example (L470) and AC-22 uses axis-aligned rays, which hides the defect. This breaks EM-20 (a), 'light does not leak around a corner', and P1: a prompt is lit behind a wall while Lighting draws a seamless shadow. The defect is at pillar level, not in the arithmetic.
- [BLOCKING] Detailed Design EM-17 / EM-4 / Formulas D.11: [main] FAULT_GLOW records have no defined anchor_px, section_id or body_id. The creative-director confirmed this: EM-17 (L202) reads mode, medium and sev from the descriptor but no position. The Graph has no pixel geometry. Without a position, the cone/radius test, the occlusion start S, the section_emission attribution and sim/render agreement cannot be implemented without guessing. [systems-designer] adds that emitter anchors (fixture, beacon, fault glow) are not covered by the ANCHOR_CLEARANCE_PX lint. An anchor on its own wall footprint lights nothing in its room and raises no diagnostic.
- [BLOCKING] Detailed Design EM-1 / AC-73: [main] The block registration omits supported_versions and added_in_format. The creative-director confirmed 0 grep hits for supported_versions. Save/Load SL-15 fails boot with SL_BOOT_NO_SUPPORTED_VERSIONS when the field is absent. AC-73 pins the descriptor exactly, so it would pass a block that cannot boot. Because MVP death means restart from the last autosave, restore is the main recovery path.
- [BLOCKING] Formulas D.3 / AC-34c / Edge Cases (behaviour-changing staleness only): [main, narrowed by godot-specialist] The 'weaker form' lantern fallback is still mandated even though the Graph now publishes run_pm (LS-19). Under it, a browned-out or starved OK machine glows at full base_pm. That is wrong lantern behaviour, and it lies about power state, which violates P1. [systems-designer] adds that run_pm is not clamped on the Emitter side, so output_pm can exceed base_pm if out_w > out_w_rated, or divide by zero at a rated value of 0. The wider status-label staleness is tracked under recommended.
- [BLOCKING] EM-24 / Formulas D.14 / EM-28 / AC-30, AC-54: [systems-designer][godot-specialist] probes_allowed is derived from T_mand_prev (L618). T_mand_prev is counted work that depends on cache hits and skips, is not canonical (absent from EM-28) and is rebuilt differently after post_restore. The same probe can therefore answer OK in a live run and UNKNOWN after a restore, which breaks kernel G4. AC-54 cannot detect this. [main] adds that no MVP consumer calls probe or lit_by (Horror HE-13/19, Attention AT-4), and that neither takes a body_id.
- [BLOCKING] Formulas D.13 / D.14 / EM-23 (W3-W4): [performance-analyst][godot-specialist] The static-layer rebuild is not priced, and it collides with the D.13 'table age <= 1 tick' contract. Rebuilds are triggered by door-leaf version bumps (continuous during door travel, roughly 10 per second, at section granularity), lamp output drift, inrush, blackout, glowstick wane steps every 50 ticks, and a cold restore. With the document's own unit costs, one room is about 12k units and a ship-wide blackout is about 140k units, against B = 10k. Blackout is the central horror beat. The open question is whether the light contract is 'never stale' or 'bounded stale', and downstream consumers (Interaction, Attention, Horror) depend on the answer. That is a design-contract decision, not just a tuning gap.
- [BLOCKING] EM-7 / AC-90 / Open Question 19 vs player-controller-input.md L127, L1137: [godot-specialist] The two GDDs contradict each other on ownership, and the creative-director confirmed it. The Controller says the toggle_beam.validate guard 'is Emitter State's to add' (both lines confirmed). EM-7 (L159) and AC-90 forbid any sim-side cooldown. The result is that no one owns the strobe guard for replay, scripted or remote submitters. Suit SE-35's drain-sampling exploit protection and the WCAG 2.3.1 flash concern then rest on the local Adapter alone. EM-7's rationale ('wall-clock-shaped') is wrong for a tick-denominated field.
- [RECOMMENDED] EM-6 / BEAM_SPILL_RADIUS_PX (creative call): [systems-designer, endorsed by creative-director] With a chest cone and zero spill, an object at the player's feet, or one tile to the side, is dark in every shape in any unlit room, even when it is within Interaction REACH_PX 72. EM-6 calls this 'a skill expression'. In practice it is a control tax caused by the user's chest-lamp decision, and it will read as a broken prompt, not as horror. P1 honesty is preserved if the spill is drawn. The cost is a small loss of P3 darkness right at the body.
- [RECOMMENDED] Status sweep (Quick ref / Dependencies / Interactions / OQ): [main] Status markers are stale. Suit, Interaction, Layout, Body, Lighting, Attention, Horror and Authoring are still labelled undesigned or 'in flight', and closed asks (Graph post_restore, A1, OccluderSource version) are still listed as open. As a result, AC-34b, AC-41, AC-14b, AC-25c, AC-59b and AC-60 are under-gated. The registry lamp_output_pm comment is also stale.
- [RECOMMENDED] EM-4 / EM-21 / EM-27: [main] Beam and ACTOR section_id and body_id have no derivation and no update rule when the actor crosses a door. Attention attributes exposure per section and Horror targets by section, so the player's exposure (P3) will be attributed to the wrong place.
- [RECOMMENDED] EM-7 NOT_LIVE vs Interaction IA-11(a) and Attention E-30: [main] EM-7 reads node(id).state in a PLAYER-origin validate, which IA-11(a) forbids. Attention E-30 expects NOT_LIVE on a dead bus, but EM-7 accepts the command and the node goes STARVED.
- [RECOMMENDED] Formulas D.7 r_lit / D.1 example: [systems-designer][godot-specialist] The closed form for r_lit runs 1 px high in about 4% of cases (219 of 5,100). The D.1 example should give 95, not 96. Lighting draws the soft pool at r_lit, so the drawn honest edge sits 1 px outside the actionable edge. That uses up the whole AC-56 tolerance.
- [RECOMMENDED] EM-6 / AC-57 / ADR candidate 5 / Lighting LO-10: [main][godot-specialist][qa-lead] The wall-overlap band is wrong: it is 1-44 px, not 10-34. AC-57 always passes. How Godot 2D shadows handle a light inside a LightOccluder2D is [UNV]. The worst case is a beam that renders black while the sim reports it lit, which happens every time a player walks up to a wall.
- [RECOMMENDED] Formulas D.14 / AC-53 / AC-64: [main][performance-analyst][qa-lead] Unit costs are unsourced. The design point is priced at M=4, while the lint bound is M=6 (85% of B). VS-B with 4 co-op beams costs about 11,668 units before any movers, which is over B. P99 hides rare hitches. No min-spec hardware is named. AC-64 tests only arithmetic. The broadphase maintenance cost (W_BROAD) and per-call read-view allocation are not counted.
- [RECOMMENDED] EM-25 read view / storage: [performance-analyst][godot-specialist] The 'copy-free, read-only, no allocation' read view cannot be enforced in GDScript as written. Dictionary order after restore is not ascending id. section_emission is O(E) per call.
- [RECOMMENDED] D.15 boot invariants: [godot-specialist] Release exports strip GDScript assert(), so invariants written as asserts disappear in the shipped build. [qa-lead] Tests cannot catch assert or Release-template clauses.
- [RECOMMENDED] EM-17 rule 3 / I-CAPS / D.7 reach: [systems-designer] There is no runtime rule for a creation at MAX_EMITTERS. FAULT_GLOW reach has no lower bound, so R=0 divides by zero.
- [RECOMMENDED] Formulas D.12 / Attention F-3 (creative call): [systems-designer] The light stimulus is shape-blind. The narrow loadout, which reveals the least, generates 2.3x the Attention load of the fan. The loadout trade-off is a user-visible P3 choice, and an accident of the formula distorts it.
- [RECOMMENDED] Acceptance Criteria (testability, coverage, harness, save points): [main][qa-lead] About ten ACs cannot fail. AC-52, AC-36 and AC-38 are compound. Numbering is out of order. There are no test seams or fakes. Mid-ARMING, FAULT_GLOW-protection and STARVED restores are not covered. lit_static, emitters_near and subject_headroom are untested. The L273/L284 eviction contradiction is unresolved. There is no smoke or playtest protocol. OQ18 qa.level is already resolved by project.yaml.
- [RECOMMENDED] ADRs / document size: [main] Workflow tier is full, but no ADRs exist. Five ADR candidates are embedded inline as rulings, and the GDD is 285 KB.
- [RECOMMENDED] Open Questions 1-4: [main] Four provisional decisions made in autonomous mode (beacon non-gating, darkness never a sanity cost, constant reach) already underpin five GDDs.

- [BLOCKING] Edge Cases (Switching, power and lamps, SALVAGED bullet) vs EM-7 / AC-33a: [main-review, promoted by creative-director] The Edge Cases bullet still says a PLAYER set_switch on a SALVAGED, dormant or tombstoned lamp node is 'rejected NOT_LIVE'. Implementers will read this doc section by section because of its size. A command contract that says opposite things in two places is an implementation defect, not polish.
- [BLOCKING] Acceptance Criteria AC-52 rows 52-1, 52-2 vs Formulas D.15 I-CAPS (a), (d): [main-review, promoted by creative-director] I confirmed rows 52-1 ('97 emitters authored fails; 96 passes') and 52-2 ('193 subjects fails; 192 passes') at lines 1150-1151. These rows ignore the I-CAPS reserves: 26 dynamic emitters, and the dynamic subject budget. A test written from them would fail a correct implementation. ACs are the contract QA builds against, so a wrong AC blocks the story.
- [BLOCKING] Detailed Rules EM-5 (E1) / States and Transitions (ABSENT to ARMING, line 269) / Formulas D.2: [main-review, promoted by creative-director] The state table sends an authored-on FIXTURE or BEACON to ARMING at instantiate. But D.2 defines arm_tick only from a set_switch apply tick, and E1 never says it issues the M14 set_load_request. At new game, every authored-on home-ship lamp (corridor lamps, bcn_main per DA-22) therefore has no defined lit tick and no defined Graph load request. This is the first lighting the player ever sees. It also bears on Pillar-level tone, since the home ship is the safe baseline the darkness is contrasted against. It must not be left to the implementer.
- [RECOMMENDED] Formulas D.14 S1 / EM-23 narrowing: [main-review] S1 does not price the scan for previously blocked pairs when a door opens (about 840 units per room per bump). With it, S1 is about 10,600 against B 10,000 in a steady scenario. I confirmed the S1 row at line 640. [creative-director] This stays RECOMMENDED, not blocking. Every unit cost is a model value until EM-SPIKE-1 calibrates it (line 659), and the overrun is about 6 %. It must still be priced honestly before the spike, or the spike calibrates the wrong formula.
- [RECOMMENDED] D.14 S3/S6/S7; EM-5 E1/E2 callers (line 149, 338): [main-review] S3 and S6 are priced at 8 lanterns (36 static emitters), but I-CAPS allows 20 (48). S7 is labelled 'pod hop', but Web Map WM-7 and CF-EM build nothing after init_world. I confirmed that line 149 still lists E1 'in its instantiate apply' and line 646 still says 'cold apply behind the hop'.
- [RECOMMENDED] EM-25 lit_static / Interactions (Sanity, HUD rows) / AC-99: [main-review] lit_static fails the same 'no MVP consumer, defer' test that deferred probe and lit_by, because Sanity no longer requires it. The Sanity and HUD rows are still marked provisional.

EM-17 states LY-23 anchor-head semantics as fact while they are unconfirmed. [creative-director] This is the most pillar-critical open seam: 'what you see lit is what the sim calls lit' is the honesty contract of the horror.
