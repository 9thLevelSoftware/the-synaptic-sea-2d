Summary: Verdict: NEEDS REVISION. All 8 sections are present and the core design is sound. Two things work well: the trail is a pure function of the Graph descriptor, and the never-cull truth channel serves Pillar 1 well.  The verdict turns on one structural gap, and I checked it against the Graph myself. D.2 and D.5 assume since_steps counts from the moment the fault begins and stops counting when the fault is isolated. The Graph does neither: - L170 measures since_steps from warn_since_step for any subject that is not FAILED. - L170 also sets since_steps to 0 for compartment descriptors. - L146 persists only the warn and fail steps. - LS-13 (L144) requires 100 consecutive warning steps before an endogenous failure.
