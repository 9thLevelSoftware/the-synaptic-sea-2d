# Design Review Log — failure-and-trail-system

## Review — 2026-10-27 — Verdict: NEEDS REVISION
Scope signal: XL
Specialists: game-designer, systems-designer, qa-lead, gameplay-programmer, performance-analyst
Blocking items: 3 | Recommended: 18
Summary: Verdict: NEEDS REVISION. All 8 sections are present and the core design is sound. Two things work well: the trail is a pure function of the Graph descriptor, and the never-cull truth channel serves Pillar 1 well.  The verdict turns on one structural gap, and I checked it against the Graph myself. D.2 and D.5 assume since_steps counts from the moment the fault begins and stops counting when the fault is isolated. The Graph does neither: - L170 measures since_steps from warn_since_step for any subject that is not FAILED. - L170 also sets since_steps to 0 for compartment descriptors. - L146 persists only the warn and fail steps. - LS-13 (L144) requires 100 consecutive warning steps before an endogenous failure.
Prior verdict resolved: First review
Findings:
- [BLOCKING] Time axis: FT-8, FT-9, FT-17 (table L151-157), D.2 (L358, L367), D.5, States T3, Edge Cases 'since_steps is 0', Interactions row 16, Player Fantasy ET-2/ET-3; ACs 16, 17, 19, 24a, 36, 38, 78, 133: HUB BLOCKER. Four findings report one defect. D.2 and D.5 assume since_steps counts from fault onset and stops counting when the fault is isolated. The Graph provides neither. I confirmed this in the Graph: L170 says since_steps = step_no - fail_since_step for a FAILED node or edge and step_no - warn_since_step otherwise. L146 says only fail_since_step and warn_since_step are persisted. L144 (LS-13) requires sev >= 1 for at least 100 consecutive steps before an endogenous failure. The four findings are: (a) [main review] a LEAK (DEGRADED, not FAILED) counts from the WORN onset, so it appears fully grown. (b) [game-designer] This is universal, not limited to LEAK. Because of the fairness gate, every endogenously failed subject arrives with since_steps >= 100, which is 600 px at sev 3 and 1500 px at sev 4, at or above TRAIL_EDGE_MAX_PX. So 'grows while you watch' (ET-2) and 'walk and it beats you' (ET-3) are unreachable for nearly every endogenous fault, and AC-17, AC-19 and AC-78 test states the gate cannot produce. (c) [gameplay-programmer] D.2 is monotone in since_steps with no intensity term. A STILL trail therefore keeps growing to the full edge, which contradicts the FT-17 table row 'the frost stops growing' (L155), T3 and the Edge Cases. AC-36 does not check extent. (d) [gameplay-programmer] D.5 has no LIVE term, so a pipe that wears for 30 minutes and then leaks is born at dull_step 3 with fuel_dried true. A live leak then shows the dried verdict, which breaks FT-17's own guarantee (L149). [creative-director] I agree with all four. This is the core of the verdict. The Player Fantasy's growth and verdict promises cannot be kept statelessly from the fields the Graph emits today.
- [BLOCKING] D.2b surface_patch_count (L384-397) / D.3 / Table of record rows 20-21 / AC-27, AC-101: [main review] COMPARTMENT and OPENING descriptors carry since_steps 0 at MVP. I confirmed this at Graph L170. D.2b therefore gives R_px = 0, so room frost and soot never spread, and AC-27 and the 21-patch cryo-bay example are unreachable. [game-designer, systems-designer, qa-lead] The main review's fix R_px = reach_px has an unstated side effect. Row 20 draws nothing at sev 1 (COOL), and at the 0 mC FROST edge D.3 already gives 480 px. Up to 24 patches would therefore pop across the room in a single step, which contradicts the 'room is slowly getting cold' dread. [systems-designer, gameplay-programmer] D.2b also uses floor(count / STRIDE) while the placement predicate gives ceil, and dist() has no stated metric, so ordering is not reproducible across clients. AC-27's '21' is correct only for even tile counts.
- [BLOCKING] FT-6, FT-7 (L127), FT-14, FT-30, FT-38 (L213), V/A.4 (L932), V/A.10 (L1020), AC-11, AC-95 (L1230), Open Question 5: [main review] The MVP seam weep is the ET-2 anchor moment and D.9 budgets it, yet it has no asset. FT-7, FT-38, V/A.4, V/A.10 and AC-95 all defer s-mist 'until the Graph states the seam-weep severity'. The Graph now states it at L170, which I confirmed: SEAM, mode 1 LEAK, GAS, sev 1 while A_seam > 0, intensity_pm = min(1000, 1000 x A_seam / A_HOLE_MAX). The sev-0 seam exception, OQ5 ('blocking') and the PROVISIONAL tags on rows 3, 4, 5, 9, 24 and 25 are stale. [game-designer] At A_seam 1 the weep is about 15 permille, which gives cover16 4 of 16 on a 16 px puff, lit-only. The first weld the player inspects may read as 'nothing is wrong'.
- [RECOMMENDED] Player Fantasy / FT-11 / D.3 / row 1 COOLANT: [main review, game-designer] A coolant leak in a COMFORT room, where the home ship spends most of its time, draws a 48 px stamp and a drip with no followable gradient. 'Follow the mark to the wound' therefore holds only in FROST rooms. [creative-director] This is the largest gap in the fantasy. It does not block the GDD, but it must be decided before /asset-spec.
- [RECOMMENDED] Open Question 4 vs FT-32 vs AC-70 / AC-114: [main review] The patch outcome has two contradictory defaults: OQ4 asks for a sev-1 pre-stage, while FT-32 and AC-70 say the patch leaves nothing. Pillar 1 ('looks fixed, still cold') is at stake.
- [RECOMMENDED] FT-12 / rows 3, 4, 9 / AC-118, AC-104: [main review] Heat hue does not match the Graph's loop provenance (reactor OVERHEAT is coolant loop 3, SCRAM-by-starve is fuel). AC-118's fuel-tank case is unreachable. FREEZE/FUEL has no form. [qa-lead] AC-118 is also capture-only and cannot run headless.
- [RECOMMENDED] FT-30, Interactions row 15, AC-139, AC-141, ET-4, AC-43: [main review] SHORT clears on breaker open (LS-11), so a 'scorch stays' after breaker open is impossible. The derelict dried-scorch verdict needs a dead electrical descriptor that the Graph may not emit.
- [RECOMMENDED] D.5 dullness / DRIED_AFTER_STEPS / DULL_STEPS / AC-133: [main review] fuel_dried flips at half the ramp, so a live fuel leak can look dried. [systems-designer] The final dried stop starts at (DULL-1)/DULL of every medium's ramp, which breaks the 7500-step floor. [qa-lead, gameplay-programmer] Odd DULL_STEPS values are undefined. [game-designer] Dulling live trails at all is the underlying fault.
- [RECOMMENDED] FT-18 / ET-4 / AC-121: [game-designer] On a derelict, the most lethal loop (O2) leaves no residue. A healthy ship and an O2-killed ship both show zero pixels, which weakens Pillar 5's 'what killed this ship is readable'.
- [RECOMMENDED] Player Fantasy competence paragraph, Overview L24, FT-7: [game-designer] 'Sees the frost before the gauge drops' has no mechanism. A wearing pipe carries only WORN, which this system never draws and which the Filter may corrupt.
- [RECOMMENDED] FT-19 / NOT_DRAWN / T9 / AC-45a, AC-46: [gameplay-programmer] The cull test uses the anchor point, not the trail bounding box. A trail whose source is off-screen but whose body crosses the view pops in, which is the suppression FT-19 forbids. AC-45a currently approves this behaviour.
- [RECOMMENDED] D.8b flash clamp / ARC_SWING_PM / arc_rate_eff / FT-28: [main review] n=3 arcs swing 13.5%, over the 9% ceiling. [systems-designer] 'The region only grows' is false. At 384x216 the region is 9,216 px, and two arcs already reach about 11%. The rate rule contradicts the shared-phase rule. [gameplay-programmer] Two hold levels are given (60% and 88%), and n_arcs_region depends on the camera, so panning flips the rate.
- [RECOMMENDED] D.9 / D.7 / FT-16, FT-19 / TRAIL_FRAME_MS_P99 / AC-80-84, AC-136: [qa-lead, systems-designer] Room frost is budgeted at 12x4, but D.2b allows 24 per room, so the worst case is about 565 stamps, over the 384 budget, and AC-81's '325' is wrong. [performance-analyst] C_STAMP_US 3 is a best case (the GDD's own estimate is 6 writes per stamp). ALL at 325 stamps is about 1.7-6 ms. The 640 us keyed cascade breaks the 0.25 ms p99 target by design. There is no idle-frame no-work rule, no warm-up rule for shader variants, and no VRAM or pool pre-size budget. [main review] There is no stamp-level fallback.
- [RECOMMENDED] D.10 / V/A.13 / D.9 draw model: [performance-analyst, gameplay-programmer, main review] The per-instance reveal threshold, dull step, rank and clock route in Godot 4.7.2 CanvasItem is unverified ([UNV]). If it fails, batching collapses toward one draw per stamp. The render structure (per-trail _draw, MultiMesh custom data, or a Sprite2D pool) is not chosen. 2D light sets and the Y-sort interleave are not modelled in D_FT.
- [RECOMMENDED] Acceptance Criteria: test seams and runners: [qa-lead, gameplay-programmer] About 10 '(auto)' ACs need rendered pixels and cannot run in the mandated headless CI. The pure resolver is only an 'ADR candidate'. There is no test-seam rule (descriptor feed, geometry provider, present_time, settings). The statelessness lint (AC-2) contradicts the diagnostics flags (AC-110, AC-111, AC-136).
- [RECOMMENDED] Untestable / non-deterministic ACs: [main review, qa-lead] AC-31 (wall clock), AC-35 (no pass value), AC-83 and AC-84 (no hardware or fixture), AC-41b, 76 and 78 (sign-off only), reviewer-pool ACs (26b, 43, 57b, 74, 76) with no defined pool, AC-6 (tautological), AC-16 (sev 2-4 fixture unnamed), and Game Feel playtest targets with no protocol. [qa-lead] No AC verifies colourblind safety with a colour-vision-deficiency simulation.
- [RECOMMENDED] D.4 / D.6 / D.2 integer semantics: [systems-designer] DENSITY_BUCKETS_PER_LEVEL 4-5 makes density_level 4 unreachable inside a legal knob range. [systems-designer, gameplay-programmer] The u64 hash and floor semantics break under GDScript's signed 64-bit integers: negative variant indices are possible, and truncation differs from floor on retreat.
- [RECOMMENDED] FT-3, FT-15, Table of record geometry: [gameplay-programmer] The descriptor identity tuple omits medium, so SEAL_STRAIN GAS and HEAT merge into one trail. Step detection and multi-tick key union are unspecified. Only RUN geometry is defined, so radial scorch, soot and frame rows have no extent semantics.
- [RECOMMENDED] Dependencies / Gaps / Interactions row 16: [main review] Six dependencies marked undesigned now exist. Sanity, Boarding & Salvage, Modular Ship Assembly and HUD rows are missing. The Gaps ACs are now testable. ET-1 depends on Onboarding, which is a Vertical Slice system.
