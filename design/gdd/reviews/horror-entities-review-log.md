- [BLOCKING] Detailed Design HE-20, HE-21; Transition guards 2 and 4; Epoch counters (line 319); E-HE-43; AC-HE-47: [main review, sharpened by ai-programmer; spot-checked by creative-director] The horror can be frozen by its own dormancy rule, which gives a breach with no horror in it (cry-wolf). - [BLOCKING] HE-39; Cross-References C-HE-6 (line 1428); Owed refresh rows (line 1459); AC-HE-124; Suit and Equipment SE-11 slot 4, SE-14, SE-15, OQ-34; Item DB IDB:249: [main review; confirmed by creative-director] Two documents contradict each other, and the GDD treats a ruling that was never applied as settled. - [BLOCKING] HE-14 conditions (a) to (e); HE-19; E-HE-20; E-HE-35; AC-HE-23, 27, 37; LURK_STANDOFF_PX tests: [ai-programmer, raised to BLOCKING by creative-director] Nothing in the horror's own rules stops a REACH from starting through a wall. HE-14 (d) is only a Euclidean check (within 96 px). E-HE-20 cites a 'segment test' that is not among conditions (a) to (e). The result is that a stalking horror in a corridor can unfold a limb through solid wall at a lit seat in the next room. The strike then misses and the cooldown is spent. A telegraph that is drawn through a wall, for a lethal mechanic, breaks the core fairness promise in P2 ('a careful player could have perceived and had somewhere to run') and Pillar 1. I am raising this from RECOMMENDED because it is a defect in the core lethal loop, not an edge case. The same distance-through-walls flaw affects the LURK and TELL standoff tests.
- [BLOCKING] HE-25; E-HE-31; AC-HE-51 versus Phase view (line 305); E-HE-2; AC-HE-28: [ai-programmer, raised to BLOCKING by creative-director] Two BLOCKING acceptance criteria cannot both pass. The Phase view marks LURK as n/a in BREACH, and AC-HE-28 asserts the record is never in LURK during BREACH. An implementation must fail one of them. It is also a cry-wolf path: a BREACH with a horror that cannot do harm.

[ai-programmer] Diagonal walk-out under Body's octant stepper takes 37 ticks, not 36, so the real slack is 3 ticks. The budget has no allowance for input and display latency, and there is no accessibility lever. Creative-director: this is the single most important feel risk in the document. If it is wrong, players will read the game as unfair, not as horror.
