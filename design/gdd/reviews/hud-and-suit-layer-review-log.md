- [BLOCKING] Formulas D.6 hud_settle_response / AC-HS-20, AC-HS-21, AC-HS-98 / GF-HS-1 / Tuning Knobs HUD_SETTLE_ZETA: [main][systems-designer][qa-lead][game-designer] AC-HS-20 is red at the GDD's own defaults: |x-1| is 0.0248 at 300 ms and 0.0214 at frame 19. [systems-designer] The miss depends on zeta alone, so it fails at every allowed zeta. At zeta 0.99 the miss is about 0.085 and the true settle is about 0.44 s, which breaks the art bible's 250-400 ms. At zeta 0.69 the overshoot is 5.004 %, just over the 5 % cap. [systems-designer][qa-lead][ui-programmer] Worse, D.6 is only a response to a single step from rest. Nothing defines what happens when the target changes again mid-settle, and that is the normal case in the anchor breach scenario (the Graph steps every 6 ticks, about every 100 ms, against a 300 ms settle). Implementers would produce visibly different needles at the most important moment of the game. [game-designer] The GDD itself says the overshoot is invisible (0.2-0.4 px).
- [BLOCKING] Formulas D.14 warmth_band_index / HS-18 / EC-HS-20 / OQ-HS-9: [systems-designer, verified by CD] D.14 contradicts the Suit's published contract. suit-and-equipment.md:245 and :1204 (SE-29) fix warmth_pm with COMFORT at 500 and edges 0/333/466/533/666/1000. HUD D.14 (hud-and-suit-layer.md:718) uses band = min(6, pm*7 div 1000), so nominal pm 500 draws band 3 (COOL), and its own example at :730 puts COMFORT at 600. The dial draws the wrong zone at rest at MVP defaults. D.14 also labels a thermal-load axis with ambient band_t names, and the Suit's axis has 5 load zones, not 7 cells. EC-HS-20 and OQ-HS-9 are stale because the Suit has already answered.
- [BLOCKING] Detailed Rules HS-22 vs AC-HS-102 / AC-HS-37 / EC-HS-22 / anchor-moment table: [main][ux-designer, verified by CD] HS-22 makes the breath mark exclusive: it goes in the strip slot only for GASP/NONE and in the reserve slot when the reserve is engaged. AC-HS-102 (hud-and-suit-layer.md:1707) asserts marks in both the strip and reserve slots. AC-HS-37 and EC-HS-22 need five simultaneous marks. The rule for the single most important mark scenario is self-contradictory. [ux-designer] HS-22 also conflates 'breath CRITICAL while reserve engaged' with 'reserve axis reaches CRITICAL'.
- [BLOCKING] Formulas D.8 bottom_row_fit / HS-54 / HS-16 / AC-HS-79, 80, 82, 115 / OQ-HS-33, OQ-HS-34: [ui-programmer][qa-lead, verified by CD at hud-and-suit-layer.md:597] D.8 is a sum inequality, but the strip is centred, so the true constraint is per side: max(W_panel, W_rail) <= floor((canvas_w - W_strip)/2) - margin - GAP, about 102 HUD px each at 1280/H4, not 204 for the pair. The '204 floor invariant' that the UX spec is told to size against accepts layouts that overlap the strip. There is no vertical fit check. :610's 'keep the previous valid layout' fallback is invalid after a resize or on first mount. [main] The offered H set and the panel geometry are undecided. [qa-lead] HS-54, AC-HS-80, AC-HS-82 and the D.8 table define the offered H set three different ways. [qa-lead] The plate width is 63 or 76 depending on where you read. [systems-designer] D.5/D.11/D.15 only work for a 180-degree sweep, yet PANEL_SWEEP_DEG defaults to 240, which cannot fit the 2x2 arrangement. [ui-programmer] PR-12 already states option (a) on the Pipeline side.
- [BLOCKING] Detailed Rules HS-6 / HS-35 / HS-41 / HS-42 (CF-HS-16, OQ-HS-18) / AC-HS-52, 61, 65, 66: [main][ux-designer] The ACK, hold-to-confirm fill/drain, glyph swap and two-tap confirm all depend on Controller reads that no GDD publishes: hold fraction, pressed action, glyph token, and confirm mode. Hold-to-confirm guards destructive actions at MVP. [ux-designer] Two-tap has no input or setting anywhere, so AC-HS-66 cannot pass or fail. A 0.8 s hold is the core loop's main motor-accessibility barrier. [game-designer dissents on whether this blocks the GDD itself; see disagreements.]
- [RECOMMENDED] HS-14 / HS-47 / D.2 / Tuning HONEST_TICK_MS / AC-HS-73, AC-HS-123: [main] The arbiter mixes the present_time and tick clocks and omits several luminance events: rail bracket, count substitutions, scanner show-start, OUTCOME dim and the ACK. [systems-designer] The 400-500 safe range comes from an average rate, but the arbiter tests a rolling window. Below 30 ticks, a window can hold 3 grid boundaries plus a mark edge, so 500 ms is the only feasible value; under u=2, MARK_DUTY_PM must be 500. The honest boundary is both 'clock-locked' and 'deferrable'. [qa-lead] The validator checks a static range instead of the derived floor.
- [RECOMMENDED] HS-47 / FLASH_MAX_PER_S scope (user decision, with OQ-HS-2/14/15): [game-designer][ux-designer] The 3/s ceiling throttles 1-px digit swaps and the 12-px prompt plate. This costs up to 1000 ms of O2 lag and a 650 ms valve-prompt delay in the anchor breach moment (Challenge aesthetic, P2). WCAG 2.3.1 weights flashes by area, and these elements are far below the area threshold. [ux-designer] reduce_flashing as documented contradicts EC-HS-25, and reduced_motion needlessly removes the mark pulse.
- [RECOMMENDED] HS-25 / OQ-HS-7, OQ-HS-8 (suit.alarm): [main][ux-designer] A deaf player who also uses reduced motion has no ship-alarm tell at MVP (P2). The GDD parks this as 'accept the gap', and design/accessibility-requirements.md does not exist to record the gap.
- [RECOMMENDED] HS-52 / UI Req 10 / AC-HS-126 (lamp-off mouse aim): [main][game-designer][ux-designer] With the lamp off and mouse aim, the player has no aim reference. Lamp-off play is a core P3 restraint strategy. A hand pose quantised to 5 facings is probably too coarse for throws, and rotating it may conflict with PR-4.
- [RECOMMENDED] HS-3 / Truth-channel classification: [main] 'Exactly six families' and 'three classes' conflict with the R and T mark classes, which breaks the Filter-immunity lint in AC-HS-42/88.
- [RECOMMENDED] HS-17 / EC-HS-53 / reserve cell: [main] The reserve numeral is optimistic under a seam leak and depends on an unregistered Suit margin. [game-designer] The reserve, the player's real death clock, has no engraved reference. The 'countdown' ban in AC-HS-7 collides with the reserve numeral.
- [RECOMMENDED] Prompts: HS-37/40/41/42 / EC-HS-31 / EC-HS-34 / D.16: [ui-programmer][ux-designer] A press during the debounce or arbiter window has no plate to carry the ACK or bar, and a rejected press leaves no trace. [systems-designer] EC-HS-31 is false at the defaults: the 9-tick debounce is below the 12-tick churn, so the plate pops for 50 ms. The cancel drain scales with channel length (up to 3 s), and overlapping channels are undefined. [ux-designer] Hold requirements are invisible before the press.
- [RECOMMENDED] AC tags / CI / AC-HS-30, 84, 88, 92, 101 / perf budget: [ui-programmer][qa-lead] Pixel and draw-call ACs tagged (auto) cannot run under the headless gdUnit4 CI. AC-HS-92 cannot fail, and the reference hardware is unnamed. There is no test-support epic (stubs, fixture runner). Per-frame Perceived records allocate in GDScript. [ui-programmer] The real budget risk is primitives and draw calls from procedurally drawn dial faces, not memory.
- [RECOMMENDED] HS-2 / HS-9 / D.9 / spikes S1, S4 / PAUSED_FROZEN: [main][ui-programmer] The coordinate space of a HUD pixel is contradictory: D.9 works in screen px, while the Pipeline uses a CanvasLayer scaled by H under a canvas_items root. - [RECOMMENDED] Cross-References / registry hygiene: [main] Stale 'HUD undesigned' labels remain in modular-ship-assembly, failure-and-trail, attention and horror-entities. Emitter EM-22 says the HUD reads lit(ref). K41/OQ-HS-17 are stale. O2_DIAL_MAX_DKPA claims a Filter consumer that does not exist.
- [RECOMMENDED] HS-53 DEAD / user decision 'death = restart from last autosave': [main] Nothing names who owns the visible step from death to restore, what the player presses, or how long the screen dwells.
- [RECOMMENDED] HS-26 / OQ-HS-11, 12 / D.20 (VS-A scope): [game-designer] The dormant lie slots are heavy MVP work that no MVP playtest exercises, and the charge cell breaks the no-redraw promise.
