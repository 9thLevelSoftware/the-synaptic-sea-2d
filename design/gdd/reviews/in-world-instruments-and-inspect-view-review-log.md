Findings:
- [BLOCKING] IW-8 / IW-19 / IW-29 floors (iv)-(v) / manifest (L362-381): [main review, confirmed by creative-director spot-check] I confirmed that IW-8 (L213) declares `beyond_witness` in {REMOTE, MAGNITUDE, TREND} without defining any of the three values, and the term appears nowhere else in design/ (10 hits, all in this file). The manifest applies the tags inconsistently. `inst_cryo_bay_p_o2`, `inst_a_machine_T` and `inst_b_reactor_T` are bound to their own compartments but tagged REMOTE. IW-19 places REMOTE ahead of clauses (a) to (c), and IW-29(v) builds the `ALARM_MARK_ON_REMOTE` lint on that tag. Because nothing separates the room a dial is mounted in from the subject it reads, neither the alarm-mark placement nor the lint can be implemented or tested. Correction to the main review: by my reading `inst_cryo_bay_T` (DIAL_L, TREND, compartment-bound) could still carry the mark, so it is not limited to `inst_home_fuel_level` and panel mounts. The rule is still incoherent. [game-designer] The same gap means Beat C, the lethal remote lie and the game's flagship P1/P4 moment, has no instrument in the manifest that can produce it.
- [BLOCKING] IW-5 / IW-9 `load` / D.1 / E-IW-4 / AC-IW-63: [main review, confirmed by creative-director against LSG LS-19 additions L206] LS-19 publishes `supply` and `demand` as true_value kinds and says 'Instruments' `load = floor(1000 x demand / max(1, supply))` is its own reading'. The Filter can only wrap a true_value, so it cannot lie about `load`, and IW-5 forbids Instruments from deriving values outside the Filter. As written, the only MVP power gauge has no lie path consistent with P4, and D.1 has no dirty-key quantum for it. [systems-designer][game-designer] The formula also makes a dead bus (supply 0, demand > 0) peg the NOTCHED overload stop with no tremor, so the dial reports 'overload' when the real fault is 'supply lost'. The player is told to fix the wrong thing. That breaks P1: an honest gauge must not mislead.
- [BLOCKING] IW-11(a)(b) / D.4 trigger / E-IW-8 / AC-IW-17 / Player Fantasy Framing L41 (honest-motion grammar): [main review RECOMMENDED; elevated by creative-director on evidence from systems-designer and game-designer] The game's core lesson is 'shake = real, wander = lie' (P1/P4), and players can only learn it if honest motion is well defined. It is not. (1) [main] IW-11(b) defines 'rest' per step, while D.4 and AC-IW-17 define it per sample. [systems-designer] Under the per-sample reading the settle can never fire on p_o2 (16.7 pm quantum) or stock (20 pm) at any REST_PM in the registered 5-20 range. (2) [game-designer] Even under the per-step reading, a jump of at least 40 pm in one 100 ms step is effectively unreachable for atmosphere and stock under the GDD's own flow rates, and below about 216 pm the overshoot is sub-pixel. The settle that the fantasy leads with ('watches a needle catch up and come to rest') therefore fires visibly only on the under-specified `load` dial. (3) [game-designer] LS-20 buckets have no hysteresis, so an honest value hovering on a quantum edge flips the needle 1-2 px at up to 10 Hz with no stress descriptor. That looks like tremor on a calm reading and teaches the lesson backwards. An implementer cannot build IW-11 to one interpretation, and the D.4 lint machinery may be guarding a motion that never happens.
- [RECOMMENDED] IW-25 / E-IW-29 / OQ-IW-17 (movement swallowed while card open): [ux-designer][game-designer] Movement intent is zeroed in `inspect_card`, and the first move press is consumed and does nothing. The GDD also says the card closes 'at any moment at no cost'. With lethal friendly fire and restart from autosave, an eaten input can kill the player, and in this game the UI frame must never betray the player. The creative-director agrees and asks that this be resolved before the UX spec rather than at the first playtest.
- [RECOMMENDED] IW-21 / IW-24 / E-IW-16 / E-IW-52 / Feel table (inspect debounce): [ux-designer][ui-programmer] The 9-tick prompt-show debounce is also used as an input lockout. Presses made the moment focus lands, or straight after a close, are silently dropped. This contradicts E-IW-52's 'no cooldown', and nothing defines what starts the timer.
- [RECOMMENDED] IW-21 / IW-31 (focus arbitration with host machine): [ux-designer] With MACHINE_PANEL instruments and a single sticky focus, either the gauge takes focus from the repair or valve prompt or the gauge cannot be inspected. `interact` on a gauge gives no feedback.
- [RECOMMENDED] S4 / IW-24 close list (context transitions): [ui-programmer] S4 has no transitions from `inspect_card` to `chart_table`, `chat` or the pause menu. In co-op the overlay does not pause the sim, so it is undefined whether the card stays drawn under a modal.
- [RECOMMENDED] IW-23 / D.6 (card placement fallback and occlusion): [ui-programmer] The fallback is 'farthest from chest point, clamped', and the clamp can put the plate over the instrument, which breaks the IW-23 invariant. This is reachable at 720p H 6 with k 4. [ux-designer] The keep-outs ignore the witness (drip, frost, crewmate) and teammates, so the plate can hide the honest second reading the fantasy depends on.
- [RECOMMENDED] IW-21 / IW-22 / Design test 1 / AC-IW-52 (card purpose): [game-designer] No MVP decision is named that needs the card's numeral rather than the zone band, so 'inspect selectively' is satisfied by never opening it. The 'a little exposed' cost is not confirmed against Attention's light term. The creative-director agrees: the card ships live at MVP and needs a stated decision to justify itself.
- [RECOMMENDED] IW-22 / VA-11 / D.6 (LINEAR card layout): [main review] The 72x72 plate cannot hold the 62 px LINEAR glass plus label and numeral rows, and no LINEAR card asset exists. Four MVP instruments are LINEAR. [ui-programmer] This needs to be resolved together with the card rendering approach.
- [RECOMMENDED] D.4 / Tuning / registry (INSTR_SETTLE_* ranges): [main review][systems-designer] The published box of settle parameters is not jointly safe (0.7/18/360 gives 1.51 px). [systems-designer] The main review's patch 'MS >= 380 at zeta 0.7' is not enough, because 0.7/18/380 still gives 1.17 px.
- [RECOMMENDED] D.5 tremor (TREMOR_GAIN, A_eff, frequency hash): [systems-designer] A_eff 2 at k=1 is practically unreachable, because round(1.5*s) reaches 2 only at |s|=1. The k=1 tremor is therefore 1 px, the HR-12 floor claim is false, and AC-IW-66 already asserts +1. Near-equal f1 and f2 (about 12% of instruments) produce long or permanent null windows with no tremor. [game-designer] At k=1 the intended 2 px tremor would match the 2 px wander, which removes the amplitude cue.
- [RECOMMENDED] D.12 / T_VIS_MAX_S / D.1 honest sample value / D.3 example: [systems-designer] The T_VIS_MAX_S safe range (1-4 s) fails four quantities at 1 s, and the lint ignores the lying-tick period T_tick. The honest sample value (true value or grid-aligned value) is unspecified. D.3's example memos 182 and 172 are unreachable. Honest lsb finer than the quantum could make digit granularity reveal honest versus lying readings (a P4 risk).
- [RECOMMENDED] D.9 gleam cap vs Perception Filter AMP_MAX_PM / LEAK_MAX_CH: [systems-designer] With #B07BE6 at alpha 1 the peak channel is 0.902, which exceeds the Filter's 0.85 ceiling. The design accepts a self-reported defect for amplitudes 943-1000 instead of fixing it.
- [RECOMMENDED] Dependencies / Interactions / IW-9 fallback / IW-29 counts / K-13, OQ-IW-2, -26, -27: [main review, confirmed by creative-director] The LS-19 additions (L206, 2026-10-27) now provide `stress_active`, `stock_pm` and the full true_value kind set. The 'IW-9 fallback' column, the `inst_cryo_bay_T` TRUTH-default (L365) and the post-fallback counts (home 4 / A 3 / B 4, A AT-FLOOR) are stale.
- [RECOMMENDED] Decisions required before implementation (HONEST_TICK_MS, BR-1..9, K-14 aim-live, HS-52 cursor, shared-component owner): [main review][ux-designer][ui-programmer] Parts of the MVP floor rest on pending decisions owned elsewhere.
