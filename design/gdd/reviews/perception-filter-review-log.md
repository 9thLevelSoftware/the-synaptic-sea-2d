# Design Review Log — perception-filter

## Review — 2026-10-27 — Verdict: NEEDS REVISION
Scope signal: XL
Specialists: systems-designer, game-designer, qa-lead, performance-analyst (spawned but returned no output: godot-specialist)
Blocking items: 3 | Recommended: 15
Summary: The Perception Filter's design is right. Its core idea is P4's 'the instruments lie, the room tells the truth', expressed through a seam grammar (gleam, glide and sound), and it is coherent, faithful to the pillars and unusually rigorous. Nothing here needs a rethink, so the verdict is NEEDS REVISION, not MAJOR REVISION NEEDED.  I checked the following in the files: - `lie_id` does not appear anywhere in the Sanity GDD. - PF-15 (line 307) makes GLEAM and the motion mode mandatory, but the cut order at lines 943, 1120 and 1854 drops housing and glyph gleams. - AC-PF-19 (0 at and below eps) and AC-PF-87 (a 1.5 s fade at disc 0.1) contradict each other. Prior verdict resolved: First review
Findings:
- [BLOCKING] Formulas D.14 / Detailed Rules PF-8, PF-46 / AC-PF-30, AC-PF-82: [main review, confirmed by systems-designer, qa-lead, game-designer; CD spot-check] The Filter's determinism hash, its tie-break and its test vectors all depend on `lie_id`, and nothing upstream creates it. A grep of sanity-and-hallucination-rules.md finds 0 occurrences of `lie_id`. [systems-designer] A second problem sits in the same hash: `epoch` is an input to the per-lie draws in D.2 and D.4, but it is presentation-local and not saved (AC-PF-70). A restore resets it, so fixing `lie_id` alone does not make D.14 restore-testable. [game-designer] PF-8 uses `lie_id = 0` to mean 'no lie', so any replacement key must be forced non-zero.
- [BLOCKING] Detailed Rules PF-15, PF-42 / Formulas D.12 / Edge Cases E-PF-50, E-PF-10, E-PF-11, E-PF-56 / AC-PF-108: [main review; extended by systems-designer, game-designer and performance-analyst; CD spot-check of lines 307, 943, 1120 and 1854] PF-15 makes GLEAM and the motion mode mandatory on every ACTIVE lie and enforces this with a lint. The over-budget fallback breaks both rules. (a) The cut order in D.12, E-PF-50 and AC-PF-108 drops housing and glyph gleams, which leaves palette-silent lies. (b) [systems-designer] That cut order cannot even reach the budget at stage 3: 26 draws minus 8 still leaves 18 against the limit of 10. (c) [game-designer] The motion-mode half of the rule is also broken: E-PF-10, E-PF-11 and E-PF-56 draw lies that have only the gleam and the sound, and static-pair housing and glyph lies have no motion signature defined. E-PF-50's claim that 'the glide and the sound need no draw' is false for those surfaces. The core leak contract contradicts itself, and the stated lint would fail its own fallback.
- [BLOCKING] Formulas D.5 RETURN / States S3 / PF-8 / AC-PF-19 vs AC-PF-87 / E-PF-2: [systems-designer; CD verified lines 731, 1772, 1773] AC-PF-19 requires amp to be 0 at and below LEAK_EPS_DEG after removal. AC-PF-87 requires amp to fade linearly from 400 to 0 over LEAK_FADE_S (1.5 s) while disc is about 0.1 deg, which is already below that threshold. The two ACs cannot both pass. S3 also leaves RETURN as soon as disc drops below eps. That puts the record in NONE (lie_id 0, HONEST) while the violet fade and the sound are still running, and the Debug Overlay would then show a gleam on an honest record. [main review] Separately, the RETURN amplitude is unbounded when the truth moves away during the return (it can reach 1409 pm), and it is discontinuous at the branch boundary disc_end = 2 x eps.
- [RECOMMENDED] Player Fantasy (V1, V4, 'room answers') vs PF-15 / PF-16 / D.5: [game-designer; CD priority] The gleam is mandatory, always on and has a floor of 400. On binary surfaces (FALSE_OK, FALSE_FAIL, housing, glyph, SPRITE_SWAP), once players learn what violet means, it tells them both where the lie is and what the truth is: invert the reading. That turns P4's 'costly falsification' into a search for violet pixels and makes the room witness redundant. This is a real tension between pillars P1 and P4, and the most important design-level finding. I do not rate it a blocker because the GDD already has levers that can address it.
- [RECOMMENDED] Formulas D.2 / D.3 / PF-13 / E-PF-7, E-PF-10, E-PF-56: [main review, systems-designer, game-designer] A FALSE_OK or FALSE_FAIL lie often cannot reach its zone. D_draw ignores how far the zone is, the departure is capped at 0.4 of the arc, and D.3's own example stops at 800 when the zone centre is 900. Leak-only lies (displayed value close to the truth, but still violet and sound) teach players that 'violet does not mean wrong' and still use up LIES_MAX slots.
- [RECOMMENDED] Formulas D.2 / PF-13 / PF-39 / AC-PF-52: [main review, systems-designer, game-designer] Reduced motion shrinks the lie (D_eff can fall to 12 deg at rm 0.5), so it works as a hidden difficulty slider. The boot assertion uses the maximum rate and so does not protect D_VIS_MIN_DEG. With D_DRAW_MIN_DEG at 12 and rm at 0.5, the departure is 6 deg, below the D_VIS minimum of 8.
- [RECOMMENDED] Formulas D.3 slew / D.5 disc range / PF-41: [systems-designer] With R_act at 8.33 pm/s, a fast change in the true value (for example a breach) leaves the lying needle lagging by up to a full dial for up to a minute. That breaks the 40% fairness cap and hides exactly the emergencies W_MIN_WARNING_S is meant to protect. room_deg is evaluated at an unspecified time, and the GLIDE target is not clamped to the dial.
- [RECOMMENDED] Formulas D.4 / AC-PF-46: [systems-designer, a concrete counterexample the main review only hinted at] At a 45-degree tangent, 0.35 Hz and A 2.0, the needle tip pixel rests for 1.16 s, which is over the art bible's 1.0 s ceiling. This cannot be fixed within the current WANDER_AMP_PX range. There is also an onset rest when the tremor stops.
- [RECOMMENDED] Formulas D.9 / PF-18 / AC-PF-75, AC-PF-102: [main review, systems-designer, qa-lead] The lying numeral's behaviour in RETURN is not specified. Its spacing guarantee breaks when N_r changes mid-run. The STALE numeral jumps to the zone centre while the needle is still short of it, and the ±1 lsb step can leave the valid range.
- [RECOMMENDED] Detailed Rules PF-20 / D.15: [main review; game-designer] The lie sound has no visibility gating, no level ceiling, no duty cycle and no plan for habituation. Four lies could play constant tones lasting minutes, which risks masking the P1 consequence sounds and getting the sound muted. See disagreements: I rule against gating it on on-screen and lit.
- [RECOMMENDED] Detailed Rules PF-23, PF-24 vs PF-G5 / HUD row / OQ-PF-1: [game-designer] The ambient violet on the beam rim at stage 2+ and the hue bleed on pipes at stage 3 contradict PF-G5 ('the only violet in screen space is a gleam on a lying reading'). They also dilute 'violet marks the liar' and act as a stage meter.
- [RECOMMENDED] AC-PF-29 / AC-SN-154 discharge / OQ-PF-27: [qa-lead, rated BLOCKING] AC-PF-29 is tagged MVP-live, but two of its three tasks are cut or stubs at MVP: the rim is conditional and the crewmate is VS-B. It is the only discharge of AC-SN-154, and pooling the trials is pseudo-replication. I rate it RECOMMENDED because it blocks story closure, not implementation; see disagreements.
- [RECOMMENDED] Acceptance Criteria (render tag) / OQ-PF-26: [qa-lead] Headless runs use the dummy rasterizer, so roughly 17 BLOCKING render ACs can pass vacuously: two blank captures compare as bit-identical, and the draw count reads 0. Render captures also lack a requirement to retain the evidence with a sign-off.
- [RECOMMENDED] PF-42 / E-PF-51 / AC-PF-76 / D.12-D.13: [performance-analyst; main review] The 1-draw MVP budget assumes a CanvasItem per-instance shader parameter that may not exist in 4.7.2, and S-1 is sequenced after rules that depend on it. There is no MVP GPU budget for the leak block in the shared ubershader. The CPU model uses unmeasured placeholders and only bounds the median. All 80 refs are polled every frame, and GDScript record allocation is not addressed.
- [RECOMMENDED] Detailed Rules PF-9 / Open Questions OQ-PF-34, OQ-PF-36: [main review] The join keys between Sanity, the Filter, Instruments and the Overlay are still provisional: the mapping from subject_ref to PerceptionRef, the true source of SCANNER, and the active_run fallback.
- [RECOMMENDED] Open Questions OQ-PF-31 / Player Fantasy: [main review; game-designer] At MVP the user decision INTENSE is knowingly under-delivered, since only class (b) ships, and the scope of PF-27 to PF-30 depends on a briefing that has not happened. The briefing should also cover how intense a single lie is (deceptive dwell is only about 40% of a lie's life).
- [RECOMMENDED] Detailed Rules PF-11 / D.3 / AC-PF-4a; Edge Cases (Instruments onset ruling); stale text: [main review] Restore-exactness is overstated compared with the slew gap. The Instruments onset-snap ruling (IW:243) has no edge case. Text left over from the consistency pass is stale: AC-PF-26's fixtures omit 2133 ms, AC-PF-23 says 'blocked', C-7 is out of date, and the Audio row says 'in-flight'. The self_modulate fallback needs a contract for a reserved channel.
- [RECOMMENDED] Acceptance Criteria (untestable list; test doubles; scenarios): [main review, qa-lead] Eight ACs are subjective or unmeasurable (AC-PF-44, 74b, 77, 57b and 1, and the Feel bullets). About 20 ACs run against stubs that are not defined. Scripted 10-minute scenarios are not named. AC-PF-72 uses random input. AC-PF-64 and AC-PF-57a are unsound. The LINEAR sight-glass branch has no AC.

- [BLOCKING] Cross-References X-5 / OQ-PF-30 (design/registry/entities.yaml, entries owned by perception-filter.md): [main-review, confirmed by creative-director spot-check] The registry entries this GDD owns still carry the superseded values. I checked them myself. `presentation_hash` (entities.yaml:5083-5094) still has `H(viewer, ref_key, lie_id, epoch)`, no `lie_key` rule, no epoch-0 rule for per-lie draws, and vectors only up to tag 10. `lie_leak_amplitude` (entities.yaml:5054-5066) still has the two-branch RETURN `amp_end*g(disc)/g(disc_end); disc_end <= 2*LEAK_EPS_DEG: linear fade`, which is the rule blocker 3 removed. `GLIDE_RATE_BAND` (entities.yaml:10035) still reads `WANDER_AMP_PX 2.0 at 0.35-0.45 Hz`, which D.4 proves fails the never-rest rule at 45 deg. The review also reports, and I did not re-check, that `lie_displayed_value`, `lying_digit_tick_schedule` and `filter_counters` are stale. Grepping for `lie_key`, `DISC_CAP_PM` and `lies_zone_missed` in the registry finds nothing. Because the registry is the cross-document source of truth, anyone who builds from it would rebuild the three defects this revision fixed.
- [RECOMMENDED] Interactions I-B / OQ-PF-39 (HUD, In-World Instruments, Debug Overlay GDDs): [main-review] Consumer GDDs have drifted from the amended Filter. Instruments and HUD do not handle BREATH or `tremor_weight_pm`. The HUD's AC for 2.0 px wander at 0.35 Hz fails at 45 deg. The Debug Overlay still has the old CLEAR rule and the old tie-break. OQ-PF-39's claim that the HUD needs 'no change beyond BREATH' is wrong. [creative-director] This drift matters for P1 (the lie must be readable on every surface). Fold it into the same confirming consistency-check as the blocker.
- [RECOMMENDED] PF-15 / D.5 / D.8 / AC-PF-29a (static-pair surfaces: glyph, housing, machine frame): [main-review] On these surfaces BREATH and GLEAM are the same violet edge pixels. With hue and audio both removed, only a 1 to 2 px breathing edge at L* about 61 remains, the same lightness as Hull Grey and Fuel Ember. So HR-5's 'each mode sufficient alone' is asserted but not shown. [creative-director] I rate this the most important design-level item after the blocker. The Filter's player promise is that a lie always leaves a tell an attentive player can learn, for every player. This needs an AC, not just a note.
- [RECOMMENDED] Formulas D.9 / PF-18 (lying numeral during RETURN): [main-review] The forced-change TICK clause keeps running in RETURN, so the digit oscillates by one lsb around the truth until CLEAR and then stops. That is a visible 'the lie just ended' signature, against PF-G9. [creative-director] Agree. The game is meant to let players catch lies by attention, not hand them an unintended end-of-lie tell.
- [RECOMMENDED] AC-PF-4a / AC-PF-4b (restore): [main-review] Both ACs compare `displayed_pm`, which includes the wander, within 1 pm while letting the wander phase differ. As written they fail by design.
- [RECOMMENDED] Formulas D.6 vs E-PF-64: [main-review] E-PF-64 says the follower is clamped by DISC_CAP_PM, but the D.6 formula has no such clamp, and no AC tests the cap.
- [RECOMMENDED] PF-16 / PF-42 / E-PF-51 vs AC-PF-76 (spike labels): [main-review, confirmed by creative-director spot-check at perception-filter.md:1186] E-PF-51 names the MultiMesh overlay spike 'S-1b', but AC-PF-76 defines S-1b as instance-parameter batching and S-1c as the overlay. So nobody can tell which spike unlocks path 2.
- [RECOMMENDED] PF-23 / PF-24 / D.7 / D.11 / Acceptance Criteria (RIM_MODE LIE_BOUND, AMBIENT_FRINGES_ENABLED false): [main-review] No AC exercises either alternate setting. The D.11 reversal-hazard argument cites STAGE_DWELL_RUNS, which does not govern the lie-driven gate. [creative-director] These ACs are needed before the user rules on OQ-PF-1 and OQ-PF-2, because I expect to recommend these exact settings.
- [RECOMMENDED] Formulas D.9 vs registry hud_flash_budget: [main-review] Two arbiters claim authority over transition spacing in a region: D.9 defers lying ticks, and hud_flash_budget says lying ticks are never deferred.
- [RECOMMENDED] V.1 PFV-7 / V.6.1 / Animation Feel table / PF-19 / AC headers / OQ-PF-16: [main-review] The revision left stale or contradictory prose: the gleam is 1 px in one place and 2 art px in another, the Animation Feel row says the tick 'stops', PF-19 says 'from the cut', the AC-PF-49 subrange label is wrong, AC-PF-57b says 'twelve' items, and OQ-PF-16 is marked CLOSED but still reads as provisional.
- [RECOMMENDED] Acceptance Criteria (untestable list): [main-review] AC-PF-42, 67, 19, 15 and 57b are not independently testable as written. AC-PF-27 has no owner for BREATH_LOCK_CEIL, and AC-PF-43b, 43c and 76 depend on reference hardware that is not named.
- [RECOMMENDED] Open Questions OQ-PF-1, OQ-PF-2, OQ-PF-31, OQ-PF-37 (user rulings): [main-review] Four user rulings are outstanding. [creative-director] They are not GDD defects, but the defaults in force (AMBIENT rim on, stage-3 bleed on) blur the line between 'the world is uncanny' and 'this specific thing is lying', which works against PF-G5. My position: recommend RIM_MODE = LIE_BOUND and AMBIENT_FRINGES_ENABLED = false. Accept MVP intensity at class b only, with deceptive_dwell_frac tracked in playtest. Resolve the P1/P4 violet-inversion tension in favour of P1 readability. The user decides.
