The main review proposed closing OQ 16 by citing EM-18/EM-21, but that closure rests on a misreading and would hide the bug. This breaks Pillar 3: what you light, you can touch.
- [BLOCKING] States and Transitions (transition table, L179-197): [gameplay-programmer, confirmed by creative-director] The table says 'every pair not listed is invalid and is reported to diagnostics', and the AC at L1015 enforces that. I read L183-195. The table has no row for WALK->CARRY_WALK on pickup (L186 covers only RUN). It has none for CARRY_WALK->WALK/RUN on drop, install or clear_carry_ref. Only IDLE/WALK->IN_POD exists, yet L702 and the AC at L1012 both use pod_hop while carrying. A literal implementation will either flag every pickup made while walking as invalid, or leave WALK active with `carry_ref` set. In the second case the wrong stride, speed and noise class (D.13) would apply.
- [BLOCKING] PC-5 / D.7 / pipeline C11 and C4 (mouse aim vs camera look-ahead feedback): [gameplay-programmer; systems-designer raises a related point; creative-director confirms] PC-5 (L123) feeds raw aim into the camera look-ahead (C4). Mouse aim reads `cursor_world` through C11. C11's `world_to_hud` uses the camera pose `pose_q` (pipeline AC L960), and that pose includes the look-ahead. So aim moves the camera, and the camera moves the cursor's world position. When the cursor is near the avatar, this loop makes the beam slew slowly towards the cursor and keep drifting after the mouse stops. The GDD forbids exactly that ('no input-side smoothing'), and Pillar 1 treats the beam as the identity object that must not lie. The cursor is hidden in gameplay, so the player sees only the drifting beam. This is an analytical result and has not been observed, but the spec as written builds the loop.
- [BLOCKING] PC-9 / PC-17 (cap falling-edge state and restore): [qa-lead BLOCKING, gameplay-programmer RECOMMENDED] A stagger fires on a falling edge from FULL to WALK_ONLY, which needs a stored previous cap. No such field exists in the published, persisted or normalised field lists. The behaviour after a restore while the cap is WALK_ONLY is therefore undefined: the player is either staggered on every load or never staggered. The stated snapshot byte-identity cannot be checked. Several other fields are also missing from the persisted list: `ramp_ticks`, `last_octant`, and the stagger and downed timers.
- [BLOCKING] Formulas D.7 / D.11 vs Acceptance Criteria L922, L943-944, L1027: [qa-lead, systems-designer; arithmetic confirmed by creative-director] Several golden values and formulas are wrong, so a correct implementation would fail the GDD's own ACs. (1) atan2(19, 8) = 67.166 degrees, which gives aim_q = 764. The D.7 example and AC L943/L944 use 765, an artefact of rounding twice. (2) D.11 weights a pixel by ODO_W[octant]. On a diagonal slide with one axis blocked, the feet then advance at 0.50 of the cardinal rate. Edge Cases L656 and AC L1027 claim 0.707, while AC L922 fixes the weight at 181, which gives 0.50. The two ACs contradict each other. (3) D.7 and D.8 never wrap the atan2 result. GDScript's % keeps the sign of the dividend, so for the whole lower half-plane `aim_q` comes out negative and the sector index `f_aim_raw` is negative too. No AC covers that half of the screen.
- [RECOMMENDED] PC-3/PC-4 rule 3, camera look-ahead (Pillars 2 and 3): [game-designer] Retreating at full run while keeping the beam on the pursuer costs nothing, and the look-ahead even shows what is behind you. L116 claims the trailing light creates tension, but no rule produces it. Only run noise is lost. This weakens 'Dread Before Chaos' at the moment it matters most. It is a creative call, and the user should make it.
- [RECOMMENDED] Player Fantasy L32 / Game Feel: [game-designer] The line 'the lamp beam turns exactly as fast as the player turns their body' is false by construction: the body snaps between 8 facings and the beam aims freely through 360 degrees. Nothing in the kinematics creates the 'heavy, not sluggish' feel the pillar depends on. Walking starts and stops instantly, and the run ramp is 6 ticks.
- [RECOMMENDED] PC-9 / D.14 DOWNED in solo MVP: [game-designer] When downed in solo, the player waits about 30 s with no choices before the restart from the last autosave (a user decision). This is the most frequent failure in the game and it has the slowest recovery, which works against flow.
- [RECOMMENDED] PC-2 contexts / inspect_card vs channels (Pillar 4): [game-designer, qa-lead] Opening `inspect_card` cancels the channel. As a result, checking the gauge on the pipe you are welding aborts the weld. That turns the Pillar 4 'second instrument' check into a heavy penalty. There is also no context x action matrix, and nothing forces the card closed on DOWNED, STAGGERED or IN_POD.
- [RECOMMENDED] PC-6 lit-abort cadence (accessibility): [game-designer, extending the main review] Long channels abort on a single unlit check every 6 ticks. Tremor, stick drift or sliding off a narrow beam edge will abort welds of up to 360 ticks. The Pillar 3 intent needs only about half a second of grace.
- [RECOMMENDED] PC-7 / P2 ordering (stop then work): [main review, systems-designer, ux-designer, game-designer] `begin_*` validation reads an `octant` that `controller.step` writes later, in P3. Releasing W and pressing E on the same tick is rejected. Pressing W and E together is accepted, then aborts silently. With no buffer, presses made 1-2 ticks early are lost.
- [RECOMMENDED] D.7/D.8/D.12 facing-dependent mouse aim origin: [gameplay-programmer, systems-designer] Aim comes from the chest point, whose position depends on facing and sheet. A flip moves it by up to 4 px horizontally, or more vertically across rows. With the cursor 10-40 px from the body, a still mouse can cause a sheet/flip/beam limit cycle. Separately, aiming from the chest makes the focus angle θ_a penalise floor objects the player is pointing straight at.
- [RECOMMENDED] Gamepad aim default and throw binding: [ux-designer, main review] R3 for throw disturbs the aim stick and collides with the dev chord. The pad aim hand-off ('follows movement until first stick use, then holds forever') brings back the lamp stuck pointing at a door.
- [RECOMMENDED] Accessibility: aim visibility, keyboard-only, ignored input: [ux-designer, game-designer, gameplay-programmer] With the lamp off, which is the stealth posture the GDD promotes, the player has no indication of aim, including the throw direction. The cursor option was retired. Keyboard-only aim is impossible. Several inputs fail silently: a toggle inside the limiter, interact with nothing in focus, and run while capped. design/accessibility-requirements.md does not exist.
- [RECOMMENDED] D.10 hold-to-confirm: [gameplay-programmer, systems-designer, ux-designer] The bar adds up uncapped wall-time per frame, so a single 1 s hitch can confirm a destructive cut. The target is not latched, so a focus change during the hold is undefined. How this composes with channels is unclear, and the tap-window timer ignores pause.
- [RECOMMENDED] Stale and layered text, and Dependencies (main review items): [main review, qa-lead] PC-2 and the States table (L199, which I confirmed) still say channels cancel in hold mode only. Conditional '(adopted ...)' tags remain even though the decision log adopted them. Pod arrival and body transfer are not listed among the resync triggers.
- [RECOMMENDED] Camera coupling I2 / knob ranges: [systems-designer, main review] The registry gives FOLLOW_TAU and LOOKAHEAD_PX independent ranges, so they form a box. Its corner (0.20, 75) breaks I2 (111 > 108). The v_ceil expression divides by zero at FOLLOW_TAU 0, which is a legal value.
- [RECOMMENDED] Acceptance Criteria hygiene: [main review, qa-lead] Ten ACs cannot be tested as written. Fixtures depend on noise. AC 937 contains an unresolved ruling. The invariant-to-AC mapping is not one-to-one. Manual ACs name no sign-off. Performance timings sit in the deterministic suite. The gdUnit4 harness is not installed.

If the cell is empty and the player has no glowstick, a dark port can never be used. Under the MVP rules this ends in death and a restore, so it is not a hard softlock, but neither GDD says whether it is intended.
