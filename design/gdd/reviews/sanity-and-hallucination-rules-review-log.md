Findings:
- [BLOCKING] Player Fantasy anchor beats 2-5 and 'What this beat proves' (L64-70); Overview; SN-15 L-G (L260) and L-2I (L255); D.7 faults 1-2; AC-SN-48/53/71: [main review; game-designer; level-designer; CD spot-check confirmed] The flagship anchor cannot happen under the rules. Beat 2 (L65) says the room's O2 band is falling and the breath shortens, which is the Suit cue on band_o2 THIN. L-G (L260) invalidates any FALSE_OK lie on the next run after the seat's band leaves OK. So the numeral-vs-breath disagreement the anchor teaches is never live. For comfort-direction lies the same rule makes the suit.o2 and suit.warmth credit in L-2I empty: while the lie is legal they agree with it. [game-designer] adds that L70's 'a death walked into' and L79's 'the leak keeps bleeding' are also false, because L-G caps what it costs to trust a comfort lie at the depth of the OK band. Beat 5 (L68) also says 'the lie has not ended' while its own cost column and SN-16/17 collapse the lie on repair or field_check.
- [BLOCKING] SN-15 L-W, L-S, L-G (L254, L259, L260); SN-16 kinds (L266); AC-SN-52/53/121: [main review; confirmed by systems-designer, game-designer, godot-specialist, level-designer; CD spot-check confirmed] L-W, L-S and L-G are written for 'FALSE_OK' only. GLIDE drifts toward comfort on a degraded or failed subject (L266), and SPRITE_SWAP-running on a stopped machine is also a comfort lie. Neither is covered. So a GLIDE-comfort lie on a lethal_fault_modes subject at stage 1 bypasses the severity ceiling, the atmosphere guard and the witness requirement. A lie with no witness is unfalsifiable on rung 1, which breaks Pillar 4.
- [BLOCKING] Formulas D.5 (L659); SN-16 pre-draw gates (L269); SN-24; AC-SN-58/121: [systems-designer; godot-specialist; game-designer — all three independently] The pre-draw gates do not include 'at least one legal kind with weight > 0'. Once the direction fix above is applied, a FAILED subject at stage 1 or a DEGRADED lethal-mode subject at stage 1 has an empty legal set: FALSE_OK and GLIDE-comfort are refused, FALSE_FAIL needs a healthy subject, and SPRITE_SWAP has weight 0 at stage 1. D.5 then calls rng_int(1, 0) after the accept draw has already been consumed. That breaks the fixed '1 or 4 draws' replay guarantee. The same gap exists for a stage-3 surface with only SPRITE_SWAP and no frame pair. This must ship together with the blocker above.
- [BLOCKING] Edge Cases EC-SN-30 (L899); SN-1/SN-23 restore; AC-SN-75/76: [godot-specialist; CD spot-check partly confirmed] EC-SN-30 treats an unresolved ledger subject_ref after restore as corruption (a hard SANITY_RESTORE_SUBJECT_UNRESOLVED). Its reasoning is that destruction is always pruned 'in the same run'. Sanity runs only when T mod 12 = 0, but Save/Load snapshots at any tick boundary (save-load GDD L453). A subject destroyed or salvaged between hook runs, followed by an autosave before the next run, therefore leaves a legitimate dead ref. The MVP death rule (restart from the last autosave) would then hard-fail on a valid save. Whether Graph or Layout destruction notifies Sanity synchronously is unverified, and the Sanity text gives no guarantee either way.
- [RECOMMENDED] SN-29, D.8, Player Fantasy 'How the fantasy deepens' (L101) and INTENSE (L118); LETHAL_FAULT_MIN_STAGE: [game-designer; systems-designer on home-ring dependence; main review on SAN_MAX-start pacing] The MVP's reachable stage envelope is never stated. In ring 1 the ambient floor is 62000 (stage 1). Stages 2 and 3 need several BREACH epochs in one sortie. The MVP is therefore effectively stage-1-only: LIES_MAX 1, no SPRITE_SWAP, no FALSE_OK on faults 2 and 8. That conflicts with the user's INTENSE decision and with the competence-grows-with-lies promise. Pacing also assumes every sortie starts at SAN_MAX, and rest-to-full depends on the home ring, which is unconstrained.
- [RECOMMENDED] SN-15 L-L (L256); SN-18/19; D.12; Player Fantasy targets 3-4: [game-designer] The mandatory GLEAM plus the motion bit, together with the state inversion, make detection and direction free after the first lie. The scanner is then a chargeless, silent, risk-free rung that dominates, so the ladder prices only confirmation. This is the deepest pillar risk in the document. It is not a defect in the rules but a tuning and identity question.
- [RECOMMENDED] SN-14, SNV-10, OQ-SN-21: [main review] The stage-2 own-cone rim fringe is a de facto stage indicator, and whether it is allowed (IA-39/G8) is still an open user ruling, yet the status reads 'Designed'.
- [RECOMMENDED] Overview, Player Fantasy vs Visual/Audio preamble: [main review] The Overview and Player Fantasy promise cone-reach light lies in the MVP, but Visual/Audio defers them to VS-A.
- [RECOMMENDED] SN-16 lit-entry batch (L268); glowsticks: [level-designer] In dark rooms the chest-lamp entry batch captures only what the doorway sweep hits first, so eligibility is biased toward gauges near the door and can be controlled by the player's facing. Thrown glowsticks (a user decision) count as resting deployables in lit_static and can trigger draws from outside the compartment, or let the player choose which subjects are rolled.
- [RECOMMENDED] SN-15 L-I vs REPAIR_BIAS; EC-SN-25: [main review; systems-designer; game-designer; level-designer] REPAIR_BIAS_S fully overlaps DEBUNK_IMMUNE_S if OQ-SN-12 is adopted, which makes the bias dead. [level-designer] prefers removing the bias in the MVP; [game-designer] notes that EC-SN-25 calls a post-repair lie intended.
- [RECOMMENDED] D.4/SN-10 lead invariants; Tuning Knobs safe ranges: [systems-designer] The lead invariants are anchored at 70000, but routine play starts from the 62000, 52000 and 43000 floors. From the ring-3 floor the real lead to the friendly-harm gate is about 186-238 s, not 300 s. Several documented safe ranges also violate the document's own boot assertions: TELL_OFF upper limit 38000, MARGIN at most 8000, cap 28-36, and the floors form two disjoint intervals.
- [RECOMMENDED] Formulas D.11: [systems-designer] The cadence model ignores LIES_MAX 1 together with PENDING at stage 1, and the rule of one onset per batch. Real stage-1 visibility is about 53%, not 72%. The 65/222/400 alternative inherits the error.
- [RECOMMENDED] D.7 rung costs; L-W hops; T_EQUIP; carry state: [main review; systems-designer; level-designer] Rung costs are lower bounds: T_EQUIP is 0, field_check has no noise class, and d* ignores reach and T_EQUIP. Two-hop witness walks have no crossing cost, and fault 8's rung 1 is unvalued. The scanner cannot be equipped while carrying.
- [RECOMMENDED] SN-16 first lie (L272); AC-SN-61: [systems-designer; game-designer; level-designer] lies_seen increments on accept, so a first lie invalidated while PENDING still consumes the teaching slot. The first lie is alarm-direction (cost-free to ignore), which teaches the opposite of the anchor, and it likely lands during the home hangover.
- [RECOMMENDED] L-W cue list (L254): [game-designer; CD confirmed L254] 'the absence of the claimed trail' is listed as a positive cue, followed by 'Absence alone does not qualify', which contradicts it.
- [RECOMMENDED] L-L / lie-target data vs perception-filter V.6.3: [godot-specialist] Needles shorter than 14.3 px cannot draw the glide, but the eligibility data does not check needle length, so a lie can claim a motion leak it cannot render.
- [RECOMMENDED] Acceptance Criteria (oracles, perf, determinism lint, dev gate, frame-rate independence, sim-lit vs render-lit): [main review; godot-specialist; game-designer] There are 11 subjective or placeholder ACs. The determinism lint misses Godot hazards (get_instance_id, hash, shuffle, pick_random, unstable sort), and the event-order key has no tiebreak. The 'dev' feature tag does not exist. Presentation rates assume 60 Hz. No AC ties sim-lit to render-lit. No experiential AC measures the fantasy.
- [RECOMMENDED] Status text (Gaps, Last Updated, C11/C17, OQ-SN-33, deferred ACs 102/113/114): [main review] Several statuses are stale: the Perception Filter GDD exists, lit_static is published, and the Attention row is partly closed.
